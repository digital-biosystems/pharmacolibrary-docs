# Mercury cadmium telluride

- **generic name:** Mercury cadmium telluride
- **T3DB:** [T3D0347](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D0347) · **PubChem:** [CID 94407](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/94407)

## About

Mercury cadmium telluride is an alloy of mercury telluride and cadmium telluride. It is an important semiconductor and also used in infrared detection. Mercury is a heavy, silvery d-block metal and one of six elements that are liquid at or near room temperature and pressure. …

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Mercury cadmium telluride is an important semiconductor and also used in infrared detection.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** High-affinity binding of the divalent mercuric ion to thiol or sulfhydryl groups of proteins is believed to be the major mechanism for the activity of mercury. Through alterations in intracellular thiol status, mercury can promote oxidative stress, lipid peroxidation, mitochondrial dysfunction, and changes in heme metabolism. …
- **Health effects:** Mercury mainly affects the nervous system. Exposure to high levels of metallic, inorganic, or organic mercury can permanently damage the brain, kidneys, and developing fetus. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 1, carcinogenic to humans.
- **Minimal risk levels (ATSDR):** Chronic Inhalation: 0.0002 mg/m3 (Mercury) 
Acute Inhalation: 0.00003 mg/m3 (Cadmium) 
Chronic Inhalation: 0.00001 mg/m3 (Cadmium) 
Intermediate Oral: 0.0005 mg/kg/day (Cadmium) 
Chronic Oral: 0.0001 mg/kg/day (Cadmium)
