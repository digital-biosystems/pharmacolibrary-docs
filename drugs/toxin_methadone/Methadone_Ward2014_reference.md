### Reviewer guidance

**The abstract-only record lacks volumes V1 and V2, leaving covariate scenarios untested and preventing simulation of the methadone model.**

The parameter coverage check found that V1 (21.5 L/70 kg) and V2 (75.1 L/70 kg) were neither emitted nor defaulted, causing the simulation to fail. Although intercompartment clearances and elimination parameters were present, the missing central and peripheral volumes prevented the base model from running. Additionally, because covariate effects were defined but only the reference individual was targeted, the record was held back as not_simulated.
