### Reviewer guidance

**The record's single-compartment busulfan model was incorrectly mapped to a retention function, causing simulated concentrations to exceed paper values by over 200-fold.**

The topology was mapped to a retention model instead of the declared one-compartment structure, resulting in an output variable representing retained fraction rather than central concentration. Consequently, the simulated peak concentration of 0.9966415745905538 kg/m3 exceeded the expected value of 0.0042696 by a ratio of 233.4274. The model lacks a volume definition necessary for this conversion, and covariate effects were not simulated. Extracted — busulfan: CLnorm 11 L/h, kel 0.0404 h−1.
