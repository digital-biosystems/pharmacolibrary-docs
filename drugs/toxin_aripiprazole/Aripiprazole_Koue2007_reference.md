### Reviewer guidance

**Aripiprazole volume and intercompartmental clearance units conflict with their pharmacokinetic definitions, causing dimension mismatch and implausible magnitudes.**

The volume parameter V (0.0181 l/h/kg) contains a time component, conflicting with its definition as a volume, while the intercompartmental clearance Q3 (3.84 l/kg) lacks the necessary time denominator. These unit inconsistencies caused dimension mismatch errors and flagged the parameters as having implausible magnitudes. Additionally, a unit conversion failure prevented the generation of a valid SI value, contributing to the record's rejection. Extracted — aripiprazole: λ2 2.65 h-1, Q3 3.84 l/kg, V 0.0181 l/h/kg, kabs 2.65 h Ϫ1, tlag 3.2 day.
