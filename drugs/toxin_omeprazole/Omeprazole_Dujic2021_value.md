### Reviewer guidance

**Gliclazide parameters exhibit dimensional mismatches: intestinal intrinsic clearance uses µL/min, OATP Km uses µM, and Jmax uses days instead of rate units.**

Intestinal MRP2 intrinsic clearance is reported as 5.0 µL/min, a flow unit inconsistent with intrinsic clearance definitions requiring mass normalization. OATP1B1 Km is given as 30.2 µM, but the dimension check interpreted µM as length rather than concentration. Additionally, OATP1B1 Jmax is listed with units of days, which conflicts with the required mass/time dimension for maximum transport rates. Extracted — Gliclazide: MW 323 g/mol, fu 0.15, Vss 0.306 L/kg, Kp 3.5, CLint 5 µL/min, Km 30.2 µM, Vmax 12.9 d; omeprazole: CL 1.21 L/h, V 77.6 L, kabs 0.95 h−1, tlag 0.26 h.
