### Reviewer guidance

**The model for gliclazide as a substrate of OATP1B1 is rejected because the gliclazide compartment is unreachable from the dose.**

The structure defines a transport substrate link from gliclazide to OATP1B1, implying gliclazide is part of the pharmacokinetic system. However, the reviewer flagged an unreachable or orphan compartment, indicating no path exists from the administered dose to gliclazide. Consequently, the gliclazide-specific parameters, including its Cmax ratio of 1.09 and AUC ratio of 1.50, cannot be mechanistically supported by the model topology. Extracted — Gliclazide: Cmax_ratio 1.09, AUC ratio 1.5; omeprazole: CL 1.21 L/h, V 77.6 L, kabs 0.95 h−1, tlag 0.26 h.
