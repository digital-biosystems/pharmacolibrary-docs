### Reviewer guidance

**The record is rejected because it assigns an absorption rate constant of 100/h to aluminum in an exposure/outcome paper, lacking any other kinetic quantity like half-life, volume, or clearance.**

The model structure consists of a single compartment with a parameter kabs defined as an absorption rate constant with a value of 100 1/h for the compound Aluminium. The review identifies the paper as an exposure/outcome study that provides no other kinetic quantities such as half-life, volume, or clearance. Consequently, the record fails to support a valid pharmacokinetic model due to the absence of essential disposition parameters beyond the absorption rate. Extracted — aluminum: kabs 100 1/h.
