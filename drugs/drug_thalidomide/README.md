## About

Thalidomide is an immunomodulatory drug used for conditions such as multiple myeloma and leprosy, among several other immune and blood disorders. It remains authorised in the European Union for multiple myeloma, but its use is tightly controlled because it causes severe birth defects, and it was famously withdrawn from use as a sedative for this reason.
