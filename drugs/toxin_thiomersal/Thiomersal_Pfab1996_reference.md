### Reviewer guidance

**The thiomersal retention model lacks the distribution amplitude parameters A1 and A2 required to complete the retention function.**

The record defines initial and terminal half-lives of 2.2 and 40.5 days for mercury but omits the corresponding amplitude parameters. Without A1 and A2, the retention structure cannot be filled. The record is therefore incomplete for a standard retention model. Extracted — Mercury: t1/2α 2.2 days, t1/2β 40.5 days.
