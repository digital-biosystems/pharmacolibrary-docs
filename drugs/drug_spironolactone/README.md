## About

Spironolactone is a potassium-sparing diuretic and aldosterone antagonist used to treat fluid build-up from heart failure, liver disease, or kidney issues, as well as high blood pressure. It is administered orally in tablet or capsule forms. It is widely used and listed as an essential medicine.
