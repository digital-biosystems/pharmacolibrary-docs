## About

Paliperidone is an atypical antipsychotic used to treat schizophrenia, schizoaffective disorder, and psychosis. It is administered orally as extended-release tablets or intramuscularly as an extended-release injection. The medication is authorized in the European Union and is widely used in clinical practice.
