**The model fails to reproduce reported levetiracetam Cmax, tmax, and half-life due to a missing bioavailability value and unit conversion errors.**

Simulated Cmax, tmax, and terminal half-life deviate significantly from reported values. The absorption bioavailability parameter was assigned a library default rather than an extracted estimate.
