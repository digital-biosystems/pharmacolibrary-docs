# Levetiracetam

- **generic name:** Levetiracetam
- **T3DB:** [T3D3019](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3019) · **DrugBank:** [DB01202](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB01202) · **PubChem:** [CID 441341](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/441341)
- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

Levetiracetam is an anticonvulsant medication used to treat epilepsy. Levetiracetam may selectively prevent hypersynchronization of epileptiform burst firing and propagation of seizure activity. Levetiracetam binds to the synaptic vesicle protein SV2A, which is thought to be involved in the regulation of vesicle exocytosis. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** rapidly and almost completely absorbed after oral administration (99%). Peak plasma concentrations occurring in about an hour following oral administration in fasted subjects.
- **Sources and uses:** Used as adjunctive therapy in the treatment of partial onset seizures in adults and children 4 years of age and older with epilepsy.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** The precise mechanism(s) by which levetiracetam exerts its antiepileptic effect is unknown. The antiepileptic activity of levetiracetam was assessed in a number of animal models of epileptic seizures. …
- **Health effects:** May cause a potentially dangerous rash that may develop into Stevens Johnson syndrome, an extremely rare but potentially fatal skin disease.
- **Treatment:** There is no specific antidote for overdose with Levetiracetam. …
