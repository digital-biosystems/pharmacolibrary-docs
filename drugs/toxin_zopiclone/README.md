# Zopiclone

- **generic name:** Zopiclone
- **T3DB:** [T3D3016](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3016) · **DrugBank:** [DB01198](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB01198) · **PubChem:** [CID 5735](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/5735)
- **groups:** approved

## About

Zopiclone is only found in individuals that have used or taken this drug. It is a novel hypnotic agent used in the treatment of insomnia. Its mechanism of action is based on modulating benzodiazepine receptors. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** rapidly absorbed following oral administration.
- **Sources and uses:** For the short-term treatment of insomnia.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Zopiclone exerts its action by binding on the benzodiazepine receptor complex and modulation of the GABA<sub>B</sub>Z receptor chloride channel macromolecular complex. Both zopiclone and benzodiazepines act indiscriminately at the benzodiazepine binding site on alpha1, alpha2, alpha3 and alpha5 GABAA containing receptors as full agonists causing an enhancement of the inhibitory actions of GABA to produce the therapeutic (hypnotic and anxiolytic) and adverse effects of zopiclone.
- **Health effects:** They cause slurred speech, disorientation and "drunken" behavior. They are physically and psychologically addictive.
