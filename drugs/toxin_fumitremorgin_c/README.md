# Fumitremorgin C

- **generic name:** Fumitremorgin C
- **T3DB:** [T3D3744](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3744) · **PubChem:** [CID 403923](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/403923)

## About

Fumitremorgin C is produced by Aspergillus fumigatus and Neosartorya fischeri.

- **Category:** Fungal Toxin, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, dermal, inhalation, and parenteral (contaminated drugs).
- **Sources and uses:** Fumitremorgin C is a mycotoxin that has been found in the fungus Aspergillus fumigatus.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Fumitremorgin C inhibits ATP-binding cassette transporter (ACBG2), also known as breast cancer resistance protein. ACBG2 is known to confer multidrug resistance and also affects the bioavailability of different drugs. …
- **Health effects:** Tremorgenic mycotoxins affect central nervous system activity. They cause a neurological disease of cattle known as 'staggers syndrome'.
- **Treatment:** To control severe tremors caused by tremorgenic mycotoxins, methocarbamol should be administered. …
