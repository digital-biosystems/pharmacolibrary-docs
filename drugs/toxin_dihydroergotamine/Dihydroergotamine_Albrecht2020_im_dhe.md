### Reviewer guidance

**Percent values for AUC metrics were incorrectly mapped to concentration-time parameters, creating a dimensional mismatch with the required mass-time-length units.**

The record lists 19% and 16% as the values for AUC0-2h and AUC0-inf respectively, despite these parameters requiring concentration-time area units. The source data labels indicate these percentages represent the coefficient of variation rather than the actual exposure values. Consequently, the model cannot convert these dimensionless percentages into the necessary physical units for simulation. Extracted — dihydroergotamine: Cmax 3.37e+03 pg/mL, AUCt 1.36e+03 h*pg/mL, AUC 19 %, AUC∞ 16 %, t1/2z 11 h, CL/F 75 L/h, V/F 1.2e+03 L.
