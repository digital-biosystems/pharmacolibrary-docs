# 2,4-Di-tert-butylphenol

- **generic name:** 2,4-Di-tert-butylphenol
- **T3DB:** [T3D4878](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4878) · **PubChem:** [CID 14339290](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/14339290)

## About

2,6-di-tert-butylphenol is an organic compound with the structural formula 2,6-(3C)2C6H3OH. This colourless solid alkylated phenol and its derivatives are used industrially as UV stabilizer and an antioxidant for hydrocarbon-based products ranging from petrochemicals to plastics. Illustrative of its usefulness, it prevents gumming in aviation fuels.

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
