### Reviewer guidance

**Brexpiprazole absorption and Km parameters have incorrect units, and V/F is in L/h, causing dimension and plausibility errors.**

The transit rate constant (1.9) and Km (0.62) are recorded in liters instead of per hour, violating dimensional consistency for rate and affinity constants. The apparent volume of distribution (63.0) is recorded in L/h, a clearance unit, making its magnitude physiologically implausible for a volume. These unit errors prevented the model from passing structural and physiological validation checks. Extracted — brexpiprazole: CL/F 0.74 L/h, V/F 63 L/h, ktr 1.9 L, Q 1 L/h, Km 0.62 L.

A second, independent reading of the paper (`gpt-oss-120b`) disagrees on the value of a value calculated based on the model parameter estimates [(1 + 0.56) × 0.74 l/h]: this record has none, the second reading none; it also differs on 9 more fields. That field shapes the model, so the record is marked disputed.
