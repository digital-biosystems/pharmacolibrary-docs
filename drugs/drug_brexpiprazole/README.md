## About

Brexpiprazole is an atypical antipsychotic used to treat major depressive disorder, schizophrenia, and agitation in Alzheimer's disease. It is taken orally as tablets. The medication is authorised in the European Union for schizophrenia and has been used in the United States since its initial approval.
