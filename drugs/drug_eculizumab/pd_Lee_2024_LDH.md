## Record summary

**As extracted:** Terminal complement activity (concentrations from this paper's PK model) drives lactate dehydrogenase (in U/L): direct sigmoid Emax (Hill) effect.

**Model:** Model and browser simulation available in **Models** and **Simulation**.

> Eculizumab inhibits terminal complement activity via an inhibitory direct sigmoid Emax model, which in turn modulates LDH through a separate direct sigmoid Emax relationship where terminal complement activity positively drives LDH production (E0=206.00 U/L, Emax=1680.00 U/L, EC50=39.00%, Hill coefficient=4.30). The paper does not explicitly provide the Emax or EC50 values for the eculizumab concentration–terminal complement activity step in the excerpts, but notes that complete inhibition occurs at eculizumab concentrations of 35 μg/mL.
