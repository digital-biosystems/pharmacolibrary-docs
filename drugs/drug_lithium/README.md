## About

Lithium is a mood stabilizer used primarily to treat bipolar disorder and major depressive disorder. It is administered orally by mouth. Blood levels are routinely measured to guide dosing and reduce the risk of toxicity. Lithium is a WHO essential medicine available as a generic medication.
