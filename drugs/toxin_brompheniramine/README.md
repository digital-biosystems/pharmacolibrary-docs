# Brompheniramine

- **T3DB:** [T3D4554](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4554) · **DrugBank:** [DB00835](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00835) · **PubChem:** [CID 6834](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/6834)

## About

Histamine H1 antagonist used in treatment of allergies, rhinitis, and urticaria. [PubChem]

- **Category:** Drug, Food Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** antihistamines are well absorbed from the gastrointestinal tract after oral administration.
- **Sources and uses:** For the treatment of the symptoms of the common cold and allergic rhinitis, such as runny nose, itchy eyes, watery eyes, and sneezing.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Brompheniramine works by acting as an antagonist of the H1 histamine receptors. It also functions as a moderately effective anticholingeric agent, likely an antimuscarinic agent similar to other common antihistamines such as diphenhydramine. …
- **Acute toxicity:** Oral, Rat: LD<sub>50</sub> = 318 mg/kg.
