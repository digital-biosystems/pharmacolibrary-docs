## About

Olanzapine is an atypical antipsychotic prescribed for schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, and other psychotic conditions. It is usually taken by mouth, though an intramuscular injection is also available for certain situations. The medication is widely prescribed and listed among essential medicines, with authorized products in the European Union.
