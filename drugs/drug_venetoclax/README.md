## About

Venetoclax is an anticancer medicine used to treat blood cancers such as chronic lymphocytic leukemia, acute myeloid leukemia, and certain B-cell lymphomas. It is an approved medicine and is authorised in the European Union for chronic lymphocytic leukemia.
