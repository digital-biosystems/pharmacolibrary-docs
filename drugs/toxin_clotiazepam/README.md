## About

Clotiazepam is only found in individuals that have used or taken this drug. It is a benzodiazepine derivative, not approved for sale in the U.S. or Canada, but has been approved in the U.K. It is a schedule IV drug in Canada.Clotiazepam acts at the benzodiazepine receptors (BZD). …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral
- **Sources and uses:** For the treatment of anxiety disorders. Used as an anxiolytic, anticonvulsant, sedative and skeletal muscle relaxant.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Clotiazepam acts at the benzodiazepine receptors (BZD). This agonizes the action of GABA, increasing the frequency of opening of the channel chlorinates and penetration of the ions chlorinates through the ionophore. …
- **Health effects:** They cause slurred speech, disorientation and "drunken" behavior. They are physically and psychologically addictive. …
- **Treatment:** General supportive measures should be employed, along with intravenous fluids, and an adequate airway maintained. …
