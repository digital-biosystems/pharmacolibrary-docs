## About

Human insulin is an anti-diabetic medication used to treat type-1 diabetes and maturity-onset diabetes of the young type 2. It is widely used and remains authorised in the European Union for diabetes, available in fast-acting, intermediate-acting, long-acting, combined, and inhaled forms.
