## Record summary

**As extracted:** Maraviroc (concentrations from the PK model of Chan_2008) drives SARS-CoV-2 viral titer and S-protein mediated cell fusion (multinucleate cell formation) (in PFU/mL): direct sigmoid Emax (Hill) effect.

**Model:** No model was generated from this record.

> In an agent-based SARS-CoV-2 tissue model, maraviroc (µM) inhibits both S-protein-mediated cell fusion and viral entry/exit (mixed-effect pharmacodynamic model) driving viral titer (PFU/mL) and multinucleate cell formation; the fitted sigmoid Emax model used EC50 = 2.5 µM, Hill power 2.5, and a drug internalization rate of 0.0116 µM·µm³/min (half-maximum cytoplasmic concentration in ~1 hour).
