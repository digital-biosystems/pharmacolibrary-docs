## About

Technetium (99mTc) sestamibi is a diagnostic radiopharmaceutical used primarily for nuclear medicine imaging of the heart, as well as in the work-up of hyperparathyroidism and breast cancer. It is given intravenously as an injection, typically from a kit prepared for solution. The drug is widely used in clinical practice for these imaging applications.
