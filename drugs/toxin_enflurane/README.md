# Enflurane

- **generic name:** Enflurane
- **T3DB:** [T3D2717](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2717) · **DrugBank:** [DB00228](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00228) · **PubChem:** [CID 3226](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/3226)
- **groups:** approved, vet_approved, withdrawn

## About

Enflurane is only found in individuals that have used or taken this drug. It is an extremely stable inhalation anesthetic that allows rapid adjustments of anesthesia depth with little change in pulse or respiratory rate. [PubChem]Enflurane induces a reduction in junctional conductance by decreasing gap junction channel opening times and increasing gap junction channel closing times. Enflurane also activates calcium dependent ATPase in the sarcoplasmic reticulum by increasing the fluidity of the lipid membrane. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** rapidly absorbed into the circulation via the lungs.
- **Sources and uses:** Used for the induction and maintenance of general anaesthesia during surgery and cesarean section and also used for analgesia during vaginal delivery.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Enflurane induces a reduction in junctional conductance by decreasing gap junction channel opening times and increasing gap junction channel closing times. Enflurane also activates calcium dependent ATPase in the sarcoplasmic reticulum by increasing the fluidity of the lipid membrane. …
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 5.4 ml/kg (oral, rat).
