## About

Ziprasidone is an antipsychotic used to treat schizophrenia and related psychotic conditions such as schizoaffective disorder, and has also been used for anxiety and Tourette syndrome. It is an approved medicine used in routine psychiatric care, though it carries a boxed warning.
