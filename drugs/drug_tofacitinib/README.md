## About

Tofacitinib is a JAK inhibitor immunosuppressant used to treat rheumatoid arthritis and ulcerative colitis, and has also been studied for conditions such as alopecia areata. It is an approved medicine, authorised in the European Union for rheumatoid arthritis, and carries a boxed warning.
