## Record summary

**As extracted:** Tofacitinib (the dose) drives ACR20 response rate: direct Emax (saturable) effect.

**Model:** Model and browser simulation available in **Models** and **Simulation**.

> The relationship between tofacitinib dose and the binary ACR20 response rate is modeled using a direct Emax mechanism on the logit domain, characterized by a placebo effect (E0) of -1.3, a maximum effect (Emax) of 3.2, and a half-maximal effective dose (ED50) of 4.2 mg, with a study effect on Emax of 1.4.
>
> <sub>Source-based summary by qwen3.8-27b from the paper's text; not human-verified</sub>

- **model family:** `emax`
- **driver:** `dose_only`
- **tier:** population
- **effect:** stimulation/unknown
