# Anastrozole

- **generic name:** Anastrozole
- **T3DB:** [T3D4796](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4796) · **DrugBank:** [DB01217](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB01217) · **PubChem:** [CID 2187](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/2187)
- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

Anastrozole is a drug indicated in the treatment of breast cancer in post-menopausal women. It is used both in adjuvant therapy (i.e. following surgery) and in metastatic breast cancer. It decreases the amount of estrogens that the body makes. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** rapidly absorbed into the systemic cirulation following oral administration. Peak plasma concentrations are usually attained within 2 hours under fasting conditions, with steady-state plasma concentrations attained in approximately 7 days.
- **Sources and uses:** For adjuvant treatment of hormone receptor positive breast cancer , as well as hormonal treatment of advanced breast cancer in post-menopausal women. Has also been used to treat pubertal gynecomastia and McCune-Albright syndrome; however, manufacturer states that efficacy for these indications have not been established.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Anastrozole selectively inhibits aromatase. The principal source of circulating estrogen (primarily estradiol) is conversion of adrenally-generated androstenedione to estrone by aromatase in peripheral tissues. …
- **Acute toxicity:** In rats, lethality is greater than 100 mg/kg.
