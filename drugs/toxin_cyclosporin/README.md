# Cyclosporin

- **generic name:** Cyclosporin
- **T3DB:** [T3D3498](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3498) · **DrugBank:** [DB00091](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00091) · **PubChem:** [CID 6435893](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/6435893)
- **groups:** approved, investigational, vet_approved

## About

A cyclic undecapeptide from an extract of soil fungi. It is a powerful immunosupressant with a specific action on T-lymphocytes. It is used for the prophylaxis of graft rejection in organ and tissue transplantation. …

- **Category:** Drug, Fungal Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** the absorption of cyclosporine from the gastrointestinal tract is incomplete and variable. The extent of absorption is dependent on the individual patient, the patient population, and the formulation. The absolute bioavailability of cyclosproine administered as Sandimmune&#8482, is dependent on the patient population, estimated to be less than 10% in liver transplant patients and as great as 89% in some renal transplant patients. Compared to an intravenous infusion, the absolute bioavailability of the oral solution is approximately 30% based upon the results in 2 patients. The cyclosporine capsules and oral solution are bioequivalent. The time of peak blood concentrations (Tmax) following oral administration of cyclosporine [modified] ranged from 1.5 - 2.0 hours.
- **Sources and uses:** For treatment of transplant (kidney, liver, and heart) rejection, rheumatoid arthritis, severe psoriasis.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Cyclosporine binds to cyclophilin. The complex then inhibits calcineurin which is normally responsible for activating transcription of interleukin 2. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 1, carcinogenic to humans.
- **Acute toxicity:** The oral LD<sub>50</sub> is 2329 mg/kg in mice, 1480 mg/kg in rats, and > 1000 mg/kg in rabbits. The I.V. LD<sub>50</sub> is 148 mg/kg in mice, 104 mg/kg in rats, and 46 mg/kg in rabbits.
