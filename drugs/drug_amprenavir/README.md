## About

This antiviral medication belongs to the protease inhibitor class and was developed for managing HIV infection. Patients take the drug by mouth in capsule or liquid forms, and it is well absorbed from the gastrointestinal tract. The drug has been withdrawn from the European Union market as the manufacturer discontinued production.
