# Diethylstilbestrol

- **generic name:** Diethylstilbestrol
- **T3DB:** [T3D4744](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4744) · **DrugBank:** [DB00255](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00255) · **PubChem:** [CID 448537](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/448537)
- **groups:** approved, withdrawn

## About

A synthetic nonsteroidal estrogen used in the treatment of menopausal and postmenopausal disorders. It was also used formerly as a growth promoter in animals. According to the Fourth Annual Report on Carcinogens (NTP 85-002, 1985), diethylstilbestrol has been listed as a known carcinogen.

- **Category:** Drug, Food Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Sources and uses:** Used in the treatment of prostate cancer. Previously used in the prevention of miscarriage or premature delivery in pregnant women prone to miscarriage or premature delivery.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Estrogens diffuse into their target cells and interact with a protein receptor, the estrogen receptor. Target cells include the female reproductive tract, the mammary gland, the hypothalamus, and the pituitary. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 1, carcinogenic to humans.
