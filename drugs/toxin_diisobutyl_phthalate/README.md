# Diisobutyl phthalate

- **generic name:** Diisobutyl phthalate
- **T3DB:** [T3D3644](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3644) · **PubChem:** [CID 6782](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/6782)

## About

DIBP is an odorless plasticizer and has excellent heat and light stability. It is the lowest cost plasticizer for cellulose nitrate. DIBP has lower density and freezing point than DBP (dibutyl phthalate, CAS No.: 84-74-2). …

- **Category:** Household Toxin, Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** DIBP is used in nitro cellulose plastic, nail polish, explosive material, lacquer manufacturing and also with methyl methacrylate applications.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Phthalate esters are endocrine disruptors. They decrease foetal testis testosterone production and reduce the expression of steroidogenic genes by decreasing mRNA expression. …
- **Health effects:** Phthalate esters are endocrine disruptors. Animal studies have shown that they disrupt reproductive development and can cause a number of malformations in affected young, such as reduced anogenital distance (AGD), cryptorchidism, hypospadias, and reduced fertility. …
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 15000 mg/kg (Oral, Rat)
LD50: 10 g/kg (Dermal, Guinea pig)
LD50: 3749 mg/kg (Intraperitoneal, Rat)
