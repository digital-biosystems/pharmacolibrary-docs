## About

Chloramphenicol is a broad-spectrum antibiotic that inhibits protein synthesis to treat serious bacterial infections, conjunctivitis, and rickettsiosis. It is administered orally, by injection into a vein, topically, or via ophthalmic and otic routes; it is well absorbed when taken by mouth. Blood levels are measured to guide the correct dose during treatment. It is widely used as an essential medicine, though systemic use is recommended only when safer antibiotics cannot be used.
