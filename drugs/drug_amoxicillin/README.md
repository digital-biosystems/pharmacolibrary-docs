## About

Amoxicillin is a penicillin antibiotic used to treat many bacterial infections, including ear, sinus, respiratory and urinary tract infections, and it is also given in combination to eradicate Helicobacter pylori. It is widely used around the world, is included on the WHO list of essential medicines, and is also approved for veterinary use.
