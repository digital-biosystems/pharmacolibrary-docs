# Rifampin

- **generic name:** Rifampin
- **T3DB:** [T3D2969](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2969) · **DrugBank:** [DB01045](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB01045) · **PubChem:** [CID 5381226](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/5381226)
- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

A semisynthetic antibiotic produced from Streptomyces mediterranei. It has a broad antibacterial spectrum, including activity against several forms of Mycobacterium. In susceptible organisms it inhibits DNA-dependent RNA polymerase activity by forming a stable complex with the enzyme. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral. Well absorbed from gastrointestinal tract.
- **Sources and uses:** For the treatment of Tuberculosis and Tuberculosis-related mycobacterial infections.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Rifampin acts via the inhibition of DNA-dependent RNA polymerase, leading to a suppression of RNA synthesis and cell death.
- **Health effects:** Antibiotic resistance
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 3, not classifiable as to its carcinogenicity to humans.
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 1570 mg/kg (Rat)
