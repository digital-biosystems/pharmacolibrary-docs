## About

Pyrantel is an antinematodal medicine used to treat intestinal worm infections such as ascariasis, enterobiasis, trichuriasis and ancylostomiasis. It is an approved human medicine and is also approved for veterinary use, and it appears on the WHO list of essential medicines.
