### Reviewer guidance

**The Doxil compartment is unreachable from the dose, leaving it without a path to the central circulation.**

The model links Doxil release to Doxorubicin via MPS_release, but Doxil itself is an orphan compartment with no input from the dosing site. This structural flaw prevents the drug from entering the system through the nanoparticle formulation, invalidating the simulation.
