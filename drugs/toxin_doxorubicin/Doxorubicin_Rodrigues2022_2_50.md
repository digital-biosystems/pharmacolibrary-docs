### Reviewer guidance

**Doxorubicin Cmax is mislabeled with a unit of µM, creating a dimension conflict for a concentration parameter and preventing unit conversion.**

The record assigns the value 1.06 µM to Doxorubicin Cmax, treating a concentration as a volume-based quantity. This mismatch with mass per volume standards caused the review to reject the entry. Additionally, the paper is cited as using 3D Human Intestinal Organoids, yet the parameter unit failed conversion to SI. Extracted — doxorubicin: Cmax 1.06 µM, CL 220 mL/h, V 1.58e+03 mL.
