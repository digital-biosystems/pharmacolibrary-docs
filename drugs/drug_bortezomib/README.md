## About

Bortezomib is an anticancer medicine (a proteasome inhibitor) used mainly to treat multiple myeloma and other blood cancers such as mantle cell lymphoma. It is approved and widely used, with several authorised products in the European Union, though it is typically given under specialist oncology care.
