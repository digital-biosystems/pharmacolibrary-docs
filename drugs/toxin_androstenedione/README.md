# Androstenedione

- **generic name:** Androstenedione
- **T3DB:** [T3D4240](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4240) · **DrugBank:** [DB01536](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB01536) · **PubChem:** [CID 6128](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/6128)
- **groups:** illicit, investigational

## About

Androstenedione is a delta-4 19-carbon steroid that is produced not only in the testis, but also in the ovary and the adrenal cortex. Depending on the tissue type, androstenedione can serve as a precursor to testosterone as well as estrone and estradiol. It is the common precursor of male and female sex hormones. …

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin, Animal Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** endogenous, ingestion
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Androstenedione is converted to testosterone and estrogen, and when taken in sufficient quantities androstenedione can cause unwanted masculinizing and feminizing effects. Androstenedione is considered an androgenic steroid precursor because testosterone is an androgen or male hormone. …
- **Health effects:** Androstenedione was legally defined as an anabolic steroid by the FDA in 2004. It poses significant health risks commonly associated with steroids. …
