## About

Human normal immunoglobulin for intravascular administration is used to treat immune deficiency syndromes, idiopathic thrombocytopenic purpura, Guillain-Barré syndrome, Kawasaki disease, and in bone marrow transplantation. It is authorised in the European Union, where products such as Privigen are approved.
