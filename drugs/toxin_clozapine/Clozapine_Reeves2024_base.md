### Reviewer guidance

**The model output was set to the parent clozapine concentration instead of the measured metabolite, and the compartmental topology omitted the metabolite compartment entirely.**

The model structure was reduced to a single-compartment enteral model, lacking the N-desmethylclozapine compartment required by the parent-metabolite topology. Consequently, the output variable pointed to the parent drug's central compartment rather than the metabolite, despite Clozapine being the measured analyte in the source data. Extracted — clozapine: V/F 750 L, kabs 0.69 h À1, CL/F 30.3 L h À1.
