### Reviewer guidance

**Norclozapine has unlinked zero compartments and an abstract-derived structure mismatch, with covariate effects untested and parameters defaulted.**

Norclozapine is defined as a metabolite with zero compartments but lacks a conversion parameter from Clozapine, leaving it unreachable. The model structure was abstract-only, resulting in a one-compartment design that failed to match the required parent-metabolite topology. Covariate impacts on clearance were defined but not simulated, while Tlag relied on default values and bioavailability was assumed to be one. Extracted — clozapine: V/F 7 L/kg, CL/F 18 L/h, kabs 0.69 h−1; Norclozapine: CL 39 L/h, CL/F 11.3 L/h.
