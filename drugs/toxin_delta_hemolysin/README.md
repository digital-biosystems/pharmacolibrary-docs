# Delta-hemolysin

- **generic name:** Delta-hemolysin
- **T3DB:** [T3D2612](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2612) · **PubChem:** none

## About

Delta-hemolysin is produced by Staphylococcus aureus. Hemolysins cause lysis of red blood cells.

- **Category:** Protein, Bacterial Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** ingestion, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Delta-hemolysin is produced by Staphylococcus aureus.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Hemolysins consists mainly of beta-sheets forms heptameric units on the cellular membrane, producing a complete beta-barrel pore. This pore allows the exchange of monovalent ions, resulting in DNA fragmentation and eventually apoptosis.
- **Health effects:** Delta-toxin is a cytotoxin produced by Staphylococcus aureus. Staphylococcus aureus is the most common cause of staph infections. …
