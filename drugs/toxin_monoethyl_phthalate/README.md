# Monoethyl phthalate

- **generic name:** Monoethyl phthalate
- **T3DB:** [T3D3961](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3961) · **PubChem:** [CID 75318](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/75318)

## About

Monoethyl phthalate (mEtP) is a metabolite, or breakdown product, of diethyl phthalate, used in perfume, cologne, deodorant, soap, shampoo, lotion, and other personal care products, particularly those containing fragrance.

- **Category:** Household Toxin, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin, Animal Toxin
- **Sources and uses:** Monoethyl phthalate (mEtP) is a metabolite, or breakdown product, of diethyl phthalate, used in perfume, cologne, deodorant, soap, shampoo, lotion, and other personal care products, particularly those containing fragrance.
