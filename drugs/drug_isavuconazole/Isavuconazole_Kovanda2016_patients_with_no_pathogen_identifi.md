### Reviewer guidance

**The isavuconazole model was held back because its absorption rate constant was missing from the source and defaulted to a generic placeholder rather than a specific estimate.**

The record defines a first-order depot structure requiring an absorption rate constant, but the paper does not report this parameter. Consequently, the model used library defaults for both the absorption rate constant and the lag time, which are not supported by the Kovanda_2016 data. The model also parameterizes clearance as apparent, assuming unity bioavailability despite the reported value of 98.2%. Extracted — isavuconazole: CLm/F 2.3 L/h, V 340 L, Fab 98.2.
