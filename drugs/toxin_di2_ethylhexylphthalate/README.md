# Di(2-ethylhexyl)phthalate

- **generic name:** Di(2-ethylhexyl)phthalate
- **T3DB:** [T3D0076](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D0076) · **PubChem:** [CID 8343](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/8343)

## About

Di(2-ethylhexyl) phthlate (DEHP) is a manufactured chemical that is commonly added to plastics to make them flexible. DEHP exposure is generally low and not harmful, but increased exposures resulting from intravenous fluids delivered through plastic tubing or ingesting contaminated foods or water may have toxic effects. This is of particular concern since DEHP is known to leach into liquid that come in contact with DEHP containing plastic.

- **Category:** Household Toxin, Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Food Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** DEHP is present in plastic products such as wall coverings, tablecloths, floor tiles, furniture upholstery, shower curtains, garden hoses, swimming pool liners, rainwear, baby pants, dolls, some toys, shoes, automobile upholstery and tops, packaging film and sheets, sheathing for wire and cable, medical tubing, and blood storage bags.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Monoethylhexylphthalate (MEHP), one of the major metabolites of DEHP, induces peroxisome proliferation by activating peroxisome proliferator activated receptors. This is believed to increase production of hydrogen peroxide by peroxisomes and enhance cell proliferation, leading to hepatotoxic and carcinogenic effects. …
- **Health effects:** Chronic and/or high levels of DEHP exposure may cause reproductive and developmental damage. This includes damaged sperm, delayed sexual maturity, and deficiencies in the development of male babies. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 2B, possibly carcinogenic to humans.
- **Minimal risk levels (ATSDR):** Intermediate Oral: 0.1 mg/kg/day
Chronic Oral: 0.06 mg/kg/day
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 33.9 g/kg (Oral, Rabbit)
LD50: 10 g/kg (Dermal, Guinea pig)
LD50: 30.7 g/kg (Intraperitoneal, Rat)
