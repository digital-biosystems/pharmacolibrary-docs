# Maitotoxin

- **generic name:** Maitotoxin
- **T3DB:** [T3D2587](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2587) · **PubChem:** [CID 71460273](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/71460273)

## About

Maitotoxin (MTX) is an extremely potent toxin produced by a species of dinoflagellates (Gambierdiscus toxicus). It activates calcium permeable, non-selective cation channels, leading to an increase in levels of cytosolic calcium ions. Ultimately, a cell death cascade is activated, resulting in membrane blebbing and eventually cell lysis.

- **Category:** Animal Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** injection (sting/bite)
- **Sources and uses:** Maitotoxin is an extremely potent toxin produced by a species of dinoflagellates (Gambierdiscus toxicus).
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Maitotoxin activates calcium permeable, non-selective cation channels, leading to an increase in levels of cytosolic calcium ions. It is thought that maitotoxin leads to the formation of pores on these ion channels. …
- **Health effects:** Maitotoxin is a potent cytotoxin.
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 50 ug/kg (Mouse)
