## About

Erythromycin is a macrolide antibiotic used to treat a range of bacterial infections, including acne, chlamydia, pertussis, legionnaires' disease, and campylobacteriosis. It is widely used in human medicine and is also approved for veterinary use; it appears on the WHO list of essential medicines.
