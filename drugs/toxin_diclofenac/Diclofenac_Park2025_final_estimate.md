### Reviewer guidance

**The record was rejected because Vmax for diclofenac has a value of 0.0631 with an inconsistent unit of h−1 instead of mass per time.**

The extracted Vmax parameter for diclofenac is 0.0631, labeled with the unit h−1. However, Vmax represents the maximum rate of enzymatic metabolism and requires a dimension of mass divided by time, not inverse time. This dimensional mismatch caused the rejection. Additionally, the paper was only available as an abstract, which may have contributed to the erroneous unit extraction. Extracted — diclofenac: Vmax 0.0631 h−1, λ1 1.56 h−1, V 10 L.
