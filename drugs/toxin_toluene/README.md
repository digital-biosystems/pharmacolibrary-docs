## About

Toluene (), also known as toluol (), is a substituted aromatic hydrocarbon with the chemical formula C6H5CH3, often abbreviated as PhCH3, where Ph stands for the phenyl group. Toluene is a colorless, water-insoluble liquid with a sweet smell, and is partially responsible for the aroma of gasoline and paint fumes. It is a mono-substituted benzene derivative, consisting of a methyl group (CH3) attached to a phenyl group by a single bond.

<p><small>Source: <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toluene?oldid=1385020808">Wikipedia</a> · <a href="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/">CC BY-SA 4.0</a></small></p>

- **Category:** Household Toxin, Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Toluene is used in paints, paint thinners, fingernail polish, lacquers, adhesives, and rubber and in some printing and leather tanning processes. Gasoline, which contains 5 to 7 perfect toluene by weight, is the largest source of atmospheric emissions and exposure of the general populace.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Toluene is a cholinesterase or acetylcholinesterase (AChE) inhibitor. A cholinesterase inhibitor (or 'anticholinesterase') suppresses the action of acetylcholinesterase. …
- **Health effects:** Acute exposure to cholinesterase inhibitors can cause a cholinergic crisis characterized by severe nausea/vomiting, salivation, sweating, bradycardia, hypotension, collapse, and convulsions. Increasing muscle weakness is a possibility and may result in death if respiratory muscles are involved. …
- **Lethal dose:** 50 mg/kg for an adult human.
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 3, not classifiable as to its carcinogenicity to humans.
- **Minimal risk levels (ATSDR):** Acute Inhalation: 1 ppm
Chronic Inhalation: 0.08 ppm
Acute Oral: 0.8 mg/kg/day
Intermediate Oral: 0.02 mg/kg/day
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 5000 mg/kg (Oral, Rat)
LD50: 1332 mg/kg (Intraperitoneal, Rat) 
LD50: 1960 mg/kg (Intravenous, Rat) 
LD50: 2250 mg/kg (Subcutaneous, Mouse) 
LD50: 12 124 mg/kg (Dermal, Rabbit) 
LC50: 400 ppm over 24 hours (Inhalation, Mouse)
- **Treatment:** If the compound has been ingested, rapid gastric lavage should be performed using 5% sodium bicarbonate. …
- **Detected in:** blood
