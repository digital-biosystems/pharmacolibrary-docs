# Cinolazepam

- **generic name:** Cinolazepam
- **T3DB:** [T3D3056](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3056) · **DrugBank:** [DB01594](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB01594) · **PubChem:** [CID 3033621](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/3033621)
- **groups:** investigational

## About

Cinolazepam is only found in individuals that have used or taken this drug. It is a benzodiazepine derivative. It possesses anxiolytic, anticonvulsant, sedative and skeletal muscle relaxant properties. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** bioavailability following oral administration is 90-100%.
- **Sources and uses:** For the management of anxiety disorders or for the short-term relief of the symptoms of anxiety or anxiety associated with depressive symptoms.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Cinolazepam binds to central benzodiazepine receptors which interact allosterically with GABA receptors. This potentiates the effects of the inhibitory neurotransmitter GABA, increasing the inhibition of the ascending reticular activating system and blocking the cortical and limbic arousal that occurs following stimulation of the reticular pathways.
- **Health effects:** They cause slurred speech, disorientation and "drunken" behavior. They are physically and psychologically addictive. …
