## About

Tetracycline is an antibiotic used against bacterial infections such as acne, gonorrhea, chlamydia, rickettsial infections and bronchitis. It remains an approved medicine, including veterinary use, and is listed among WHO essential medicines, though some products have been withdrawn in places.
