### Reviewer guidance

**L-DOPA's AUC∞ unit h/ml lacks mass, failing dimensional analysis and lacking SI conversion, causing rejection.**

The terminal half-life is recorded as 0.00 h, which is pharmacokinetically invalid. More critically, the AUC∞ parameter has the unit h/ml, missing the mass dimension required for concentration-time area units like ng×h/ml. This dimensional mismatch and the absence of a convertible SI unit prevent the parameter from being used in the model.
