# Uranium dioxide

- **generic name:** Uranium dioxide
- **T3DB:** [T3D1163](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D1163) · **PubChem:** [CID 10916](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/10916)

## About

Uranium dioxide or uranium(IV) oxide (UO2), also known as urania or uranous oxide, is an oxide of uranium, and is a black, radioactive, crystalline powder that naturally occurs in the mineral uraninite. It is used in nuclear fuel rods in nuclear reactors. A mixture of uranium and plutonium dioxides is used as MOX fuel. It has been used as an orange, yellow, green, and black color in ceramic glazes and glass.

<p><small>Source: <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uranium_dioxide?oldid=1380085553">Wikipedia</a> · <a href="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/">CC BY-SA 4.0</a></small></p>

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Uranium dioxide is used for fuel in nuclear reactors. Uranium oxides are also used to colour glass and ceramics.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Uranium is combined with either bicarbonate or a plasma protein in the blood but once in the kidney, it is released and forms complexes with phosphate ligands and proteins in the tubular wall, causing damage. Uranium may also inhibit both sodium transport-dependent and independent ATP utilization and mitochondrial oxidative phosphorylation in the renal proximal tubule. …
- **Health effects:** Uranium primarily damages the kidney, but may also damage the lungs, central nervous system, and immune system. Uranium's radioactivity is believed to damage the DNA, resulting in carcinogenic effects and reproductive and developmental damage.
- **Carcinogenicity:** Uranium: Group 1, carcinogenic to humans
- **Minimal risk levels (ATSDR):** Intermediate Inhalation: 0.0004 mg/m3 (Soluble uranium salts); Chronic Inhalation: 0.0003 mg/m3 (Soluble uranium salts); Intermediate Oral: 0.002 mg/kg/day (Soluble uranium salts); Intermediate Inhalation: 0.008 mg/m3 (Insoluble uranium compounds)
