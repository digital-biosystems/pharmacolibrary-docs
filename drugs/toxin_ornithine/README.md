# Ornithine

- **generic name:** Ornithine
- **T3DB:** [T3D4329](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4329) · **DrugBank:** [DB00129](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00129) · **PubChem:** [CID 6262](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/6262)
- **groups:** approved, investigational, nutraceutical

## About

Ornithine is an amino acid produced in the urea cycle by the splitting off of urea from arginine. It is a central part of the urea cycle, which allows for the disposal of excess nitrogen. L-Ornithine is also a precursor of citrulline and arginine. …

- **Category:** Drug, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin, Animal Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** absorbed from the small intestine via a sodium-dependent active transport process
- **Sources and uses:** Used for nutritional supplementation, also for treating dietary shortage or imbalance. It has been claimed that ornithine improves athletic performance, has anabolic effects, has wound-healing effects, and is immuno-enhancing.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** L-Ornithine is metabolised to L-arginine. L-arginine stimulates the pituitary release of growth hormone. …
- **Health effects:** Chronically high levels of ornithine are associated with at least 9 inborn errors of metabolism including: Cystathionine Beta-Synthase Deficiency, Hyperornithinemia with gyrate atrophy, Hyperornithinemia-hyperammonemia-homocitrullinuria syndrome, Hyperornithinemia-hyperammonemia-homocitrullinuria syndrome, Hyperprolinemia Type II, Lysinuric Protein Intolerance, Ornithine Aminotransferase Deficiency, Ornithine Transcarbamylase Deficiency and Prolinemia Type II.
- **Acute toxicity:** Oral, rat LD<sub>50</sub> = 10000 mg/kg
