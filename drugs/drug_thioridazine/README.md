## About

Thioridazine is a first-generation antipsychotic phenothiazine used for schizophrenia and schizophreniform disorder. It is administered orally in tablet, pill, solution, or suspension forms. The branded product was withdrawn worldwide due to severe cardiac arrhythmias, but generic versions remain available in the United States.
