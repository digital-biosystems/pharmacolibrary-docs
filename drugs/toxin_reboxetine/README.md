- **groups:** approved, investigational, withdrawn

## About

Reboxetine is an antidepressant drug used in the treatment of clinical depression, panic disorder and ADD/ADHD. Its mesylate (i.e. methanesulfonate) salt is sold under tradenames including Edronax, Norebox, Prolift, Solvex, Davedax or Vestra. Reboxetine has two chiral centers, but it only exists as two enantiomers, (R,R)-(-)- and (S,S)-(+)-reboxetine.

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** reboxetine is rapidly and extensively absorbed following oral administration.
- **Sources and uses:** For the treatment of clinical depression.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Reboxetine is a selective inhibitor of noradrenaline reuptake. It inhibits noradrenaline reuptake <i>in vitro</i> to a similar extent to the tricyclic antidepressant desmethylimipramine. …
