### Reviewer guidance

**Dextrorphan intercompartmental clearance is reported with an inverse time unit instead of volume per time, causing a dimensional mismatch that prevented unit conversion.**

The parameter labeled k12 for Dextrorphan is defined as apparent intercompartmental clearance, requiring a volume over time unit such as L/h. The record specifies the unit as h⁻¹, which is dimensionally incompatible with clearance. Because this specific unit representation could not be mapped to standard SI units, the parameter value remained unconverted. Extracted — dextromethorphan: kabs 2.6 h−1, V/F 4.87e+03 L, kel 0.1 h−1; Dextrorphan: kabs 9.1 h−1, kel 0.2 h−1, V1/F 6.98e+03 L, Q 0.57 h−1.
