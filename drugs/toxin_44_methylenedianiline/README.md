# 4,4'-Methylenedianiline

- **generic name:** 4,4'-Methylenedianiline
- **T3DB:** [T3D4672](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4672) · **PubChem:** [CID 7577](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/7577)

## About

4,4′-Methylenedianiline (MDA) is an organic compound with the formula CH2(C6H4NH2)2. It is a colorless solid, although commercial samples can appear yellow or brown. It is produced on an industrial scale, mainly as a precursor to polyurethanes.

<p><small>Source: <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4%2C4%27-Methylenedianiline?oldid=1380083435">Wikipedia</a> · <a href="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/">CC BY-SA 4.0</a></small></p>

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** inhalation, oral, dermal
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** After 4,4'-Methylenedianiline enters to our body, it is transported in the blood or stored in tissues. The N-N-acetylation of the compound leads to the formation of some toxic derivatives. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 2B, possibly carcinogenic to humans.
