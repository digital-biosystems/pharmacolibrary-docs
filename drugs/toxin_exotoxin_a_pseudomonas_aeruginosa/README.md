# Exotoxin A (Pseudomonas aeruginosa)

- **generic name:** Exotoxin A (Pseudomonas aeruginosa)
- **T3DB:** [T3D2607](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2607) · **PubChem:** none

## About

Pseudomonas exotoxin (or exotoxin A) is an exotoxin produced by Pseudomonas aeruginosa.

- **Category:** Protein, Bacterial Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** ingestion, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Pseudomonas exotoxin (or exotoxin A) is an exotoxin produced by Pseudomonas aeruginosa.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Pseudomonas exotoxin has exactly the same mechanism of action as the diphtheria toxin; it causes the ADP ribosylation of eucaryotic elongation factor 2 resulting in inhibition of protein synthesis in the affected cell. Although it is partially-identical to diphtheria toxin, it is antigenically-distinct. …
- **Health effects:** Pseudomonas exotoxin (or exotoxin A) is an exotoxin produced by Pseudomonas aeruginosa. Pseudomonas aeruginosa can infect damaged tissues or people with reduced immunity. …
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 3 ug/mg (Intravenous, Mouse)
