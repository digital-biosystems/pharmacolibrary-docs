## About

Flunitrazepam is a benzodiazepine sedative prescribed for severe sleep disorders and to assist with anesthesia. It is given by mouth in the form of tablets, capsules, or liquid drops. Although approved for medical use, the drug is also widely recognized for its illicit application.
