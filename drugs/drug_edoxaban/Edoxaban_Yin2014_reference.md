### Reviewer guidance

**The record was rejected because the 13.1% value for female clearance was misinterpreted as a total clearance parameter instead of a covariate effect.**

The record was built from the abstract alone, so reported summary statistics stood in for a fitted model. The parameter labeled as total clearance actually describes a 13.1% difference between female and male subjects, creating a dimension mismatch. A second reader disagreed with the extraction of both the 67.2% relative bioavailability and the 13.1% clearance difference, marking them as null. Extracted — edoxaban: Frel 67.2 %, CL 13.1 %.

A second, independent reading of the paper (`gpt-oss:120b`) disagrees on which compound was dosed: this record has edoxaban, the second reading unknown; it also differs on 4 more fields. That field shapes the model, so the record is marked disputed.
