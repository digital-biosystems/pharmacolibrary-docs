# Chloroneb

- **generic name:** Chloroneb
- **T3DB:** [T3D3807](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3807) · **PubChem:** [CID 17581](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/17581)

## About

Chloroneb (1,4-dichloro-2,5-dimethoxybenzene) is a chlorinated dimethoxybenzene. It is used as a fungicide and is currently registered for use on a wide variety of food crops but is primarily used for pre-plant cottonseed treatment as well as on commercial turf and ornamentals. The markets for chloroneb seed treatment uses include: sugar beets, soybeans, cotton, and beans. …
