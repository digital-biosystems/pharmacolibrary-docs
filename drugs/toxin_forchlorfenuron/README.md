# Forchlorfenuron

- **generic name:** Forchlorfenuron
- **T3DB:** [T3D4513](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4513) · **PubChem:** [CID 93379](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/93379)

## About

Forchlorfenuron is a diphenylurea-derivative cytokinin growth stimulating substance used as plant growth regulator (PGR) to enhance fruit set, size and increase yields. It is absorbed by most plant parts and acts synergistically with natural auxins to promote cell division and growth. It has been approved for use on kiwi fruit and grapes in the USA, and it has been associated with exploding watermelons in China. …

- **Category:** Food Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Sources and uses:** Forchlorfenuron is a diphenylurea-derivative cytokinin growth stimulating substance used as plant growth regulator (PGR) to enhance fruit set, size and increase yields. Forchlorfenuronis is commonly used in horticulture to stimulate the growth of kiwi fruit and grapes.
