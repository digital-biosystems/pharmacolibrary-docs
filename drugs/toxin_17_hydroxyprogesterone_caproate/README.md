# 17-Hydroxyprogesterone caproate

- **generic name:** 17-Hydroxyprogesterone caproate
- **T3DB:** [T3D4792](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4792) · **PubChem:** [CID 169870](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/169870)

## About

17α-Hydroxyprogesterone caproate is a synthetic steroid hormone that is similar to medroxyprogesterone acetate and megestrol acetate. It is an ester derivative of 17α-hydroxyprogesterone formed from caproic acid (hexanoic acid). 17α-Hydroxyprogesterone caproate was previously marketed under the trade name Delalutin by Squibb, which was approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) in 1956 and withdrawn from marketing in 1999. …

- **Category:** Food Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 2B, possibly carcinogenic to humans.
