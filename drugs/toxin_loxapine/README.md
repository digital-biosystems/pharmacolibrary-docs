# Loxapine

- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

Loxapine is only found in individuals that have used or taken this drug. It is an antipsychotic agent used in schizophrenia. Loxapine is a dopamine antagonist, and also a serotonin 5-HT2 blocker. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, Intramuscular.
Systemic bioavailability of the parent drug was only about one third that after an equivalent intramuscular dose (25 mg base) in male volunteers.
- **Sources and uses:** For the management of the manifestations of psychotic disorders such as schizophrenia
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Loxapine is a dopamine antagonist, and also a serotonin 5-HT2 blocker. The exact mode of action of Loxapine has not been established, however changes in the level of excitability of subcortical inhibitory areas have been observed in several animal species in association with such manifestations of tranquilization as calming effects and suppression of aggressive behavior.
- **Acute toxicity:** LD<sub>50</sub>=65 mg/kg (Orally in mice)
- **Treatment:** The treatment of overdosage is essentially symptomatic and supportive. …
