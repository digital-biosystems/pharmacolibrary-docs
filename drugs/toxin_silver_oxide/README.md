# Silver oxide

- **generic name:** Silver oxide
- **T3DB:** [T3D1612](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D1612) · **PubChem:** [CID 88641](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/88641)
- **groups:** investigational

## About

Silver oxide is an oxide of silver. It is used to prepare other silver compounds, and in the silver oxide battery. Silver is a metallic element with the chemical symbol Ag and atomic number 47. …

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Silver oxide is used to prepare other silver compounds, and in the silver oxide battery.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Metallic silver is oxidized and may deposit in the tissues, causing arygria. The silver ion is known to inhibit glutathione peroxidase and NA+,K+-ATPase activity, disrupting selenium-catalyzed sulfhydryl oxidation-reduction reactions and intracellular ion concentrations, respectively. …
- **Health effects:** Silver itself is not toxic to humans, but most silver salts are. In large doses, silver and compounds containing it can be absorbed into the circulatory system and become deposited in various body tissues, leading to argyria, which results in a blue-grayish pigmentation of the skin, eyes, and mucous membranes. …
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 1027 mg/kg (Oral, Mouse)
- **Treatment:** EYES: irrigate opened eyes for several minutes under running water. …
- **Detected in:** blood, cerebrospinal fluid
