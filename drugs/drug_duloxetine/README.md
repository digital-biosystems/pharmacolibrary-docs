## About

Duloxetine is a serotonin–norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor used to treat major depressive disorder, anxiety disorders, and chronic pain conditions such as fibromyalgia and neuropathic pain. The medication is taken by mouth and is well absorbed in the gut. It is authorized in the European Union and is available as a generic medication in multiple regions.
