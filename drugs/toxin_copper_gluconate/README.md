# Copper gluconate

- **generic name:** Copper gluconate
- **T3DB:** [T3D1193](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D1193) · **PubChem:** [CID 10692](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/10692)
- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

Copper gluconate is the copper salt of D-gluconic acid. It is used in dietary supplements and to treat conditions such as acne vulgaris, common cold, hypertension, premature labor, Leishmaniasis, visceral postoperative complications. Copper is a chemical element with the symbol Cu and atomic number 29. …

- **Category:** Household Toxin, Food Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Copper gluconate is used in dietary supplements and to treat conditions such as acne vulgaris, common cold, hypertension, premature labor, Leishmaniasis, visceral postoperative complications.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Excess copper is sequestered within hepatocyte lysosomes, where it is complexed with metallothionein. Copper hepatotoxicity is believed to occur when the lysosomes become saturated and copper accumulates in the nucleus, causing nuclear damage. …
- **Health effects:** People must absorb small amounts of copper every day because copper is essential for good health, however, high levels of copper can be harmful. Very-high doses of copper can cause damage to your liver and kidneys, and can even cause death. …
- **Lethal dose:** 10 to 20 grams for an adult human (copper salts).
- **Minimal risk levels (ATSDR):** Acute Oral: 0.01 mg/kg/day 
Intermediate Oral: 0.01 mg/kg/day
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 1710 mg/kg (Oral, Rat)
- **Detected in:** blood, cerebrospinal fluid, saliva, urine
