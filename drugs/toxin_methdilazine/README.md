# Methdilazine

- **generic name:** Methdilazine
- **T3DB:** [T3D2924](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2924) · **DrugBank:** [DB00902](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00902) · **PubChem:** [CID 14677](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/14677)
- **groups:** approved

## About

Methdilazine is only found in individuals that have used or taken this drug. It is a phenothiazine compound with antihistaminic activity. It is used in the treatment of various dermatoses to relieve pruritus.Methdilazine binds to the histamine H<sub>1</sub> receptor. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** well absorbed in the digestive tract.
- **Sources and uses:** Used for the symptomatic relief of hypersensitivity reactions and particularly for the control of pruritic skin disorders
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Methdilazine binds to the histamine H<sub>1</sub> receptor. This blocks the action of endogenous histamine, which subsequently leads to temporary relief of the negative symptoms brought on by histamine.
