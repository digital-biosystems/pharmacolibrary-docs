## About

Efbemalenograstim alfa is a long-acting colony stimulating factor (a G-CSF fusion protein) intended to stimulate white blood cell production, such as in neutropenia associated with cancer treatment. It is not an established marketed medicine; it has been investigated in clinical studies and is not authorised in the European Union.
