# Penicillic acid

- **generic name:** Penicillic acid
- **T3DB:** [T3D3738](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3738) · **PubChem:** [CID 5385314](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/5385314)

## About

Penicillic acid is a mycotoxin produced by several species of Aspergillus and Penicillium. It has antibiotic activity and is cytotoxic, hepatotoxic, and carcinogenic. Penicillic acid is a human health hazard because it can be found on contaminated crops such as corn.

- **Category:** Fungal Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, dermal, inhalation, and parenteral (contaminated drugs).
- **Sources and uses:** Penicillic acid is a mycotoxin produced by several species of Aspergillus and Penicillium. It is a human health hazard because it can be found on contaminated crops such as corn.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Penicillic acid has been shown to inhibit alcohol and lactate dehydrogenases by forming covalent adducts with either cysteine or lysine residues at the enzyme active sites. Penicillic acid also binds directly to the active center cysteine in the large subunit of caspase-8, thus inhibiting FasL-induced apoptosis by targeting self-processing of caspase-8. …
- **Health effects:** Penicillic acid is cytotoxic, hepatotoxic, and carcinogenic.
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 3, not classifiable as to its carcinogenicity to humans.
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 600 mg/kg (Oral, Mouse)
LD50: 250 mg/kg (Intravenous, Mouse)
LD50: 90 mg/kg (Intraperitoneal, Mouse)
