# Encainide

- **generic name:** Encainide
- **T3DB:** [T3D4738](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4738) · **DrugBank:** [DB01228](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB01228) · **PubChem:** none
- **groups:** approved, withdrawn

## About

All drug products containing encainide hydrochloride. Encainide hydrochloride, formerly marketed as Enkaid capsules, was associated with increased death rates in patients who had asymptomatic heart rhythm abnormalities after a recent heart attack. The manufacturer of Enkaid capsules voluntarily withdrew the product from the US market on December 16, 1991.

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Sources and uses:** Encainide is a class Ic antiarrhythmic agent which was used for management of irregular heartbeats, such as atrial fibrillation, atrial flutter, ventricular tachycardia, and ventricular fibrillation.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Encainide is a sodium channel blocker, binding to voltage gated sodium channels. It stabilizes the neuronal membrane by inhibiting the ionic fluxes required for the initiation and conduction of impulses. …
