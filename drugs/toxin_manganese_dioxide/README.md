# Manganese dioxide

- **generic name:** Manganese dioxide
- **T3DB:** [T3D1135](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D1135) · **PubChem:** [CID 14801](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/14801)

## About

Manganese dioxide is an oxide of manganese that occurs naturally as the mineral pyrolusite, the main ore of manganese. It is used primarily for dry-cell batteries, but is also used as an oxidant in organic synthesis. Manganese is a naturally occurring metal with the symbol Mn and the atomic number 25. …

- **Category:** Household Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation
- **Sources and uses:** Manganese dioxide is used primarily for dry-cell batteries, but is also used as an oxidant in organic synthesis.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Manganese is a cellular toxicant that can impair transport systems, enzyme activities, and receptor functions. It primarily targets the central nervous system, particularily the globus pallidus of the basal ganglia. …
- **Health effects:** Manganese mainly affects the nervous system and may cause behavioral changes and other nervous system effects, which include movements that may become slow and clumsy. This combination of symptoms when sufficiently severe is referred to as “manganism”.
- **Minimal risk levels (ATSDR):** Chronic Inhalation: 0.0003 mg/m3
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: >3478 mg/kg (Oral, Rat)
- **Treatment:** EYES: irrigate opened eyes for several minutes under running water. …
- **Detected in:** blood, cerebrospinal fluid, saliva, urine
