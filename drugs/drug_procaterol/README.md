# procaterol

- **groups:** approved, withdrawn

## About

Procaterol is a selective beta-2-adrenergic receptor agonist prescribed to manage asthma. It is administered orally or via inhalation to relieve respiratory symptoms. The medication was widely utilized in Japan and other nations but was not sold in the United States or United Kingdom.
