## About

Ciprofloxacin is a fluoroquinolone antibiotic used to treat various bacterial infections, including pneumonia, urinary tract infections, and typhoid fever. It is usually taken orally, but it can also be given intravenously or as eye and ear drops for specific infections. Ciprofloxacin is widely used as a generic medication and is listed as critically important for human medicine.
