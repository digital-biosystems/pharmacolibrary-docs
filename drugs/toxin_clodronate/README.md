# Clodronate

## About

Clodronate is only found in individuals that have used or taken this drug. It is a diphosphonate which affects calcium metabolism. It inhibits bone resorption and soft tissue calcification. [PubChem]The bisphosphonate group binds strongly to the bone mineral, hydroxyapatite. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, Intravenous.
After oral administration, absorption is estimated at 1&ndash, 3% of the ingested dose because of the low uptake from the gastrointestinal tract.
- **Sources and uses:** A diphosphonate which affects calcium metabolism. It inhibits bone resorption and soft tissue calcification. …
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** The bisphosphonate group binds strongly to the bone mineral, hydroxyapatite. This explains the specific pharmacological action of these compounds on mineralized tissues, especially bone. …
- **Health effects:** Decreases in serum calcium following substantial overdosage may be expected in some patients.
