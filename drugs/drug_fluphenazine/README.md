## About

Fluphenazine is a typical antipsychotic belonging to the phenothiazine class, used primarily to treat chronic psychoses such as schizophrenia. It is administered orally as tablets or liquids, or via injection intramuscularly or subcutaneously; depot injection versions are available for increased adherence. It is widely used as a generic medication and is listed as an essential medicine by the World Health Organization, though it was discontinued in Australia.
