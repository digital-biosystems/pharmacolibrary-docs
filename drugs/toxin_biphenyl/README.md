# Biphenyl

- **generic name:** Biphenyl
- **T3DB:** [T3D0389](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D0389) · **PubChem:** [CID 7095](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/7095)

## About

Biphenyl is found in alcoholic beverages. Fungistat, especially for citrus fruits. Biphenyl is used as food preservative and flavouring agent. …

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** inhalation, dermal, eye contact
- **Sources and uses:** Organic synthesis; heat transfer agent; fungistat in packaging of citrus fruit; plant disease control; manufacture of benzidine; dyeing assistant for polyesters.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Biphenyl alters the permeability properties of mitochondrial membranes.
- **Health effects:** Exposure to diphenyl can cause necrosis in the liver and kidney, with regions of cirrhosis in liver; also, degenerative changes in heart muscle, damage to the central and peripheral nervous systems, as well as death can result from diphenyl poisoning.
- **Carcinogenicity:** No indication of carcinogenicity (not listed by IARC).
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 3280 mg/kg (Oral, Rat)
LD50: 2400 mg/kg (Oral, Rabbit)
LD50: 2500 mg/kg (Dermal, Rabbit)
- **Treatment:** Consider gastric lavage, after ingestion of a potentially life-threatening amount of poison if it can be performed soon after ingestion. …
