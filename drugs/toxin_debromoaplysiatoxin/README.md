# Debromoaplysiatoxin

- **generic name:** Debromoaplysiatoxin
- **T3DB:** [T3D4056](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4056) · **PubChem:** [CID 440576](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/440576)

## About

Debromoaplysiatoxin is an algal toxin found in blue-green algae. It is one of the causative toxins of a series of red alga (Gracilaria coronopifolia) poisonings in Hawaii, which broke out in succession in September of 1994.

- **Category:** Animal Toxin, Bacterial Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Sources and uses:** Debromoaplysiatoxin is an algal toxin found in blue-green algae. It is one of the causative toxins of a series of red alga (Gracilaria coronopifolia) poisonings in Hawaii, which broke out in succession in September of 1994.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Debromoaplysiatoxin have the ability to evoke biological and biochemical responses in cells, and belong to the TPA (12-O-tetradecanoylphorbol 13-acetate) type tumor promoters. Debromoaplysiatoxin is a typical tumor promoter. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** Not listed by IARC.
