## About

Methotrexate is an antimetabolite and immunosuppressant used to treat cancers and autoimmune conditions. It is administered orally or via intravenous, subcutaneous, intramuscular, intrathecal, or intra-arterial injection. Blood levels are routinely measured to guide dosing. It is a widely used essential medicine authorized in the European Union.
