### Reviewer guidance

**KD was labeled as an equilibrium dissociation constant but had a half-life unit, and clearance was outside physiological limits.**

The equilibrium dissociation constant for methotrexate-target binding was assigned a value of 0.0945 with a time unit of hours. This conflicts with the parameter's meaning as a binding affinity measure, which is dimensionally distinct from a rate constant. Additionally, the total clearance for 7-OH methotrexate was calculated as 0.123 L/hr.kg, which the review identified as outside the physiological window. Extracted — methotrexate: AUC 278 μM.hr, Cmax 21.1 μM, tmax 9.2 hr, KD 0.0945 hr, t1/2z 7.33 hr; 7-OH methotrexate: CL 0.123 l/hr.kg, V 0.3 L, tlag 0.36 hour.
