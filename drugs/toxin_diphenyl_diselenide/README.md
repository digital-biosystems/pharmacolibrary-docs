# Diphenyl diselenide

- **generic name:** Diphenyl diselenide
- **T3DB:** [T3D1818](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D1818) · **PubChem:** [CID 15460](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/15460)

## About

Chemical compound.

<p><small>Source: <a href="https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q3538196">Wikidata Q3538196</a> · <a href="https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/">CC0</a></small></p>

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Diphenyl diselenide is a useful reagent in organic synthesis.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Selenium readily substitutes for sulfur in biomolecules and in many biochemical reactions, especially when the concentration of selenium is high and the concentration of sulfur is low. Inactivation of the sulfhydryl enzymes necessary for oxidative reactions in cellular respiration, through effects on mitochondrial and microsomal electron transport, might contribute to acute selenium toxicity. …
- **Health effects:** Chronic oral exposure to high concentrations of selenium compounds can produce a disease called selenosis. The major signs of selenosis are hair loss, nail brittleness, and neurological abnormalities (such as numbness and other odd sensations in the extremities). …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 3, not classifiable as to its carcinogenicity to humans.
- **Minimal risk levels (ATSDR):** Chronic Oral: 0.005 mg/kg/day
