# Cadmium telluride

- **generic name:** Cadmium telluride
- **T3DB:** [T3D0387](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D0387) · **PubChem:** [CID 91501](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/91501)

## About

Cadmium telluride (CdTe) is a stable crystalline compound formed from cadmium and tellurium. It is mainly used as the semiconducting material in cadmium telluride photovoltaics and an infrared optical window. It is usually sandwiched with cadmium sulfide to form a p–n junction solar PV cell.

<p><small>Source: <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cadmium_telluride?oldid=1379971805">Wikipedia</a> · <a href="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/">CC BY-SA 4.0</a></small></p>

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Cadmium telluride is used as a semiconductor in solar cells and in infrared, x-ray, and gamma-ray detectors.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Cadmium initially binds to metallothionein and is transported to the kidney. Toxic effects are observed once the concentration of cadmium exceeds that of available metallothionein, and it has also been shown that the cadmium-metallothionein complex may be damaging. …
- **Health effects:** Chronic exposure to cadmium fumes can cause chemical pneumonitis, pulmonary edema, and lung diseases such as bronchitis and emphysema. Cadmium also accumulates in the kidneys, causing permanent damage. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 1, carcinogenic to humans.
- **Minimal risk levels (ATSDR):** Acute Inhalation: 0.00003 mg/m3 
Chronic Inhalation: 0.00001 mg/m3 
Intermediate Oral: 0.0005 mg/kg/day 
Chronic Oral: 0.0001 mg/kg/day
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 2100 mg/kg (Intraperitoneal, Mouse)
