## About

Lopinavir is a protease inhibitor antiviral used to treat HIV infections, usually combined with ritonavir. It is given orally as tablets or capsules and is poorly absorbed by mouth. It is a WHO essential medicine, used as second-line therapy in some regions while remaining common in low- and middle-income countries, especially for children.
