# Mercury(II) sulfide

- **generic name:** Mercury(II) sulfide
- **T3DB:** [T3D0341](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D0341) · **PubChem:** [CID 62402](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/62402)

## About

Mercury sulfide or mercury(II) sulfide is a chemical compound composed of the chemical elements mercury and sulfur. It is represented by the chemical formula HgS. It is virtually insoluble in water.

<p><small>Source: <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercury_sulfide?oldid=1380082364">Wikipedia</a> · <a href="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/">CC BY-SA 4.0</a></small></p>

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Mercury(II) sulfide is used as a semiconductor and as a red pigment, vermillion.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** High-affinity binding of the divalent mercuric ion to thiol or sulfhydryl groups of proteins is believed to be the major mechanism for the activity of mercury. Through alterations in intracellular thiol status, mercury can promote oxidative stress, lipid peroxidation, mitochondrial dysfunction, and changes in heme metabolism. …
- **Health effects:** Mercury mainly affects the nervous system. Exposure to high levels of metallic, inorganic, or organic mercury can permanently damage the brain, kidneys, and developing fetus. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 3, not classifiable as to its carcinogenicity to humans.
- **Minimal risk levels (ATSDR):** Chronic Inhalation: 0.0002 mg/m3
