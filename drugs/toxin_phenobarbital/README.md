# Phenobarbital

- **generic name:** Phenobarbital
- **T3DB:** [T3D3006](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3006) · **DrugBank:** [DB01174](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB01174) · **PubChem:** [CID 4763](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/4763)
- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

Phenobarbital is only found in individuals that have used or taken this drug. It is a barbituric acid derivative that acts as a nonselective central nervous system depressant. It promotes binding to inhibitory gamma-aminobutyric acid subtype receptors, and modulates chloride currents through receptor channels. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** absorbed in varying degrees following oral, rectal or parenteral administration. The salts are more rapidly absorbed than are the acids. The rate of absorption is increased if the sodium salt is ingested as a dilute solution or taken on an empty stomach.
- **Sources and uses:** For the treatment of all types of seizures except absence seizures.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Phenobarbital acts on GABAA receptors, increasing synaptic inhibition. This has the effect of elevating seizure threshold and reducing the spread of seizure activity from a seizure focus. …
- **Health effects:** They cause slurred speech, disorientation and "drunken" behavior. They are physically and psychologically addictive. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 2B, possibly carcinogenic to humans.
