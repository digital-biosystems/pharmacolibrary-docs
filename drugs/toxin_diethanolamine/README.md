# Diethanolamine

- **generic name:** Diethanolamine
- **T3DB:** [T3D4845](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4845) · **PubChem:** [CID 8113](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/8113)
- **groups:** investigational

## About

Diethanolamine, often abbreviated as DEA, is an organic chemical compound which is both a secondary amine and a dialcohol. A dialcohol has two hydroxyl groups in its molecule. Like other amines, diethanolamine acts as a weak base. …

- **Category:** Household Toxin, Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Food Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 2B, possibly carcinogenic to humans.
