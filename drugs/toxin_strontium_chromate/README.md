# Strontium chromate

- **generic name:** Strontium chromate
- **T3DB:** [T3D0712](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D0712) · **PubChem:** [CID 24599](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/24599)

## About

Strontium chromate is an inorganic compound with the formula SrCrO4.

<p><small>Source: <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strontium_chromate?oldid=1380007123">Wikipedia</a> · <a href="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/">CC BY-SA 4.0</a></small></p>

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** inhalation, oral, dermal
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Hexavalent chromium's carcinogenic effects are caused by its metabolites, pentavalent and trivalent chromium. The DNA damage may be caused by hydroxyl radicals produced during reoxidation of pentavalent chromium by hydrogen peroxide molecules present in the cell. …
- **Health effects:** Hexavalent chromium is a known carcinogen. Chronic inhalation especially has been linked to lung cancer. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 1, carcinogenic to humans.
- **Minimal risk levels (ATSDR):** Intermediate Oral: 0.005 mg/kg/day 
Chronic Oral: 0.001 mg/kg/day
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 811 mg/kg (Oral, Rat)
