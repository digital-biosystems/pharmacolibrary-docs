# Dibromomethane

- **generic name:** Dibromomethane
- **T3DB:** [T3D1795](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D1795) · **PubChem:** [CID 3024](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/3024)

## About

Dibromomethane is an organobromide compound. It may be produced synthetically or naturally by marine algae. Dibromomethane is used as a solvent, gage fluid and in organic synthesis. …

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** It may be produced synthetically or naturally by marine algae. Dibromomethane is used as a solvent, gage fluid and in organic synthesis. …
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Bromine is a powerful oxidizing agent and is able to release oxygen free radicals from the water in mucous membranes. These free radicals are also potent oxidizers and produce tissue damage. …
- **Health effects:** Bromine vapour causes irritation and direct damage to the mucous membranes. Elemental bromine also burns the skin. …
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 3738 mg/kg (Subcutaneous, Mouse); LC50: 40 g/m3 over 2 hours (Inhalation, Rat)
- **Treatment:** EYES: irrigate opened eyes for several minutes under running water. …
