**The risankizumab model's simulated peak concentration is 0.0016 versus the paper's 0.0145, and bioavailability was left at a default value rather than extracted.**

The simulated maximum concentration of 0.0016 kg/m3 differs from the paper's reported 0.0145 by a factor of over 9. This discrepancy indicates the model structure or parameters do not align with the observed peak levels. Additionally, the bioavailability parameter was not extracted from the paper, causing the model to rely on library placeholders for this critical property. Extracted — risankizumab: CL 0.24 L/day, V 9.12 L, kabs 0.908 day−1.
