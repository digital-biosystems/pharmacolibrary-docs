# Ethyl ether

- **generic name:** Ethyl ether
- **T3DB:** [T3D0255](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D0255) · **PubChem:** [CID 3283](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/3283)
- **groups:** investigational

## About

Diethyl ether, also known as ether and ethoxyethane, is a clear, colorless, and highly flammable liquid with a low boiling point and a characteristic odor. It is the most common member of a class of chemical compounds known generically as ethers. Diethyl ether is used as a common solvent and has been used as a general anesthetic. …

- **Category:** Household Toxin, Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation
- **Sources and uses:** Diethyl ether is used as a common solvent and has been used as a general anesthetic. It can also be found in recreational drug use.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Diethyl ether inhibits alcohol dehydrogenase. This slows down the metabolism of ethanol and also inhibits the metabolism of other drugs requiring oxidative metabolism.
- **Health effects:** Diethyl ether causes CNS depression. Death due to respiratory depression may result from severe and prolonged exposure.
- **Lethal dose:** 30 mL for an adult human.
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 996 mg/kg (Intravenous, Mouse)
LD50: 2420 mg/kg (Intraperitoneal, Mouse)
LD50: 1213 mg/kg (Oral, Rat)
LC50: 186 mg/L (Inhalation, Mouse)
- **Treatment:** There is no antidote for diethyl ether poisoning, thus treatment is symptomatic and supportive.
