### Reviewer guidance

**The ciclesonide parent–metabolite record was quarantined because ciclesonide's clearance, volume of distribution, absorption rate constant and lag time had no values and library placeholders stood in, while only des-CIC's CLm/F (302 L/h) and Vm/F (1310 L) were reported.**

The paper was read abstract-only, so summary statistics stood in for a fitted model, and the structure did not match the required parent–metabolite topology. The builder defaulted ciclesonide's Cl, Vd, ka and Tlag and invented an absorption rate constant not reported in the source, both deemed unacceptable. The apparent parameterization assumed F=1 and Fm=1 with no molar correction, so des-CIC's CLm/F (302 L/h) and Vm/F (1310 L) are apparent (CL/fm, V/fm) values. Extracted — ciclesonide: CLm/F 302 L/h, Vm/F 1.31e+03 L.

A second, independent reading of the paper (`gpt-oss-120b`) disagrees on which compound was dosed: this record has ciclesonide, the second reading unknown; it also differs on 7 more fields. That field shapes the model, so the record is marked disputed.
