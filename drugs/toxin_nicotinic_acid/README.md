# Nicotinic acid

- **generic name:** Nicotinic acid
- **T3DB:** [T3D2841](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2841) · **DrugBank:** [DB00627](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00627) · **PubChem:** [CID 938](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/938)
- **groups:** approved, investigational, nutraceutical

## About

Nicotinic acid, also known as niacin or vitamin B3, is a water-soluble vitamin whose derivatives such as NADH, NAD, NAD+, and NADP play essential roles in energy metabolism in the living cell and DNA repair. The designation vitamin B3 also includes the amide form, nicotinamide or niacinamide. Severe lack of niacin causes the deficiency disease pellagra, whereas a mild deficiency slows down the metabolism decreasing cold tolerance. …

- **Category:** Drug, Household Toxin, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, intramuscular. Both nicotinic acid and nicotinamide are efficiently absorbed from the stomach and small intestine.
- **Sources and uses:** For the treatment of type IV and V hyperlipidemia. It is indicated as ajunctive therapy. …
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Niacin binds to Nicotinate D-ribonucleotide phyrophsopate phosphoribosyltransferase, Nicotinic acid phosphoribosyltransferase, Nicotinate N-methyltransferase and the Niacin receptor. Niacin is the precursor to nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide (NAD) and nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide phosphate (NADP), which are vital cofactors for dozens of enzymes. …
- **Health effects:** Niacin at extremely high doses can have life-threatening acute toxic reactions. Extremely high doses of niacin can also cause niacin maculopathy, a thickening of the macula and retina which leads to blurred vision and blindness. …
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 7000 mg/kg (Oral, Rat)
LD50: 730 mg/kg (Intraperitoneal, Rat)
LD50: 3500 mg/kg (Subcutaneous, Mouse)
- **Treatment:** Supportive measures should be undertaken in the event of an overdose.
- **Detected in:** blood, cerebrospinal fluid, saliva, urine
