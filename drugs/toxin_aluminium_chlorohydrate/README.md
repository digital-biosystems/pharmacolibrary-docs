# Aluminium chlorohydrate

- **generic name:** Aluminium chlorohydrate
- **T3DB:** [T3D1486](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D1486) · **PubChem:** none
- **groups:** approved

## About

Aluminium chlorohydrate is a group of specific aluminium salts having the general formula AlnCl(3n-m)(OH)m. It is used in deodorants and antiperspirants and as a coagulant in water purification. The Food and Drug Administration considers the use of aluminium chlorohydrate in antiperspirants to be safe and it is permitted in concentrations up to 25%. …

- **Category:** Household Toxin, Food Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation
- **Sources and uses:** Aluminium chlorohydrate is used in deodorants and antiperspirants and as a flocculant in water purification.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** The main target organs of aluminum are the central nervous system and bone. Aluminum binds with dietary phosphorus and impairs gastrointestinal absorption of phosphorus. …
- **Health effects:** Aluminum targets the nervous system and causes decreased nervous system performance and is associated with altered function of the blood-brain barrier. The accumulation of aluminum in the body may cause bone or brain diseases. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** Not listed by IARC. …
- **Minimal risk levels (ATSDR):** Intermediate Oral: 1.0 mg/kg/day 
Chronic Oral: 1.0 mg/kg/day
- **Treatment:** EYES: irrigate opened eyes for several minutes under running water. …
