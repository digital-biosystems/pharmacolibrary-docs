# Gamma-Butyrolactone

- **generic name:** Gamma-Butyrolactone
- **T3DB:** [T3D2562](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2562) · **DrugBank:** [DB04699](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB04699) · **PubChem:** [CID 7302](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/7302)
- **groups:** experimental, illicit

## About

One of the furans with a carbonyl thereby forming a cyclic lactone. It is an endogenous compound made from gamma-aminobutyrate and is the precursor of gamma-hydroxybutyrate. It is also used as a pharmacological agent and solvent.

- **Category:** Household Toxin, Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Food Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** inhalation, oral, injection, dermal.
- **Sources and uses:** Common solvent and reagent in chemistry and is used as an aroma compound, as a stain remover, as a superglue remover, as a paint stripper, and as a solvent in some wet aluminium electrolytic capacitors.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Gamma-butyrolactone is rapidly converted to gamma-hydroxybutyrate. This may account for the subsequent central nervous system depressant. …
- **Health effects:** CNS depression; amnesia and hypotonia. Rarely, hypertension, orthostatic hypotension, apnea, dyskinesias, dystonias, and hypomania occur.
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 3, not classifiable as to its carcinogenicity to humans.
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 17.2 mL/kg (Oral, Rat) (581)
- **Treatment:** In case of oral exposure, administer a benzodiazepine IV. …
- **Detected in:** blood
