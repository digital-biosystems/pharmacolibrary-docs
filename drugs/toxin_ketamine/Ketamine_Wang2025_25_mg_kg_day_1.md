### Reviewer guidance

**Ketamine clearance (6993 mL/h/kg) and volume (6567 L/kg) are implausibly high, likely due to unit extraction errors, causing rejection.**

The extracted clearance for ketamine is 6993 mL/h/kg and the volume of distribution is 6567 L/kg. These values fall far outside physiological ranges for rats and dogs, indicating a likely magnitude error from the unit parsing process. The review notes that a reported unit could not be converted to SI, which may have contributed to this incorrect parameter scaling.
