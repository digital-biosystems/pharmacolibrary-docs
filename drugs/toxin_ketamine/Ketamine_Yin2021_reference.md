### Reviewer guidance

**The uptake rate constant for ketamine is incorrectly recorded with unit kg·d instead of inverse time, leading to rejection.**

The record for ketamine uptake lists the parameter kuptake with a unit of kg·d, which has dimensions of mass times time. This conflicts with the definition of an uptake rate constant, which must have dimensions of inverse time. Additionally, the model was constructed solely from abstract summary statistics rather than a fitted model. Extracted — ketamine: t1/2z 3.58 d, V 4.7 L/kg, kabs 3.3 /h.
