### Reviewer guidance

**The S-ketamine intercompartmental clearance Q3 is rejected because its unit L/h conflicts with the required volumetric flow per mass unit.**

The record contains structural parameters Q and Q2 for S-ketamine, but the rejection cites a dimensional error in Q3 where the listed unit is L/h. This unit represents volume per time, whereas the parameter requires volume per mass per time. The discrepancy prevented the conversion to standard SI units, leading to the record's rejection.
