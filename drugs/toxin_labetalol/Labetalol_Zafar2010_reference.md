### Reviewer guidance

**The record uses the midpoint of a printed range for the 19.8 h−1 absorption rate constant, failing to represent the reported variability.**

The absorption rate constant for Labetalol hydrochloride is recorded as 19.8 h−1, derived from a midpoint rather than the specific range reported in the source. The terminal half-life is listed as 3.5 hours and clearance as 8.0 mL/min, but the reliance on a single point estimate for the input kinetics compromises the record's accuracy.
