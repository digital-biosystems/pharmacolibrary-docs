### Reviewer guidance

**The clearance parameter was extracted as 8.0 mL/min labeled 'Endogenous CL', incorrectly treating labetalol disposition as biomarker kinetics.**

The clearance parameter carries the verbatim label 'Endogenous CL (mL/min)' with a value of 8.0, triggering a biomarker-kinetics rejection for a drug PK record. Although other parameters like a volume of 4.0 L/kg and a half-life of 1.68 h were extracted, the model builder substituted a single retention pool sharing the half-life, which lacks a volume and cannot predict concentration. This structural mismatch caused severe prediction errors, including a simulated Cmax of 0.994 versus the reported 0.000178. Extracted — labetalol: t1/2z 1.68 h, CL 8 mL/min, V 4 L/kg, kabs 19.8 h−1.
