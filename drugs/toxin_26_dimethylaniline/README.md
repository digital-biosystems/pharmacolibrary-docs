# 2,6-Dimethylaniline

- **generic name:** 2,6-Dimethylaniline
- **T3DB:** [T3D4218](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4218) · **PubChem:** [CID 6896](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/6896)

## About

2,6-Dimethylaniline is a metabolite of lidocaine. Lidocaine, Xylocaine, or lignocaine is a common local anesthetic and antiarrhythmic drug. Lidocaine is used topically to relieve itching, burning and pain from skin inflammations, injected as a dental anesthetic or as a local anesthetic for minor surgery.

- **Category:** Cigarette Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Sources and uses:** 2,6-Dimethylaniline is found in cigarette smoke; it is a pharmacologically inactive metabolite of some drugs (e.g., the local anesthetic lidocaine) and pesticides (e.g., metalaxyl); and it is an impurity in technical grade metalaxyl.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Treatment of AS52 cells with N-OH-2,6-dimethylaniline (2,6-DMA) and 2,6-DMAP led to intracellular production of reactive oxygen species (ROS). DNA strand breaks were observed in a dose-dependent manner in AS52 cells when treated with each of the N-OH-2,6-DMA and 2,6-DMAP. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 2B, possibly carcinogenic to humans.
