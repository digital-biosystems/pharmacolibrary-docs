### Reviewer guidance

**Km and Vmax units are dimensionally invalid for Michaelis-Menten kinetics, and the record is abstract-only rather than a fitted model.**

The Km value of 8.3 microM represents a concentration, which conflicts with the required mass or substance per volume dimension for enzyme affinity. The Vmax value of 0.15 mumol/min was flagged for a unit conflict regarding the expected mass per time dimension. Additionally, the model was constructed solely from abstract summary statistics instead of a fitted model. Extracted — n nitrosodimethylamine: Km 8.3 microM, Vmax 0.15 mumol/min, CL 3.45e+03 mL/min, V 6.48e+04 mL, tlag 5.25 h.
