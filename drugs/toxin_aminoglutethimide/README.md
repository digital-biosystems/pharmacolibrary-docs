# Aminoglutethimide

- **generic name:** Aminoglutethimide
- **T3DB:** [T3D4806](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4806) · **DrugBank:** [DB00357](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00357) · **PubChem:** [CID 2145](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/2145)
- **groups:** approved, withdrawn

## About

An aromatase inhibitor that produces a state of 'medical' adrenalectomy by blocking the production of adrenal steroids. It also blocks the conversion of androgens to estrogens. Aminoglutethimide has been used in the treatment of advanced breast and prostate cancer. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** rapidly and completely absorbed from gastrointestinal tract. The bioavailability of tablets is equivalent to equal doses given as a solution.
- **Sources and uses:** For the suppression of adrenal function in selected patients with Cushing's syndrome, malignant neoplasm of the female breast, and carcinoma in situ of the breast.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Aminoglutethimide reduces the production of D5-pregnenolone and blocks several other steps in steroid synthesis, including the C-11, C-18, and C-21 hydroxylations and the hydroxylations required for the aromatization of androgens to estrogens, mediated through the binding of aminoglutethimide to cytochrome P-450 complexes. Specifically, the drug binds to and inhibits aromatase which is essential for the generation of estrogens from androstenedione and testosterone. …
- **Acute toxicity:** Oral LD50s (mg/kg): rats, 1800; dogs, >100. Intravenous LD50s (mg/kg): rats, 156; dogs, >100.
