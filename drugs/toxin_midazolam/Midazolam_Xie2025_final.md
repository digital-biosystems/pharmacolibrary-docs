### Reviewer guidance

**Record refused because simulated terminal half-life (0.33056611886714504 h) is inconsistent with paper values (35.5 h, 140.0 h) and midazolam absorption rate constant is missing.**

The simulated terminal half-life is 0.33056611886714504 h, which is far smaller than the 35.5 h and 140.0 h expected from the paper. The check could not compute valid ratios (0.0093 and 0.0024) due to unit handling issues. Additionally, the parameter coverage check failed because the absorption rate constant (kabs) for midazolam was neither extracted nor defaulted. Extracted — midazolam: CL 22.6 L/h, V 16 L, kabs 6.4 h−1; 1-hydroxymidazolam: CL 67.1 L/h, V 86.2 L, fm 0.6.
