**The metabolite clearance value of 264 L/h contradicts the parent drug's 43.9 L/h clearance, and bioavailability was substituted with library defaults rather than paper estimates.**

The reported clearance for 1'-Hydroxymidazolam (264 L/h) significantly exceeds that of midazolam (43.9 L/h), which is physiologically implausible for this metabolic pair. Additionally, bioavailability and lag time were not extracted, forcing the use of library placeholders instead of values derived from the study. The model structure also diverged from the required three-compartment design, utilizing a single-compartment enteral topology instead. Extracted — midazolam: CL 43.9 L/h, V1 56.7 L, kabs 6.4 h−1; 1'-Hydroxymidazolam: CL 264 L/h, V 300 L; Voriconazole: n_transit -23.8.
