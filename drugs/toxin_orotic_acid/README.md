# Orotic acid

- **generic name:** Orotic acid
- **T3DB:** [T3D4292](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4292) · **DrugBank:** [DB02262](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB02262) · **PubChem:** [CID 967](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/967)
- **groups:** investigational

## About

Orotic acid is a minor dietary constituent. Indeed, until it was realized that it could be synthesized by humans, orotic acid was known as vitamin B-13. The richest dietary sources are cow's milk and other dairy products as well as root vegetables such as carrots and beets. …

- **Category:** Animal Toxin, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Sources and uses:** This is an endogenously produced metabolite found in the human body. It is used in metabolic reactions, catabolic reactions or waste generation.
- **Health effects:** Chronically high levels of orotic acid are associated with at least 4 inborn errors of metabolism including: Argininemia, Citrullinemia Type I, Purine nucleoside phosphorylase deficiency and Orotic Aciduria.
