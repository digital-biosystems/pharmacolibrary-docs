# Hydromorphone

- **generic name:** Hydromorphone
- **T3DB:** [T3D2752](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2752) · **DrugBank:** [DB00327](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00327) · **PubChem:** [CID 5284570](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/5284570)
- **groups:** approved, illicit, investigational

## About

Hydromorphone is only found in individuals that have used or taken this drug. It is an opioid analgesic derived from morphine and used mainly as an analgesic. It has a shorter duration of action and is more potent than morphine. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** parental (intravenous, intramuscular), oral, enteral(rectal).
Better absorbed orally than morphine.
- **Sources and uses:** For the relief of moderate to severe pain such as that due to surgery, cancer, trauma/injury, or burns.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Hydromorphone is a narcotic analgesic; its principal therapeutic effect is relief of pain. Hydromorphone interacts predominantly with the opioid mu-receptors. …
- **Health effects:** Medical problems can include congested lungs, liver disease, tetanus, infection of the heart valves, skin abscesses, anemia and pneumonia. Death can occur from overdose.
- **Treatment:** In the treatment of overdosage, primary attention should be given to the reestablishment of adequate respiratory exchange through provision of a patent airway and institution of assisted or controlled ventilation. …
