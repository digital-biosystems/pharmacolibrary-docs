## About

Prednisolone is a glucocorticoid steroid used to treat inflammatory and autoimmune conditions such as ulcerative colitis, myasthenia gravis, nephrotic syndrome, and various eye, skin, and blood disorders. It is widely used in human medicine, appears on the WHO essential medicines list, and is also approved for veterinary use.
