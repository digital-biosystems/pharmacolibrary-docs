# Trimethylaluminium

- **generic name:** Trimethylaluminium
- **T3DB:** [T3D1536](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D1536) · **PubChem:** [CID 16682925](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/16682925)

## About

Trimethylaluminium (TMA) is an organoaluminium compound. It is used in the production of methylaluminoxane and Tebbe's reagent, as well as in semiconductor fabrication to grow thin film, high-k dielectrics. Aluminum is the most abundant metal in the earth's crust and is always found combined with other elements such as oxygen, silicon, and fluorine.

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation
- **Sources and uses:** Trimethylaluminium is used in the production of methylaluminoxane and Tebbe's reagent, as well as in semiconductor fabrication to grow thin film, high-k dielectrics.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** The main target organs of aluminum are the central nervous system and bone. Aluminum binds with dietary phosphorus and impairs gastrointestinal absorption of phosphorus. …
- **Health effects:** Aluminum targets the nervous system and causes decreased nervous system performance and is associated with altered function of the blood-brain barrier. The accumulation of aluminum in the body may cause bone or brain diseases. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** Not listed by IARC. …
- **Minimal risk levels (ATSDR):** Intermediate Oral: 1.0 mg/kg/day 
Chronic Oral: 1.0 mg/kg/day
