# Mangafodipir

- **generic name:** Mangafodipir
- **T3DB:** [T3D1137](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D1137) · **PubChem:** [CID 160036](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/160036)

## About

Mangafodipir is a contrast agent delivered intravenously to enhance contrast in magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) of the liver. It has two parts, paramagnetic manganese (II) ions and the chelating agent fodipir (dipyridoxyl diphosphate, DPDP). Normal liver tissue absorbs the manganese more than abnormal or cancerous tissue. …

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation
- **Sources and uses:** Mangafodipir is a contrast agent delivered intravenously to enhance contrast in magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) of the liver.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Manganese is a cellular toxicant that can impair transport systems, enzyme activities, and receptor functions. It primarily targets the central nervous system, particularily the globus pallidus of the basal ganglia. …
- **Health effects:** Manganese mainly affects the nervous system and may cause behavioral changes and other nervous system effects, which include movements that may become slow and clumsy. This combination of symptoms when sufficiently severe is referred to as “manganism”.
- **Minimal risk levels (ATSDR):** Chronic Inhalation: 0.0003 mg/m3
