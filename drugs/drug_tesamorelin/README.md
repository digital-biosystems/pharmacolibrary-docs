## About

Tesamorelin is a pharmaceutical drug that acts as a growth hormone-releasing agent, and it has been studied for investigational uses in addition to an approved indication. It is an approved medicine, but a marketing application for it in the European Union was withdrawn, so its use is limited to other regions.
