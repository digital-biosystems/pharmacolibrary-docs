## About

Ganciclovir is a nucleoside analogue antiviral used to treat cytomegalovirus infections and herpes simplex virus keratitis. It is given intravenously, orally, or as an ophthalmic gel. Oral absorption is poor, so the injection form is often preferred for severe infections. The drug is authorised in the European Union, though some specific products have been withdrawn.
