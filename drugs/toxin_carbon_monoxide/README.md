## About

Carbon monoxide, with the chemical formula CO, is a colorless, odorless, and tasteless gas. It is the product of the incomplete combustion of carbon-containing compounds, notably in internal-combustion engines. It consists of one carbon atom covalently bonded to one oxygen atom. …

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** inhalation
- **Sources and uses:** Carbon monoxide is a major atmospheric pollutant in urban areas, chiefly from exhaust of internal combustion engines, but also from improper burning of various other fuels.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Carbon monoxide possesses a higher affinity than oxygen for hemoglobin, leading to the formation of carboxyhemoglobin, this provoking anoxemia. Carbon monoxide also binds to myoglobin, impairing its ability to utilize oxygen. …
- **Health effects:** Chronic exposure to low levels of carbon monoxide may cause persistent headaches, lightheadedness, depression, confusion, memory loss, and nausea and vomiting.
- **Lethal dose:** 5000 ppm over 5 minutes for an adult human.
- **Treatment:** Carbon monoxide poisoning is first treated by immediate removal from the source of exposure. …
- **Detected in:** blood
