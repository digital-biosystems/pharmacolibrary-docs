# Nickel acetate

- **generic name:** Nickel acetate
- **T3DB:** [T3D0684](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D0684) · **PubChem:** [CID 9756](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/9756)

## About

Chemical compound.

<p><small>Source: <a href="https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q27265453">Wikidata Q27265453</a> · <a href="https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/">CC0</a></small></p>

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** inhalation, oral, dermal
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Nickel is known to substitute for other essential elements in certain enzmes, such as calcineurin. It is genotoxic, and some nickel compounds have been shown to promote cell proliferation. …
- **Health effects:** The most common harmful health effect of nickel in humans is an allergic reaction. This usually manifests as a skin rash, although some people experience asthma attacks. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 1, carcinogenic to humans.
- **Minimal risk levels (ATSDR):** Chronic Inhalation: 0.0001 mg/m3; Intermediate Oral: 0.01 mg/kg/day
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 350 mg/kg (Oral, Rat)
