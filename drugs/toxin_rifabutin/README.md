# Rifabutin

- **generic name:** Rifabutin
- **T3DB:** [T3D2838](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2838) · **DrugBank:** [DB00615](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00615) · **PubChem:** [CID 6323490](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/6323490)
- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

Rifabutin is only found in individuals that have used or taken this drug. It is a broad-spectrum antibiotic that is being used as prophylaxis against disseminated Mycobacterium avium complex infection in HIV-positive patients. [PubChem]Rifabutin acts via the inhibition of DNA-dependent RNA polymerase in gram-positive and some gram-negative bacteria, leading to a suppression of RNA synthesis and cell death.

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** rifabutin is readily absorbed from the gastrointestinal tract, with an absolute bioavailability averaging 20%.
- **Sources and uses:** For the prevention of disseminated <i>Mycobacterium avium</i> complex (MAC) disease in patients with advanced HIV infection. Used in the treatment of tuberculosis. …
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Rifabutin acts via the inhibition of DNA-dependent RNA polymerase in gram-positive and some gram-negative bacteria, leading to a suppression of RNA synthesis and cell death.
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 4.8 g/kg (male mouse)
