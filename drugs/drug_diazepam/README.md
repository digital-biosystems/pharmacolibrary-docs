## About

Diazepam is a benzodiazepine medication used to treat conditions such as anxiety, seizures, and alcohol withdrawal. It is typically taken orally, but can also be given as an intramuscular or intravenous injection, rectal suppository, or nasal spray. Diazepam is widely used around the world and is listed as a WHO essential medicine.
