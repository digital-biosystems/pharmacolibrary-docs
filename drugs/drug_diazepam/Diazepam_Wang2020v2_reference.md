### Reviewer guidance

**The record was rejected because clearance for nordazepam is reported in mL/kg instead of mL/kg/h, and AUC is in ng·mL/h instead of ng·h/mL.**

The reported unit for nordazepam clearance, mL/kg, describes a volume per mass rather than the required volume per mass per time for a rate process. Additionally, the area under the curve for nordazepam is reported in ng·mL/h, which is dimensionally inconsistent with the standard concentration-time integral unit of ng·h/mL. These dimensional conflicts in the structural parameters for the metabolite nordazepam prevented the model from being validated. Extracted — nordazepam: V 12.1 mL/kg, CL 110 mL/kg, AUC 82.5 ng·mL/h, tmax 94 h, Cmax 1.1 ng/mL, kabs 4.1 h⁻¹; diazepam: k12 0.7 h⁻¹.
