# Hydroxychloroquine

- **generic name:** Hydroxychloroquine
- **T3DB:** [T3D3512](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3512) · **DrugBank:** [DB01611](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB01611) · **PubChem:** [CID 3652](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/3652)
- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

Hydroxychloroquine is only found in individuals that have used or taken this drug. It is a chemotherapeutic agent that acts against erythrocytic forms of malarial parasites. Although the exact mechanism of action is unknown, it may be based on ability of hydroxychloroquine to bind to and alter DNA. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** very rapidly and completely absorbed following oral administration.
- **Sources and uses:** For the suppressive treatment and treatment of acute attacks of malaria due to <i>Plasmodium vivax</i>, <i>P. malariae</i>, <i>P. ovale</i>, and susceptible strains of <i>P. falciparum</i>. It is also indicated for the treatment of discoid and systemic lupus erythematosus, and rheumatoid arthritis.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Although the exact mechanism of action is unknown, it may be based on ability of hydroxychloroquine to bind to and alter DNA. Hydroxychloroquine has also has been found to be taken up into the acidic food vacuoles of the parasite in the erythrocyte. …
