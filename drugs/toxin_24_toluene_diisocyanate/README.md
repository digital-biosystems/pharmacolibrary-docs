# 2,4-Toluene diisocyanate

- **generic name:** 2,4-Toluene diisocyanate
- **T3DB:** [T3D1667](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D1667) · **PubChem:** [CID 11443](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/11443)

## About

2,4-Toluene diisocyanate is a chemical compound of cyanide.

- **Category:** Household Toxin, Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** 2,4-Toluene diisocyanate is a cholinesterase or acetylcholinesterase (AChE) inhibitor. A cholinesterase inhibitor (or 'anticholinesterase') suppresses the action of acetylcholinesterase. …
- **Health effects:** Acute exposure to cholinesterase inhibitors can cause a cholinergic crisis characterized by severe nausea/vomiting, salivation, sweating, bradycardia, hypotension, collapse, and convulsions. Increasing muscle weakness is a possibility and may result in death if respiratory muscles are involved. …
- **Lethal dose:** 200 to 300 milligrams for an adult human (cyanide salts).
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 2B, possibly carcinogenic to humans.
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 5800 mg/kg (Oral, Rat); LC50: 10 ppm over 4 hours (Inhalation, Mouse)
- **Treatment:** If the compound has been ingested, rapid gastric lavage should be performed using 5% sodium bicarbonate. …
