### Reviewer guidance

**The model failed to reproduce the reported 1.1-hour terminal half-life, simulating 12.7 hours, and used library defaults for bioavailability and lag time.**

The terminal half-life for duloxetine is reported as 1.1 hours, but the simulation yielded 12.7 hours, a discrepancy far exceeding tolerance. Additionally, the model structure was simplified to a single compartment, missing the required parent-metabolite topology. Key parameters F and Tlag were left at library defaults rather than being estimated from the data. Extracted — duloxetine: CL 55.9 L/h, V 1.02e+03 L, t1/2z 1.1 h, kabs 1.46 h-1.
