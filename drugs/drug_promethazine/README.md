## About

Promethazine is a first-generation antihistamine used to manage pain, vomiting, motion sickness, and postoperative issues. It is usually swallowed as a tablet or syrup, though it can also be given by injection into a muscle or placed rectally. This medication is a generic product available under various brand names around the world.
