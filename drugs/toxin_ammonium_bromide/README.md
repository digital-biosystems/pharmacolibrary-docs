# Ammonium bromide

- **generic name:** Ammonium bromide
- **T3DB:** [T3D1721](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D1721) · **PubChem:** [CID 25514](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/25514)

## About

Ammonium bromide is a chemical compound of bromine. It is used for photography in films, plates and papers, for fireproofing wood, in lithography and process engraving, in corrosion inhibitors, and in pharmaceutical preparations. Bromine is a halogen element with the symbol Br and atomic number 35. …

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Ammonium bromide is used for photography in films, plates and papers, for fireproofing wood, in lithography and process engraving, in corrosion inhibitors, and in pharmaceutical preparations.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Bromine is a powerful oxidizing agent and is able to release oxygen free radicals from the water in mucous membranes. These free radicals are also potent oxidizers and produce tissue damage. …
- **Health effects:** Bromine vapour causes irritation and direct damage to the mucous membranes. Elemental bromine also burns the skin. …
- **Treatment:** EYES: irrigate opened eyes for several minutes under running water. …
- **Detected in:** blood, cerebrospinal fluid, urine
