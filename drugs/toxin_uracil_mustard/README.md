# Uracil mustard

- **generic name:** Uracil mustard
- **T3DB:** [T3D4718](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4718) · **DrugBank:** [DB00791](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00791) · **PubChem:** [CID 6194](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/6194)
- **groups:** approved

## About

Uracil mustard is only found in individuals that have used or taken this drug. It is a nitrogen mustard derivative of uracil. It is a alkylating antineoplastic agent that is used in lymphatic malignancies, and causes mainly gastrointestinal and bone marrow damage. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Sources and uses:** Used for its antineoplastic properties.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** After activation, it binds preferentially to the guanine and cytosine moieties of DNA, leading to cross-linking of DNA, thus inhibiting DNA synthesis and function.
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 2B, possibly carcinogenic to humans.
