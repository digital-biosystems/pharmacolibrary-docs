# Mercury selenide

- **generic name:** Mercury selenide
- **T3DB:** [T3D0345](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D0345) · **PubChem:** [CID 9838575](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/9838575)

## About

Mercury selenide (HgSe; sometimes mercury(II) selenide) is a chemical compound of mercury and selenium. It is a grey-black crystalline solid semi-metal with a sphalerite structure. The lattice constant is 0.608 nm.

<p><small>Source: <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercury_selenide?oldid=1380120601">Wikipedia</a> · <a href="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/">CC BY-SA 4.0</a></small></p>

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Mercury selenide is used as an ohmic contact to wide-gap II-VI semconductors. It is also a byproduct of steel processing.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** High-affinity binding of the divalent mercuric ion to thiol or sulfhydryl groups of proteins is believed to be the major mechanism for the activity of mercury. Through alterations in intracellular thiol status, mercury can promote oxidative stress, lipid peroxidation, mitochondrial dysfunction, and changes in heme metabolism. …
- **Health effects:** Mercury mainly affects the nervous system. Exposure to high levels of metallic, inorganic, or organic mercury can permanently damage the brain, kidneys, and developing fetus. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 3, not classifiable as to its carcinogenicity to humans.
- **Minimal risk levels (ATSDR):** Chronic Inhalation: 0.0002 mg/m3 (Mercury); Chronic Oral: 0.005 mg/kg/day (Selenium)
