### Reviewer guidance

**The Desloratadine metabolite compartment is orphaned with no formation path from Loratadine, and the model relies on abstract-only summary statistics rather than fitted parameters.**

The Desloratadine compartment is defined as a metabolite formed from Loratadine but possesses zero central compartments (n_cmt: 0) and an unknown link parameter, rendering it unreachable from the dose input. This structural flaw constitutes an orphaned metabolite, preventing proper mass balance simulation. Furthermore, the entire model was constructed solely from the paper's abstract, meaning reported summary statistics like the 5.62 h half-life and 61.46 L/h/kg clearance were used as static values instead of being derived from a fitted population model. Extracted — loratadine: t1/2z 5.62 h, CL/F 61.5 L/h/kg, CL 57.3 L/h/kg; Desloratadine: CL/F 16 L/h/kg, CL 14.2 L/h/kg.
