## About

Belladonna total alkaloids, derived from the deadly nightshade plant, are used to treat functional gastrointestinal disorders such as spasms and cramps. They are classified in the ATC system as plain belladonna alkaloids for digestive use, but no EU marketing authorisation is listed, so their availability today appears limited.
