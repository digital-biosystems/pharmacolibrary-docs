# Alpha-hemolysin

- **generic name:** Alpha-hemolysin
- **T3DB:** [T3D2610](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2610) · **PubChem:** none

## About

Alpha-hemolysin is the major cytotoxic agent released by bacterium Staphylococcus aureus.

- **Category:** Protein, Bacterial Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** ingestion, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Alpha-hemolysin is the major cytotoxic agent released by bacterium Staphylococcus aureus.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Alpha-hemolysin consists mainly of beta-sheets forms heptameric units on the cellular membrane, producing a complete beta-barrel pore. This pore allows the exchange of monovalent ions, resulting in DNA fragmentation and eventually apoptosis.
- **Health effects:** Alpha-toxin is a potent cytotoxin produced by Staphylococcus aureus. Staphylococcus aureus is the most common cause of staph infections. …
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 40-60 ng/kg (Intravenous, Mouse)
