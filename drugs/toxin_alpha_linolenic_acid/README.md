## About

Alpha-linolenic acid (ALA) is a polyunsaturated omega-3 fatty acid. It is a component of many common vegetable oils and is important to human nutrition.

- **Category:** Drug, Household Toxin, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin, Animal Toxin
- **Sources and uses:** For nutritional supplementation and for treating dietary shortage or imbalance.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Alpha Linolenic Acid or ALA is considered an essential fatty acid because it is required for human health, but cannot be synthesized by humans. It is in fact a plant-derived fatty acid. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** Not listed by IARC.
