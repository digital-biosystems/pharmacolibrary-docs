- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

Sorafenib is an anticancer protein kinase inhibitor used to treat liver cancer (hepatocellular carcinoma) and kidney cancer (renal cell carcinoma), and it has also been studied for other cancers such as thyroid cancer and acute myeloid leukemia. It is authorised in the European Union and widely used in cancer care.
