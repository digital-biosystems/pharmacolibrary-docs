## About

Zolpidem is a nonbenzodiazepine hypnotic used for the short-term treatment of insomnia and sleep disorders. It is administered orally via standard, extended-release, or sublingual tablets, which are well-absorbed by the body. The drug is widely used and is authorized for medical use in the United States, where it is a commonly prescribed medication.
