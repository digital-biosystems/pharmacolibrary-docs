# Monomethyl phthalate

- **generic name:** Monomethyl phthalate
- **T3DB:** [T3D3886](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3886) · **PubChem:** [CID 20392](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/20392)

## About

Monomethyl phthalate is a metabolite, or breakdown product, of dimethyl phthalate, used in insect repellant, plastic, and as a rocket propellant.

- **Category:** Household Toxin, Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Food Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Sources and uses:** Monomethyl phthalate is a metabolite, or breakdown product, of dimethyl phthalate, used in insect repellant, plastic, and as a rocket propellant.
- **Detected in:** saliva
