## About

Allantoin is a diureide of glyoxylic acid with the chemical formula C4H6N4O3. It is also called 5-ureidohydantoin, glyoxyldiureide, and 5-ureidohydantoin. It is a product of oxidation of uric acid. …

- **Category:** Household Toxin, Animal Toxin, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin
