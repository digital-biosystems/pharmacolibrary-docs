## About

Aloxiprin, a polycondensate of aspirin and aluminium oxide, is a salicylic acid derivative classified as a platelet aggregation inhibitor and analgesic, suggesting use against pain and thrombosis. It is not an approved medicine in major databases, being listed only as experimental, so its current availability is unclear.
