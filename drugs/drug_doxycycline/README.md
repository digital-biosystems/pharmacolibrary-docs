## About

Doxycycline is a tetracycline antibiotic used to treat many bacterial infections, including pneumonia, acne, Lyme disease, and chlamydia, and to prevent malaria. It is widely used in human medicine and is also approved for veterinary use, and it appears on the WHO list of essential medicines.
