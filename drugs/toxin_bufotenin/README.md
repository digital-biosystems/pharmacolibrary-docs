# Bufotenin

- **generic name:** Bufotenin
- **T3DB:** [T3D2534](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2534) · **DrugBank:** [DB01445](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB01445) · **PubChem:** [CID 10257](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/10257)
- **groups:** experimental, illicit

## About

Bufotenin (5-OH-DMT), is a tryptamine related to the neurotransmitter serotonin. It is an alkaloid found in the skin of some species of toads; in mushrooms, higher plants, and mammals. Bufotenin is a chemical constituent in the venom and eggs of several species of toads belonging to the Bufo genus, but most notably in the Colorado River toad (Bufo alvarius) as it is the only toad species in which bufotenin is present in large enough quantities for a psychoactive effect. …

- **Category:** Animal Toxin, Fungal Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** ingestion
- **Sources and uses:** Bufotenin is a toxin found in some toads (genus Bufo) and mushrooms (genus Amanita).
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Bufotenin is a neurotoxin similar to the mushroom toxin psilocin. Thus it likely acts as a partial agonist at the 5-HT2A serotonin receptor in the brain where it mimics the effects of serotonin (5-HT). …
- **Health effects:** Bufotenin is neurotoxic and can cause hallucinations.
