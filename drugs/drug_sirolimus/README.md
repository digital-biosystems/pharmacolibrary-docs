## About

Sirolimus is an mTOR kinase inhibitor used to prevent organ transplant rejection and treat conditions like lymphangioleiomyomatosis and renal cell carcinoma. It is administered orally in forms such as tablets, solutions, and gel for topical use on facial angiofibromas. Blood levels are routinely measured to guide dosing. It is widely used, with products authorised in the European Union for transplantation and dermatological indications.
