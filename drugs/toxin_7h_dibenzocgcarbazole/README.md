# 7H-Dibenzo[c,g]carbazole

- **generic name:** 7H-Dibenzo[c,g]carbazole
- **T3DB:** [T3D4627](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4627) · **PubChem:** [CID 9134](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/9134)

## About

7H-Dibenzo[c,g]carbazole (DBC) is is a needle-like solid, a potent multispecies, multisite carcinogen present in the environment. The metabolic activation pathways of DBC are not completely known. It may irritate the eyes, nose, and throat.

- **Category:** Cigarette Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Sources and uses:** This is a toxic chemical found in cigarettes or generated by tobacco combustion.
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 2B, possibly carcinogenic to humans.
