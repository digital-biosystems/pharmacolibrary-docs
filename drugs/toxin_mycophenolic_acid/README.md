# Mycophenolic acid

- **generic name:** Mycophenolic acid
- **T3DB:** [T3D2960](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2960) · **DrugBank:** [DB01024](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB01024) · **PubChem:** [CID 446541](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/446541)
- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

Mycophenolic acid is an an immunosuppresant drug and potent anti-proliferative, and can be used in place of the older anti-proliferative azathioprine. It is usually used as part of triple therapy including a calcineurin inhibitor (ciclosporin or tacrolimus) and prednisolone. It is also useful in research for the selection of animal cells that express the E. coli gene coding for XGPRT (xanthine guanine phosphoribosyltransferase).

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** bioavailability following oral administration of Myfortic delayed-release tablet ranges from 70-95%
- **Sources and uses:** For the prophylaxis of organ rejection in patients receiving allogeneic renal transplants, administered in combination with cyclosporine and corticosteroids.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Mycophenolic acid is a potent, selective, uncompetitive, and reversible inhibitor of inosine monophosphate dehydrogenase (IMPDH), and therefore inhibits the de novo pathway of guanosine nucleotide synthesis without incorporation into DNA. Because T- and B-lymphocytes are critically dependent for their proliferation on de novo synthesis of purines, whereas other cell types can utilize salvage pathways, mycophenolic acid has potent cytostatic effects on lymphocytes. …
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 352 mg/kg (Oral, Rat) 
LD50: 1000 mg/kg (Oral, Mouse)
LD50: >6000 mg/kg (Oral, Rabbit)
