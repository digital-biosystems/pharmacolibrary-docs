# Sodium hypochlorite

- **generic name:** Sodium hypochlorite
- **T3DB:** [T3D4970](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4970) · **PubChem:** [CID 23665760](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/23665760)
- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

Sodium Hypochlorite is the sodium salt of hypochlorous acid. When dissolved in water it is commonly known as bleach or liquid bleach, and is frequently used as a disinfectant or a bleaching agent. Household bleach is, typically a solution containing 3-8% sodium hypochlorite and 0.01-0.05% sodium hydroxide. …

- **Category:** Synthetic Toxin, Industrial/Workplace Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Sodium hypochlorite is frequently used as a disinfectant or a bleaching agent.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Sodium hypochlorite is a strong oxidizer. Oxidation reactions are corrosive, solutions burn skin and cause eye damage, especially when used in concentrated forms. …
- **Health effects:** Chlorine Bleach is a strong corrosive material. Sodium hypochlorite solutions frequently produce small amounts of chlorine gas. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 3, not classifiable as to its carcinogenicity to humans.
- **Treatment:** EYES: irrigate opened eyes for several minutes under running water. …
