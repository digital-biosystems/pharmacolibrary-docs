## What this record describes

**As extracted:** Fexuprazan (concentrations from this paper's PK model) drives gastric pH: indirect response — drug inhibits the production of gastric pH.

**Model:** A model was generated (see the **Models** tab); it has no in-browser simulator.

> Fexuprazan plasma concentrations inhibit the production of gastric pH (modeled as pOH) via an indirect response model with a Hill coefficient of 2.14, characterized by an Imax of 0.401, an IC50 of 1.16 ng/mL, and a kin of 0.139 pOH/min. A simple direct effect sigmoid Emax model was also applied using unbound stomach concentrations, though specific EC50 and Emax values for this alternative model are not provided in the excerpts.
>
