# Manganese carbonate

- **generic name:** Manganese carbonate
- **T3DB:** [T3D1145](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D1145) · **PubChem:** [CID 24189101](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/24189101)

## About

Manganese carbonate is a chemical compound of manganese that occurs naturally as the mineral rhodochrosite. It is widely used as an additive to plant fertilizers to cure manganese deficient crops, in health foods, in ceramics as a glaze colorant and flux, and in concrete stains. Manganese is a naturally occurring metal with the symbol Mn and the atomic number 25. …

- **Category:** Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation
- **Sources and uses:** Manganese carbonate is widely used as an additive to plant fertilizers to cure manganese deficient crops, in health foods, in ceramics as a glaze colorant and flux, and in concrete stains.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Manganese is a cellular toxicant that can impair transport systems, enzyme activities, and receptor functions. It primarily targets the central nervous system, particularily the globus pallidus of the basal ganglia. …
- **Health effects:** Manganese mainly affects the nervous system and may cause behavioral changes and other nervous system effects, which include movements that may become slow and clumsy. This combination of symptoms when sufficiently severe is referred to as “manganism”.
- **Minimal risk levels (ATSDR):** Chronic Inhalation: 0.0003 mg/m3
- **Detected in:** blood, cerebrospinal fluid, saliva, urine
