## About

Parathyroid gland extract is a parathyroid hormone preparation used to help regulate calcium levels in the body, mainly in conditions of low blood calcium such as hypoparathyroidism. It is classified under parathyroid hormones and analogues for calcium homeostasis and is used only rarely, having largely been replaced by newer parathyroid hormone medicines.
