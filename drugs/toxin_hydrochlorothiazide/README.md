# Hydrochlorothiazide

- **generic name:** Hydrochlorothiazide
- **T3DB:** [T3D4781](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4781) · **DrugBank:** [DB00999](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00999) · **PubChem:** [CID 3639](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/3639)
- **groups:** approved, investigational, vet_approved

## About

A thiazide diuretic often considered the prototypical member of this class. It reduces the reabsorption of electrolytes from the renal tubules. This results in increased excretion of water and electrolytes, including sodium, potassium, chloride, and magnesium. …

- **Category:** Drug, Food Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** 50-60%
- **Sources and uses:** For the treatment of high blood pressure and management of edema.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Hydrochlorothiazide, a thiazide diuretic, inhibits water reabsorption in the nephron by inhibiting the sodium-chloride symporter (SLC12A3) in the distal convoluted tubule, which is responsible for 5% of total sodium reabsorption. Normally, the sodium-chloride symporter transports sodium and chloride from the lumen into the epithelial cell lining the distal convoluted tubule. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 2B, possibly carcinogenic to humans.
- **Acute toxicity:** The oral LD50 of hydrochlorothiazide is greater than 10 g/kg in the mouse and rat.
- **Detected in:** blood
