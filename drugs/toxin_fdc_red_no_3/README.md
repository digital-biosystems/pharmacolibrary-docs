# Fd&c red no. 3

- **generic name:** Fd&c red no. 3
- **T3DB:** [T3D4914](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4914) · **PubChem:** [CID 27872](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/27872)

## About

Fd&c red no. 3 is used as a food additive [EAFUS] ("EAFUS: Everything Added to Food in the United States. [http://www.eafus.com/]").<br /> Fd&c red no. 3 belongs to the family of Xanthenes. These are polycyclic aromatic compounds containing a xanthene moiety, which consists of two benzene ring joined to each other by a pyran ring.

- **Category:** Household Toxin, Food Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
