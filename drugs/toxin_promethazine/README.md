# Promethazine

- **generic name:** Promethazine
- **T3DB:** [T3D4560](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4560) · **DrugBank:** [DB01069](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB01069) · **PubChem:** [CID 4927](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/4927)
- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

A phenothiazine derivative with histamine H1-blocking, antimuscarinic, and sedative properties. It is used as an antiallergic, in pruritus, for motion sickness and sedation, and also in animals. [PubChem]

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** on average, 88% of a promethazine dose is absorbed after oral administration, however, the absolute bioavailability is only 25% because of first-pass clearance.
- **Sources and uses:** For the treatment of allergic disorders, and nausea/vomiting.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Like other H1-antagonists, promethazine competes with free histamine for binding at H1-receptor sites in the GI tract, uterus, large blood vessels, and bronchial muscle. The relief of nausea appears to be related to central anticholinergic actions and may implicate activity on the medullary chemoreceptor trigger zone.
- **Acute toxicity:** IV, Mouse: LD<sub>50</sub>=55mg/kg
