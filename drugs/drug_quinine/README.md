## About

Quinine is an antimalarial drug used to treat malaria, including falciparum malaria, and has also been used for babesiosis and muscle cramps. It remains an approved medicine, is listed among WHO essential medicines, and is used worldwide mainly for malaria, though it carries a boxed warning.
