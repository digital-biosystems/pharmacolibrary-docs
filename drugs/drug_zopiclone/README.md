## About

Zopiclone is a sedative drug from the benzodiazepine-related group used to treat insomnia. It is given by mouth as tablets and is well absorbed from the gut. It is widely prescribed for short-term sleep issues but is not sold commercially in the United States, where a related compound is used instead.
