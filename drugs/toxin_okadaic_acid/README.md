## About

Okadaic acid is found in mollusks. Okadaic acid is found in the marine sponges Halichondria okadai and Halichondria melanodocia and shellfish. It is a metabolite of Prorocentrum lima. …

- **Category:** Animal Toxin, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Sources and uses:** Okadaic acid is found in mollusks. Okadaic acid is found in the marine sponges Halichondria okadai and Halichondria melanodocia and shellfish. …
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Okadaic acid (OA) dramatically increases both nerve growth factor (NGF) mRNA content (50-fold) and NGF secretion (100-fold) in astrocytes. Okadaic acid also activated NGF gene transcription, which was preceded by an induction of c-fos and c-jun gene transcription. …
