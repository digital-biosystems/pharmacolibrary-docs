# Enterolactone

- **generic name:** Enterolactone
- **T3DB:** [T3D3967](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3967) · **PubChem:** [CID 123917](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/123917)

## About

Enterolactone is a mammalian lignan that have a similar biphenolic structure to lignans from plants. Lignans are compounds with estrogenic properties and are probably the most important source of phytoestrogens in western diets. Mammalian lignans are formed from precursors that are contained mainly in vegetables, whole grain products and berries, via action of intestinal microflora. …

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin, Animal Toxin
- **Sources and uses:** Enterolactone is produced in the colon by the action of bacteria on secoisolariciresinol, matairesinol and its glycosides.
