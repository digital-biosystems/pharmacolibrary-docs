# m-Cresol

- **generic name:** m-Cresol
- **T3DB:** [T3D1842](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D1842) · **DrugBank:** [DB01776](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB01776) · **PubChem:** [CID 342](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/342)
- **groups:** experimental

## About

m-Cresol is an isomer of p-cresol and o-cresol. Cresols are organic compounds which are methylphenols. They are a widely occurring natural and manufactured group of aromatic organic compounds which are categorized as phenols (sometimes called phenolics). …

- **Category:** Household Toxin, Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Cresols are used to as solvents, disinfectants and deodorizers, as well as to make other chemicals. They may be formed normally in the body from other compounds. …
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** m-Cresol is a cholinesterase or acetylcholinesterase (AChE) inhibitor. A cholinesterase inhibitor (or 'anticholinesterase') suppresses the action of acetylcholinesterase. …
- **Health effects:** Acute exposure to cholinesterase inhibitors can cause a cholinergic crisis characterized by severe nausea/vomiting, salivation, sweating, bradycardia, hypotension, collapse, and convulsions. Increasing muscle weakness is a possibility and may result in death if respiratory muscles are involved. …
- **Minimal risk levels (ATSDR):** Intermediate Oral: 0.1 mg/kg/day 
Chronic Oral: 0.1 mg/kg/day
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 242 mg/kg (Oral, Rat)
LD50: 168 mg/kg (Intraperitoneal, Mouse)
LD50: 2050 mg/kg (Dermal, Rabbit)
- **Treatment:** If the compound has been ingested, rapid gastric lavage should be performed using 5% sodium bicarbonate. …
- **Detected in:** blood, urine
