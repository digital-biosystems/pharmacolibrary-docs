### Reviewer guidance

**Fentanyl clearance and transfer constant units are dimensionally invalid, and the peripheral compartment is unreachable in the one-compartment model.**

Clearance and the central-to-peripheral transfer constant are recorded with values like 13.9-24.3 and 0.455-0.908 rather than valid units, causing dimension mismatches. Although peripheral volumes are defined, the model structure is one-compartment, leaving the peripheral compartment unreachable from the dose. The second reader notes the paper likely covers multiple opioids, complicating the attribution of these specific parameters. Extracted — fentanyl: t1/2α 1 min, t1/2β 17 min, t1/2γ 151 min, V2 0.46 L/kg, V3 1.74 L/kg, CL 76.1 13.9-24.3, k13 0.088 0.455-0.908, MRT 126 min, … (+3).

A second, independent reading of the paper (`gpt-oss-120b`) disagrees on which compound was dosed: this record has fentanyl, the second reading fentanyl, alfentanil, sufentanil. That field shapes the model, so the record is marked disputed.
