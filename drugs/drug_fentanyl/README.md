## About

Fentanyl is a potent synthetic opioid used to treat severe pain, including cancer and postoperative pain. It is given intravenously during anesthesia or intubation, and can also be applied through patches, lozenges, or tablets for pain management. It is widely used in healthcare globally and is authorized in the European Union.
