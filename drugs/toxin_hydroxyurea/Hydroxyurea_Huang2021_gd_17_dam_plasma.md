### Reviewer guidance

**The Cmax/dose and AUC/dose parameters for hydroxyurea have an invalid 'e' unit, causing dimensional mismatch checks to fail and preventing SI conversion.**

The Cmax/dose and AUC/dose parameters are recorded with the unit 'e' (esiemens), which does not match the expected mass per volume or mass per time dimensions for these pharmacokinetic metrics. This invalid unit specification prevented the system from converting the values of 16 and 27 into SI units. Consequently, the record was rejected due to unresolved dimensional conflicts in the dose-normalized exposure metrics.
