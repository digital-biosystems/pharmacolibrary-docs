# Thioridazine

- **generic name:** Thioridazine
- **T3DB:** [T3D2858](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2858) · **DrugBank:** [DB00679](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00679) · **PubChem:** [CID 5452](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/5452)
- **groups:** approved, withdrawn

## About

A phenothiazine antipsychotic used in the management of psychoses, including schizophrenia, and in the control of severely disturbed or agitated behavior. It has little antiemetic activity. Thioridazine has a higher incidence of antimuscarinic effects, but a lower incidence of extrapyramidal symptoms, than chlorpromazine. (From Martindale, The Extra Pharmacopoeia, 30th ed, p618)

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral
- **Sources and uses:** For the treatment of schizophrenia and generalized anxiety disorder.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Thioridazine blocks postsynaptic mesolimbic dopaminergic D1 and D2 receptors in the brain; blocks alpha-adrenergic effect, depresses the release of hypothalamic and hypophyseal hormones and is believed to depress the reticular activating system thus affecting basal metabolism, body temperature, wakefulness, vasomotor tone, and emesis.
- **Acute toxicity:** LD<sub>50</sub>=956-1034 mg/kg (Orally in rats).
