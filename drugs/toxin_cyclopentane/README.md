# Cyclopentane

- **generic name:** Cyclopentane
- **T3DB:** [T3D3465](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3465) · **PubChem:** [CID 9253](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/9253)

## About

Cyclopentane is a cyclic hydrocarbon. It is a component of gasoline and is used in the manufacture of synthetic resins and rubber adhesives, and also as a blowing agent in the manufacture of polyurethane insulating foam.

- **Category:** Household Toxin, Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Cyclopentane is a component of gasoline and is used in the manufacture of synthetic resins and rubber adhesives, and also as a blowing agent in the manufacture of polyurethane insulating foam.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Petroleum distillates are central nervous system depressants and cause pulmonary damage.
- **Health effects:** Petroleum distillates are aspiration hazards and may cause pulmonary damage, central nervous system depression, and cardiac effects such as cardiac arrhythmias. They may also affect the blood, immune system, liver, and kidney.
- **Carcinogenicity:** Cyclopentane is found in gasoline, which is possibly carcinogenic to humans (Group 2B).
- **Treatment:** Treatment is mainly symptomatic and supportive. …
