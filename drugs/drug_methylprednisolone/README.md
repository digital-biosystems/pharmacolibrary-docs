## About

Methylprednisolone is a glucocorticoid used to treat inflammatory and autoimmune conditions such as arthritis, multiple sclerosis, lupus nephritis, and graft-versus-host disease. It is widely used in human medicine and is also approved for veterinary use, appearing on the WHO essential medicines list.
