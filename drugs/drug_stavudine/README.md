## About

Stavudine is a nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitor used to treat HIV infection and prevent transmission after potential exposure. The medication is administered orally as capsules or tablets and is well absorbed. It is no longer a first-line treatment and has a boxed warning due to serious side effects, leading to its withdrawal from the European Union.
