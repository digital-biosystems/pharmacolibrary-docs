## About

Decamethrin (deltamethrin) is a pyrethroid insecticide used to kill ectoparasites such as lice, mites, and other insects on humans and animals. It is classified as an experimental compound in DrugBank, meaning it has not received regulatory approval as a medicine and remains investigational rather than an established therapeutic drug.
