# Triethanolamine

- **generic name:** Triethanolamine
- **T3DB:** [T3D4835](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4835) · **PubChem:** [CID 7618](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/7618)
- **groups:** approved

## About

Triethanolamine is used as a food additive [EAFUS] ("EAFUS: Everything Added to Food in the United States. [http://www.eafus.com/]"). Triethanolamine belongs to the family of Alkylamines. These are organic compounds containing an alkylamine group.

- **Category:** Household Toxin, Food Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 3, not classifiable as to its carcinogenicity to humans.
