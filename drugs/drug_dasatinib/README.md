## About

Dasatinib is a targeted therapy that works by blocking tyrosine kinases to treat chronic myelogenous leukemia and acute lymphoblastic leukemia. The medication is taken by mouth in tablet or syrup forms. It is approved in the European Union and listed in the World Health Organization's essential medicines.
