## About

This is a diagnostic radiopharmaceutical in which cells are labelled with indium-111 tropolonate, used in nuclear medicine imaging to detect sites of inflammation or infection in the body. It is classified in the anatomical therapeutic group of diagnostic radiopharmaceuticals for inflammation and infection detection, and is used in specialised hospital settings for such imaging.
