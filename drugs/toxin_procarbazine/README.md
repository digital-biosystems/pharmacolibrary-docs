# Procarbazine

- **generic name:** Procarbazine
- **T3DB:** [T3D4711](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4711) · **DrugBank:** [DB01168](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB01168) · **PubChem:** [CID 4915](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/4915)
- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

Procarbazine is only found in individuals that have used or taken this drug. It is an antineoplastic agent used primarily in combination with mechlorethamine, vincristine, and prednisone (the MOPP protocol) in the treatment of Hodgkin's disease. The precise mode of cytotoxic action of procarbazine has not been clearly defined. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** procarbazine is rapidly and completely absorbed.
- **Sources and uses:** For use with other anticancer drugs for the treatment of stage III and stage IV Hodgkin's disease.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** The precise mode of cytotoxic action of procarbazine has not been clearly defined. There is evidence that the drug may act by inhibition of protein, RNA and DNA synthesis. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** Procarbazine is part of MOPP, a combination chemotherapy regimen that is carcinogenic to humans (Group 1).
- **Acute toxicity:** LD<sub>50</sub>=785 mg/kg (orally in rats)
