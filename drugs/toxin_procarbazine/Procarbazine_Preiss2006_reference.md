### Reviewer guidance

**The maximum plasma concentration for procarbazine is reported as 12.5 min, a time unit incompatible with a concentration parameter.**

The record derives exclusively from the paper's abstract, meaning reported summary statistics substitute for a fitted model. A structural parameter check failed because the maximum plasma concentration value of 12.5 carries the unit 'min', which has a dimension of time rather than the required mass per volume. This dimensional conflict between the value and its unit led to the rejection of the record. Extracted — procarbazine: Cmax 12.5 min, CL/F 35.8 l/min, t1/2z 9.2 min.
