## About

Doxorubicin is an anthracycline antibiotic chemotherapy drug used to treat various types of cancer, including breast cancer and lymphoma. It is given by intravenous injection, with intravesical administration also used for specific bladder conditions. The medication is widely used globally and is listed among the World Health Organization's essential medicines, with authorized products available in the European Union.
