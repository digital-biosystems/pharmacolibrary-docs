# O-Desmethylangolensin

- **generic name:** O-Desmethylangolensin
- **T3DB:** [T3D3965](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3965) · **PubChem:** [CID 89472](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/89472)

## About

O-Desmethylangolensin (ODMA) is a metabolite of the soy isoflavone daidzein by intestinal bacteria in approximately 80-90% of persons. Studies suggest beneficial health effects associated with daidzein-metabolizing phenotypes, and there is some small association between ODMA production and some phenotypes. Few dietary factors are associated with daidzein-metabolizing phenotypes. …

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin, Animal Toxin
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** O-desmethylangolensin (ODMA) exerts androgen receptor antagonistic activity and agonistic activity towards estrogen receptor (ER)-α and ERβ. The structure of ODMA is somewhat similar to estrogen.
