### Reviewer guidance

**The record was rejected because Vmax was mislabeled as volume of distribution, causing a unit dimension mismatch.**

The parameter labeled Vmax (611 pmol/mg/min) was assigned the meaning of volume of distribution, which requires cubic length units. This created a conflict between the enzymatic rate units and the expected volume dimension. Additionally, the elimination rate constant was mislabeled as Km with a value of 206 1/h. Because the record was built from the abstract alone, these summary statistics were treated as fitted model parameters without validation. Extracted — CYP3A4 protein: V 611 pmol/mg/min, kel 206 1/h; testosterone: kabs 0.09 hour−1.
