# Meclizine

- **generic name:** Meclizine
- **T3DB:** [T3D2874](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2874) · **DrugBank:** [DB00737](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00737) · **PubChem:** [CID 4034](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/4034)
- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

Meclizine is only found in individuals that have used or taken this drug. It is a histamine H1 antagonist used in the treatment of motion sickness, vertigo, and nausea during pregnancy and radiation sickness. [PubChem]Along with its actions as an antagonist at H<sub>1</sub>-receptors, meclizine also possesses anticholinergic, central nervous system depressant, and local anesthetic effects. Meclizine depresses labyrinth excitability and vestibular stimulation and may affect the medullary chemoreceptor trigger zone.

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral. Well absorbed
- **Sources and uses:** For the prevention and treatment of nausea, vomiting, or dizziness associated with motion sickness.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Along with its actions as an antagonist at H<sub>1</sub>-receptors, meclizine also possesses anticholinergic, central nervous system depressant, and local anesthetic effects. Meclizine depresses labyrinth excitability and vestibular stimulation and may affect the medullary chemoreceptor trigger zone.
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 1600 mg/kg (oral)
LD50: 659 mg/kg (i.p.)
