# Ciguatoxin 1

- **generic name:** Ciguatoxin 1
- **T3DB:** [T3D2463](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2463) · **PubChem:** [CID 5311333](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/5311333)

## About

Ciguatoxin is a fish toxin consisting of apolycyclic ethers produced by Gambierdiscus (dinoflagellates) from gambiertoxins. It is ingested by fish and in turn may be ingested by humans, causing ciguatera poisoning.

- **Category:** Animal Toxin, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral
- **Sources and uses:** It is a marine toxin, found in reef fish.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Ciguatoxin lowers the threshold for opening voltage-gated sodium channels in synapses of the nervous system. The effect of opening a sodium channel will cause depolarization, which could sequentially cause paralysis, heart contraction, and changing the senses of hearing and cold. …
- **Health effects:** Ciguatoxin poisoning can cause paralysis, heart contraction, and changing the senses of hearing and cold. Because it does not cross the blood brain barrier (BBB), ciguatoxins solely affect the peripheral nervous system (PNS).
