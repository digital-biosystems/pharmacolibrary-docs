# Clobazam

- **generic name:** Clobazam
- **T3DB:** [T3D4564](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4564) · **DrugBank:** [DB00349](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00349) · **PubChem:** [CID 2789](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/2789)
- **groups:** approved, illicit, investigational

## About

Clobazam belongs to the 1,5-benzodiazepine class of drugs and is expected to have a better side-effect profile compared to older 1,4-benzodiazepines. It has been marketed as an anxiolytic since 1975 and an anticonvulsant since 1984. The oral preparation was FDA approved on October 21, 2011. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** after oral administration of clobazam, it is almost completely absorbed (87% of dose). Bioavailability relative to solution was almost at 100%. Food does not affect absorption. 
Tmax = 1-3 hours.
- **Sources and uses:** For treatment and management of epilepsy and seizures associated with Lennox-Gastaut syndrome, a difficult-to-treat form of childhood epilepsy.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Clobazam binds at distinct binding sites associated with the chloride ionopore at the post-synaptic GABA receptor. These GABA receptors are in various locations in the CNS (limbic, reticular formation) and clobazam increases the duration of time for which the chloride ionopore is open. …
