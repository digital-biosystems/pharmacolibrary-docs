# Vanadyl sulfate

- **generic name:** Vanadyl sulfate
- **T3DB:** [T3D1637](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D1637) · **PubChem:** [CID 34007](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/34007)

## About

Vanadyl sulfate is a sulfate of vanadium that occurs naturally as the mineral pauflerite. It is a common source of vanadium in the laboratory. Vanadyl sulfate is an intermediate in the extraction of vanadium from petroleum residues and is also a component of some food supplements and drugs. …

- **Category:** Food Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Vanadyl sulfate is a common source of vanadium in the laboratory. Vanadyl sulfate is an intermediate in the extraction of vanadium from petroleum residues and is also a component of some food supplements and drugs.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Vanadium damages alveolar macrophages by decreasing the macrophage membrane integrity, thus impairing the cells' phagocytotic ability and viability. The pentavalent form of vanadium, vanadate, is a potent inhibitor of the Ca+-ATPase and Na+,K+-ATPase of plasma membranes, which decreases intracellular ATP concentration. …
- **Health effects:** Breathing high levels of vanadium affects the lungs, throat, and eyes. Ingestion of vanadium may cause kidney and liver damage, birth defects, or death.
- **Minimal risk levels (ATSDR):** Acute Inhalation: 0.0002 mg/m3; Intermediate Oral: 0.003 mg/kg/day
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 4 mg/kg (Intraperitoneal, Mouse); LD50: 560 mg/kg (Subcutaneous, Mouse); LD50: 4450 mg/kg (Dermal, Rabbit)
- **Treatment:** EYES: irrigate opened eyes for several minutes under running water. …
- **Detected in:** blood, cerebrospinal fluid, saliva, urine
