### Reviewer guidance

**Dose-normalized parameters have invalid mass-cubed units, and apparent volume and clearance are physiologically implausible due to unit extraction errors.**

The dose-normalized AUC and Cmax are recorded with units implying mass cubed per volume, creating a dimensional conflict with standard pharmacokinetic definitions. Consequently, the apparent volume of distribution (6644.5 L/kg) and clearance (1048.8 L/h/kg) are orders of magnitude larger than physiological limits. The specific units provided could not be converted to standard international units, leading to these erroneous magnitudes. Extracted — 11 dimethylbiguanide: AUC 1.3e+04 ng·h/mL, AUC/dose 963 ng·h·kg/mL·mg, Cmax 1.49e+03 ng/mL, Cmax/dose 109 ng·kg/mL·mg, tmax 4.3 h, t1/2z 4.3 h, V/F 6.64e+03 L/kg, CL/F 1.05e+03 L/h·kg.
