### Reviewer guidance

**AUC values for Midazolam, Phenobarbital, and Topiramate use unit h*nmol/mL, which lacks a mass dimension required for clearance calculations and cannot be converted.**

The extracted AUC parameters for Midazolam (26.42), Phenobarbital (1325.69), and Topiramate (707.62) are recorded in h*nmol/mL. This unit expresses substance amount over time rather than mass over time, creating a dimensional mismatch with clearance parameters. Consequently, these exposure metrics cannot be converted to standard international units, preventing the construction of a valid pharmacokinetic model. Extracted — Midazolam: V 1.47 L/kg, CL 5.89 mL/min/kg, t1/2z 2.89 h, kel 0.24 1/h, Cmin 1.2e+03 ng/mL, AUC 26.4 h*nmol/mL; fentanyl: V 3.06 L/kg, CL 27.8 mL/min/kg, t1/2z 1.27 h, kel 0.54 1/h, AUC 0.2 h*nmol/mL; Phenobarbital: V 0.63 L/kg, CL 0.13 mL/min/kg, t1/2z 55.4 h, kel 0.00634 1/h, Cavg 45.9 µg/mL, AUC 1.33e+03 h* nmol/mL; Topiramate: V 0.63 L/kg, CL 0.22 mL/min/kg, t1/2z 32.7 h, kel 0.00929 1/h, AUC 708 h*nmol/mL.
