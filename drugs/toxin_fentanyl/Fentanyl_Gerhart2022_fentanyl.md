### Reviewer guidance

**Methadone's Michaelis constant and intrinsic clearance have wrong dimensions, and its metabolites lack dose paths.**

Methadone's Km value of 35.50 uses molar concentration, while intrinsic clearance of 24.67 uses molar flow, both conflicting with expected mass or volume-based kinetics. The metabolites R-methadone, S-methadone, and EDDP are structurally isolated because no pathway exists from the administered dose to the Methadone parent. Furthermore, one parameter's unit could not be converted to standard SI units, leaving the model incomplete. Extracted — Methadone: MW 336 g/mol, fu 0.11, Km 35.5 µmol/L, kel 33.3 min-1, CL 24.7 L/μmol/min; fentanyl: V 2.45 L.
