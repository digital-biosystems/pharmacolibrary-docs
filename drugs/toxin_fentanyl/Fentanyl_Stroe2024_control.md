**AUC values for Midazolam, Phenobarbital, and Topiramate are rejected because the unit h*nmol/mL lacks a standard SI conversion factor, preventing calculation of consistent mass-based parameters.**

The record reports AUCs of 26.23, 1156.60, and 606.03 h*nmol/mL for Midazolam, Phenobarbital, and Topiramate respectively. The review rejects these because the unit h*nmol/mL cannot be converted to the required SI mass-time dimension. Consequently, the specific SI values for these AUCs remain unestablished due to this missing conversion factor.
