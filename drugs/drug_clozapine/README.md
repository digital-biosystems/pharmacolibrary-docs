## About

Clozapine is an atypical antipsychotic medication primarily used to treat schizophrenia and schizoaffective disorder. It is administered orally in the form of tablets or a suspension. Blood levels are routinely measured to guide the correct dose. It is widely used and listed on the World Health Organization's essential medicines list.
