# Neopentane

- **generic name:** Neopentane
- **T3DB:** [T3D3430](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3430) · **PubChem:** [CID 10041](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/10041)

## About

Neopentane is a hydrocarbon and one of three isomers of pentane. Pentanes are components of some fuels, such as gasoline, and are also used as specialty solvents in the laboratory.

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Pentanes are components of some fuels, such as gasoline, and are also used as specialty solvents in the laboratory.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Pentane is a central nervous system depressant. It affects the peripheral nervous system through demyelinization and axonal degeneration.
- **Health effects:** Pentane is a central nervous system depressant and can cause loss of consciousness and coma at high doses. Ingestion may cause pulmonary toxicity due to pentane aspiration, including chemical pneumonitis, acute lung injury, and hemorrhage. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** Neopentane is found in gasoline, which is possibly carcinogenic to humans (Group 2B).
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 100 mg/kg (Intraperitoneal, Mouse)
- **Treatment:** Treatment is mainly symptomatic and supportive. …
