### Reviewer guidance

**The orciprenaline record was rejected because renal clearance was reported as 10 per cent, a dimensionless fraction that cannot serve as a clearance parameter in the one-compartment model.**

The record, built from the abstract of Dengler_1976 alone, lists orciprenaline with terminal half-life 15 hr, volume of distribution 700 liters, total clearance 1400 ml/min, and renal clearance 10 per cent. The renal clearance value carries the unit 'per cent', which is not a clearance dimension and so fails the dimensional check on a structural parameter. Because only the abstract was read, these summary statistics stood in for a fitted model. Extracted — orciprenaline: t1/2z 15 hr, V 700 liters, CL 1.4e+03 ml/min, CLR 10 per cent.

A second, independent reading of the paper (`gpt-oss-120b`) disagrees on the value of renal clearance: this record has 10, the second reading none; it also differs on 1 more field. That field does not shape the model.
