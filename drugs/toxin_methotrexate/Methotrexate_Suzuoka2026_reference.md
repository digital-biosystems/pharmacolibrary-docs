### Reviewer guidance

**The record was held back because bioavailability F and absorption lag Tlag were left at library defaults instead of values extracted from the paper.**

The first-order absorption rate constant kabs was set to 0.0 per minute, implying no absorption. However, the bioavailability F and lag time Tlag were not explicitly reported in the paper and defaulted to internal conventions. This means the simulated exposure profile relies on unverified assumptions rather than fitted estimates specific to this pediatric population. Extracted — methotrexate: CL 3.62 L/h, V1 9.95 L, Q 0.079 L/h, V2 1.1 L, kabs 0 1/min.
