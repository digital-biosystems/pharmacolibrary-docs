### Reviewer guidance

**The volume of distribution (0.025 L) and clearance (47.0 mL/min·kg) for methotrexate are physiologically implausible due to likely unit extraction errors.**

The model records a total volume of 0.025 L for methotrexate, which is unphysiologically small for a drug with observed tissue distribution, suggesting a scaling error. Concurrently, the clearance is reported as 47.0 mL/min·kg, a value inconsistent with the tiny volume, indicating a failure in unit conversion or scaling. Additionally, one reported unit was missing from the standard conversion list, preventing proper SI transformation and leaving the parameter magnitude unverified.
