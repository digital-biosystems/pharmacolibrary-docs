### Reviewer guidance

**Methotrexate clearance is implausibly low, terminal half-life has a dimensionally incorrect unit, and area under the curve units could not be converted to SI.**

The reported clearance of 0.027 L/h and volume of 0.018 L for mice result in values outside the physiological window. The terminal half-life is assigned the unit h-1, which is a frequency dimension incompatible with the time dimension required for half-life. Furthermore, the area under the curve unit of day*µM was not recognized for SI conversion, preventing proper standardization of this summary statistic. Extracted — methotrexate: CL 0.027 L/h, V 0.018 L, t1/2z 0.43 h-1, AUC 0.19 day*µM, kabs 0 1/min.
