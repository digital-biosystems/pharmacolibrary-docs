### Reviewer guidance

**Bioavailability (F) and absorption lag time (Tlag) were assigned library defaults rather than paper-specific estimates, and defined covariate effects were not simulated.**

The record lists bioavailability and absorption lag time as defaulted parameters because no explicit values were extracted from the source paper. Although clearance, volume, and intercompartmental clearance covariates for age, body surface area, weight, GFR, and BUN are present, the model evaluation only simulated the reference individual. Consequently, the predictive impact of these covariate relationships remains unverified.
