# Pilocarpine

- **generic name:** Pilocarpine
- **T3DB:** [T3D2979](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2979) · **DrugBank:** [DB01085](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB01085) · **PubChem:** [CID 5910](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/5910)
- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

Pilocarpine is only found in individuals that have used or taken this drug. It is a slowly hydrolyzed muscarinic agonist with no nicotinic effects. Pilocarpine is used as a miotic and in the treatment of glaucoma. [PubChem]Pilocarpine is a cholinergic parasympathomimetic agent. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, eye contact. There was a decrease in the rate of absorption of pilocarpine from SALAGEN Tablets when taken with a high fat meal by 12 healthy male volunteers
- **Sources and uses:** For the treatment of radiation-induced dry mouth (xerostomia) and symptoms of dry mouth in patients with Sj&ouml;grens syndrome.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Pilocarpine is a cholinergic parasympathomimetic agent. It increase secretion by the exocrine glands, and produces contraction of the iris sphincter muscle and ciliary muscle (when given topically to the eyes) by mainly stimulating muscarinic receptors.
