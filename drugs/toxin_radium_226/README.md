# Radium-226

- **generic name:** Radium-226

## About

Chemical compound.

<p><small>Source: <a href="https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q27286584">Wikidata Q27286584</a> · <a href="https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/">CC0</a></small></p>

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** inhalation, injection, oral, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Radium has been used as a radiation source for treating cancer, in radiography of metals, and combined with other metals as a neutron source for research and radiation instrument calibration. Until the 1960s, radium was a component of the luminous paints used for watch and clock dials, instrument panels in airplanes, military instruments, and compasses.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Ionizing radiation produced by radium damages the DNA, resulting in gene mutations and chromosomal aberrations. This can both initiate and promote carcinogenesis, and interfere with reproduction and development. …
- **Health effects:** Radium is highly radioactive and its decay product, radon gas, is also radioactive. It has been shown to cause effects on the blood (anemia) and eyes (cataracts). …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 1, carcinogenic to humans.
