## Record summary

**As extracted:** Dexrazoxane (concentrations from the PK model of Baldwin_1996) drives % cell viability (in %): indirect response — drug stimulates the loss of % cell viability.

**Model:** Model available in **Models**; browser simulation unavailable.

> Dexrazoxane concentrations (µM) act on % cell viability via an indirect response model where the drug stimulates cell loss (characterized by Smax and SC50) against a background of first-order cell growth (kg). For JIMT-1, key parameters include kg = 0.0171 h⁻¹, Smax = 0.0482 h⁻¹, SC50 = 21.2 µM, IC50 = 97.5 µM, and Imax = 0.74, while for MDA-MB-468 they are kg = 0.0167 h⁻¹, Smax = 0.29 h⁻¹, SC50 = 17.5 µM, IC50 = 36 µM, and Imax = 0.825.
