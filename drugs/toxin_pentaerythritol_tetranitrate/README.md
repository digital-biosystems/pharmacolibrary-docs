# Pentaerythritol tetranitrate

## About

Pentaerythritol tetranitrate (PETN), also known as PENT, PENTA, TEN, corpent, penthrite is the nitrate ester of pentaerythritol, and is structurally very similar to nitroglycerin. PETN is best known as an explosive. It is one of the most powerful high explosives known. …

- **Category:** Household Toxin, Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, dermal, inhalation
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Nitrate's toxicity is a result of it's conversion to nitrite once in the body. Nitrite causes the autocatalytic oxidation of oxyhemoglobin to hydrogen peroxide and methemoglobin. …
- **Health effects:** Nitrate and nitrite poisoning causes methemoglobinemia. Nitrites may cause pregnancy complications and developmental effects. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** No indication of carcinogenicity (not listed by IARC).
- **Treatment:** Methemoglobinemia can be treated with supplemental oxygen and methylene blue 1% solution administered intravenously slowly over five minutes followed by IV flush with normal saline. …
