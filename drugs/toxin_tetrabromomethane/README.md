# Tetrabromomethane

- **generic name:** Tetrabromomethane
- **T3DB:** [T3D1762](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D1762) · **PubChem:** [CID 11205](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/11205)

## About

Tetrabromomethane, also known as carbon tetrabromide, is a carbon bromide. It is used as a solvent for greases, waxes and oils, in plastic and rubber industry for blowing, vulcanization, and polymerization, as a sedative and as an intermediate in manufacturing agrochemicals. Due to its non-flammability it is used as an ingredient in fire resistant chemicals. …

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** It is used as a solvent for greases, waxes and oils, in plastic and rubber industry for blowing, vulcanization, and polymerization, as a sedative and as an intermediate in manufacturing agrochemicals. Due to its non-flammability it is used as an ingredient in fire resistant chemicals. …
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Bromine is a powerful oxidizing agent and is able to release oxygen free radicals from the water in mucous membranes. These free radicals are also potent oxidizers and produce tissue damage. …
- **Health effects:** Bromine vapour causes irritation and direct damage to the mucous membranes. Elemental bromine also burns the skin. …
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 298 mg/kg (Subcutaneous, Mouse); LD50: 56 mg/kg (Intravenous, Mouse)
- **Treatment:** EYES: irrigate opened eyes for several minutes under running water. …
