## About

Aripiprazole is an atypical antipsychotic used primarily for schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, and autism-related irritability. It is given orally as tablets or solutions, which have good absorption, or by intramuscular injection for extended-release treatment. Aripiprazole is widely used and authorized in the European Union, where it is indicated for schizophrenia and bipolar disorder.
