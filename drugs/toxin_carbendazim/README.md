# Carbendazim

- **generic name:** Carbendazim
- **T3DB:** [T3D0942](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D0942) · **PubChem:** [CID 25429](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/25429)
- **groups:** investigational

## About

Carbendazim is a widely used, broad-spectrum benzimidazole fungicide. It is also a metabolite of benomyl (another widely used fungicide). It is also employed as a casting worm control agent in amenity turf situations such as golf greens and tennis courts. …

- **Category:** Pesticide, Food Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** inhalation, oral, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** It is a systemic fungicide that is selectively toxic to microorganisms and invertebrates. It is also employed as a casting worm control agent in amenity turf situations such as golf greens and tennis courts. …
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Carbendazim targets beta tubulin in actively dividing cells. It binds to microtubules, interfering with cell functions, such as meiosis and intracellular transportation.
- **Health effects:** Carbendazim is a suspected endocrine disruptor. It is also a developmental toxin. …
- **Minimal risk levels (ATSDR):** The MRLs for fresh produce in the European Union are now between 0.1 and 0.7 mg/kg
- **Acute toxicity:** Acute oral LD50 for rats is >15000 mg/kg and >2500 mg/kg for dogs
- **Treatment:** For acute exposures and first aid: EYES: irrigate opened eyes for several minutes under running water. …
