## About

Pentobarbital is a short-acting barbiturate sedative used to treat status epilepticus and assist with anesthesia. It is administered via intravenous, intramuscular, oral, or rectal routes, with specific formulations available for injection, tablets, capsules, or suppositories. The drug is approved for veterinary use, frequently used for euthanasia, and utilized in human executions and physician-assisted suicide in some regions.
