- **what it is:** guideline estimate — CPIC/DPWG-derived estimate, not a primary-paper estimate.
- **source:** the SEFF/SEOM dosing guideline ([ClinPGx PA166254881](https://www.clinpgx.org/guidelineAnnotation/PA166254881))
- **gene:** DPYD
- **mechanism:** metabolism — the gene's enzyme clears the drug
- **applies to:** pharmacokinetics (exposure)
- **phenotype (as the guideline names it):** Poor metabolizer (Activity score 0–0.5)
- **parameter it changes:** CL (`Q22`)
- **effect:** not converted to a θ — the guideline gives dosing advice (below), not a fold-change on the parameter
- **guideline recommendation:** Poor metabolizer (Activity score 0–0.5) | Two mutated alleles (*2A or *13) One mutated allele (*2A or *13) and one mutated allele (c.2846A&gt;T or HapB3) | Complete or almost complete DPD deficiency and increased risk for severe or even fatal drug toxicity when treated with fluoropyrimidines | Contraindicated treatment with fluoropyrimidines; look for alternative agents*
- **phenotype groups:** standard phenotypes (PM / IM / NM / RM / UM) from the reference table

### Notes from the extraction

- guideline-derived estimate, NOT extracted from a per-genotype PK table
- guideline recommends avoid/alternative therapy — not a CL θ
