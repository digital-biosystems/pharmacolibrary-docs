# Diazepam

- **generic name:** Diazepam
- **T3DB:** [T3D2903](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2903) · **DrugBank:** [DB00829](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00829) · **PubChem:** [CID 3016](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/3016)
- **groups:** approved, illicit, investigational, vet_approved

## About

Diazepam is a benzodiazepine with anticonvulsant, anxiolytic, sedative, muscle relaxant, and amnesic properties and a long duration of action. Its actions are mediated by enhancement of gamma-aminobutyric acid activity. It is used in the treatment of severe anxiety disorders, as a hypnotic in the short-term management of insomnia, as a sedative and premedicant, as an anticonvulsant, and in the management of alcohol withdrawal syndrome. (From Martindale, The Extra Pharmacopoeia, 30th ed, p589).

- **Category:** Drug, Food Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** parenteral (intramuscular), oral, enteral(rectal).Essentially complete, with a bioavailability of 93%.
- **Sources and uses:** Used in the treatment of severe anxiety disorders, as a hypnotic in the short-term management of insomnia, as a sedative and premedicant, as an anticonvulsant, and in the management of alcohol withdrawal syndrome.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Benzodiazepines bind nonspecifically to benzodiazepine receptors which mediate sleep, affects muscle relaxation, anticonvulsant activity, motor coordination, and memory. As benzodiazepine receptors are thought to be coupled to gamma-aminobutyric acid-A (GABA<sub>A</sub>) receptors, this enhances the effects of GABA by increasing GABA affinity for the GABA receptor. …
- **Health effects:** They cause slurred speech, disorientation and "drunken" behavior. They are physically and psychologically addictive. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 3, not classifiable as to its carcinogenicity to humans.
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 1200 mg/kg (oral, rat)
LD50: 1000 mg/kg (oral, dog) 
LD50: 700 mg/kg (oral, mice)
- **Treatment:** General supportive measures should be employed, along with intravenous fluids, and an adequate airway maintained. …
