- **generic name:** Zolpidem
- **T3DB:** [T3D2787](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2787) · **DrugBank:** [DB00425](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00425) · **PubChem:** [CID 5732](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/5732)
- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

Zolpidem (sold under the brand names Ambien, Ambien CR, Stilnox, and Sublinox) is a prescription medication used for the treatment of insomnia, as well as some brain disorders. It is a short-acting nonbenzodiazepine hypnotic of the imidazopyridine class that potentiates gamma-aminobutyric acid (GABA), an inhibitory neurotransmitter, by binding to GABAA receptors at the same location as benzodiazepines. It works quickly (usually within 15 minutes) and has a short half-life (two to three hours). …

- **Category:** Drug, Food Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral.
Zolpidem is rapidly absorbed from the GI tract.
- **Sources and uses:** For the short-term treatment of insomnia.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Zolpidem modulates the alpha-subunit, known as the benzodiazepine receptor, within the GABA<sub>A</sub> receptor chloride channel macromolecular complex. Unlike the benzodiazepines, which non-selectively interact with all three alpha-receptor subtypes, Zolpidem preferentially binds to the alpha-1 receptor.
- **Health effects:** They cause slurred speech, disorientation and "drunken" behavior. They are physically and psychologically addictive.
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 695 mg/kg (Oral, Rat)
- **Treatment:** General symptomatic and supportive measures should be used along with immediate gastric lavage where appropriate. …
