## About

Cefuroxime is a second-generation cephalosporin antibiotic used to treat a range of bacterial infections, including pneumonia, sinusitis, pharyngitis, urinary tract infections, gonorrhea, Lyme disease, and skin infections. It is an approved antibiotic that is widely used in human medicine, available both alone and in combination with other antibacterials.
