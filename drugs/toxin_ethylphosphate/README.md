# Ethylphosphate

- **generic name:** Ethylphosphate
- **T3DB:** [T3D4828](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4828) · **DrugBank:** [DB03347](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB03347) · **PubChem:** [CID 6535](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/6535)
- **groups:** experimental

## About

Ethylphosphate is produced by the reaction between diethylphosphate and water, with ethanol as a byproduct.

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Food Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Sources and uses:** Ethylphosphate is produced by the reaction between diethylphosphate and water, with ethanol as a byproduct.
