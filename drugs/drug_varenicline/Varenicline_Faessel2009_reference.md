### Reviewer guidance

**The record was rejected because the varenicline exposure parameter AUC(0-24) is recorded as 197 ng, a unit missing the time dimension expected for an area under the concentration-time curve.**

For varenicline in healthy adolescent smokers, the abstract-only source reports AUC(0-24) = 197 ng, but an area under the curve must carry units of concentration times time (e.g., ng·h/L), so the structural parameter fails a dimensional check. The other parameters — CL/F 10.4 L/h, V/F 211 L, and a fixed absorption rate constant of 0.98 h⁻¹ — are dimensionally consistent. Because only the paper's abstract was read, these summary statistics stood in for a fitted model, which also limits the record. Extracted — varenicline: CL/F 10.4 L/h, AUC 197 ng, V/F 211 L, kabs 0.98 h -1.

A second, independent reading of the paper (`gpt-oss-120b`) disagrees on how the model is parameterised: this record has apparent, the second reading mechanistic; it also differs on 5 more fields. That field shapes the model, so the record is marked disputed.
