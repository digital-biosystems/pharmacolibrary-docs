## About

Amikacin is an aminoglycoside antibiotic used to treat serious bacterial infections like sepsis, meningitis, and resistant tuberculosis. Blood levels are routinely measured to guide dosing. It is listed on the WHO Essential Medicines list and has authorized products in the European Union.
