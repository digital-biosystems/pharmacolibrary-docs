# Selegiline

- **generic name:** Selegiline
- **T3DB:** [T3D2965](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2965) · **DrugBank:** [DB01037](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB01037) · **PubChem:** [CID 5195](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/5195)
- **groups:** approved, vet_approved

## About

A selective, irreversible inhibitor of Type B monoamine oxidase. It is used in newly diagnosed patients with Parkinson's disease. It may slow progression of the clinical disease and delay the requirement for levodopa therapy. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral. Rapidly absorbed from the gastrointestinal tract.
- **Sources and uses:** Monotherapy for initial treatment of Parkinson's disease, as well as an adjunct therapy in patients with a decreased response to levodopa/carbadopa. Also used for the palliative treatment of mild to moderate Alzheimer's disease and at higher doses, for the treatment of depression.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Although the mechanisms for selegiline's beneficial action in the treatment of Parkinson's disease are not fully understood, the selective, irreversible inhibition of monoamine oxidase type B (MAO-B) is thought to be of primary importance. MAO-B is involved in the oxidative deamination of dopamine in the brain. …
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 63 mg/kg (Intravenous, Rat)
