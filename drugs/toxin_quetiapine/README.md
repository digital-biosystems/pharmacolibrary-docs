# Quetiapine

- **generic name:** Quetiapine
- **T3DB:** [T3D2555](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2555) · **DrugBank:** [DB01224](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB01224) · **PubChem:** [CID 5002](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/5002)
- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

The most common side effect is sedation, and is prescribed specifically for this effect in patients with sleep disorders. Seroquel will put the patient into a drowsy state, and will help the patient fall asleep. It is one of the most sedating of all anti psychotic drugs, rivaling even the most sedating older antipsychotics. …

- **Category:** Drug, Food Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** inhalation (MSDS, A308), ingestion (MSDS, A308), dermal (MSDS, A308)
Rapidly and well absorbed.
- **Sources and uses:** Quetiapine is indicated for the treatment of schizophrenia, depressive episodes associated with bipolar disorder, acute manic episodes associated with bipolar I disorder, and maintenance treatment of bipolar I disorder.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** The mechanism of action of quetiapine, as with other drugs used to treat schizophrenia, is unknown. However, it is thought that the drug's therapeutic activity in schizophrenia is mediated through a combination of dopamine type 2 and serotonin type 2 (5HT<sub>2</sub>) receptor antagonism. …
- **Health effects:** Tachycardia, development of tardive dyskinesia, an incurable neurological disorder, and a neuroleptic malignant syndrome may result from use or overdose of Quetiapine.
- **Treatment:** There is no specific antidote. …
- **Detected in:** blood, cerebrospinal fluid
