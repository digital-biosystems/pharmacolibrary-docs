# Dihydrotestosterone

- **generic name:** Dihydrotestosterone
- **T3DB:** [T3D4751](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4751) · **DrugBank:** [DB02901](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB02901) · **PubChem:** [CID 10635](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/10635)
- **groups:** illicit, investigational

## About

Dihydrotestosterone is a potent androgenic metabolite of testosterone. Dihydrotestosterone (DHT) is generated by a 5-alpha reduction of testosterone. Unlike testosterone, DHT cannot be aromatized to estradiol therefore DHT is considered a pure androgenic steroid. -- Pubchem; Dihydrotestosterone (DHT) (INN: androstanolone) is a biologically active metabolite of the hormone testosterone, formed primarily in the prostate gland, testes, hair follicles, and adrenal glands by the enzyme 5-alpha-reductase by means of reducing the alpha 4,5 double-bond. …

- **Category:** Food Toxin, Natural Toxin, Animal Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** bioavailability is very low (0-2%) following oral administration.
- **Sources and uses:** Dihydrotestosterone is an endogenously produced metabolite found in the human body. It is used in metabolic reactions, catabolic reactions or waste generation.
