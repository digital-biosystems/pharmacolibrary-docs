# 1,1,2,2-Tetrabromoethane

- **generic name:** 1,1,2,2-Tetrabromoethane
- **T3DB:** [T3D1804](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D1804) · **PubChem:** [CID 6588](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/6588)

## About

1,1,2,2-Tetrabromoethane is an organobromide compound. It is used to separate mineral ores from its supporting rock by means of preferential flotation. Bromine is a halogen element with the symbol Br and atomic number 35. …

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** It is used to separate mineral ores from its supporting rock by means of preferential flotation. Diatomic bromine does not occur naturally, but bromine salts can be found in crustal rock.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Bromine is a powerful oxidizing agent and is able to release oxygen free radicals from the water in mucous membranes. These free radicals are also potent oxidizers and produce tissue damage. …
- **Health effects:** Bromine vapour causes irritation and direct damage to the mucous membranes. Elemental bromine also burns the skin. …
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 1200 mg/kg (Oral, Rat); LD50: 5250 mg/kg (Dermal, Rat); LD50: 443 mg/kg (Intraperitoneal, Mouse)
- **Treatment:** EYES: irrigate opened eyes for several minutes under running water. …
