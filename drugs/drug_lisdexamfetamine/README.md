## About

Lisdexamfetamine is a centrally acting sympathomimetic stimulant prescribed for attention deficit hyperactivity disorder, narcolepsy, and binge eating disorder. It is taken by mouth in capsule or chewable tablet form, as it is well absorbed through the digestive tract. The medication is widely used and is authorized for sale in the European Union.
