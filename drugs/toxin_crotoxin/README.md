# Crotoxin

- **generic name:** Crotoxin
- **T3DB:** [T3D2543](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2543) · **PubChem:** none

## About

Crotoxin is a peptide toxin produced by the South American rattlesnake (Crotalus durissus terrificus).

- **Category:** Protein, Animal Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** injection (sting/bite)
- **Sources and uses:** Crotoxin is a peptide toxin produced by the South American rattlesnake (Crotalus durissus terrificus).
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Crotoxin inhibits neuromuscular transmission by presynaptically blocking acetylcholine release from the nerve termini.
- **Health effects:** Crotoxin is neurotoxic.
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 0.262 mg/kg (Intravenous, Mouse)
LD50: 0.216 mg/kg (Intraperitoneal, Mouse)
