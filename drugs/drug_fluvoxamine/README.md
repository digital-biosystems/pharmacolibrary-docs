## About

Fluvoxamine is a selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor used to treat major depressive disorder and obsessive-compulsive disorder. It is given by mouth in various solid dosage forms. This medication is a standard option in psychiatric care and appears on the World Health Organization's essential medicines list.
