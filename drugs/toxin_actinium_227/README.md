# Actinium-227

- **generic name:** Actinium-227

## About

Chemical compound.

<p><small>Source: <a href="https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q81986738">Wikidata Q81986738</a> · <a href="https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/">CC0</a></small></p>

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation
- **Sources and uses:** Actinium's radioactivity make it a valuable as a neutron source for energy.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** The ionizing radiation produced by actinium causes cellular damage that includes DNA breakage, accurate or inaccurate repair, apoptosis, gene mutations, chromosomal change, and genetic instability. This leads to loss of normal cell and tissue homeostasis, and development of malignancy. …
- **Health effects:** Actinium is extremely radioactive and thus poses a dangerous cancer risk. It can also cause acute radiation syndrome.
- **Carcinogenicity:** Internalized radionuclides that emit α or β particles are carcinogenic to humans (Group 1). …
