# Verapamil

- **generic name:** Verapamil
- **T3DB:** [T3D3544](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3544) · **DrugBank:** [DB00661](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00661) · **PubChem:** [CID 2520](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/2520)
- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

Verapamil is only found in individuals that have used or taken this drug. Verapamil is a calcium channel blocker that is a class IV anti-arrhythmia agent. [PubChem]Verapamil inhibits voltage-dependent calcium channels. Specifically, its effect on L-type calcium channels in the heart causes a reduction in ionotropy and chronotropy, thuis reducing heart rate and blood pressure. …

- **Category:** Drug, Food Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** orally, 90‰ЫТ100% of Verapamil is absorbed. Intravenous.
- **Sources and uses:** Drug used for the treatment of hypertension, angina, and cluster headache prophylaxis.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Verapamil inhibits voltage-dependent calcium channels. Specifically, its effect on L-type calcium channels in the heart causes a reduction in ionotropy and chronotropy, thuis reducing heart rate and blood pressure. …
- **Health effects:** Cardiovascular: angina pectoris, AV block (2&#176; & 3&#176;), atrioventricular dissociation, CHF, pulmonary edema, chest pain, claudication, myocardial infarction, palpitations, purpura (vasculitis), syncope. Digestive system: diarrhea, dry mouth, gastrointestinal distress, gingival hyperplasia. …
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 8 mg/kg (Intravenous, Mouse)
- **Treatment:** Treatment of overdosage should be supportive. …
