## About

Zinc is a mineral used as a dietary supplement and, in the form of zinc salts, to treat the copper-accumulation disorder hepatolenticular degeneration (Wilson's disease). It is widely available as a supplement, and one zinc product is authorised in the European Union for Wilson's disease.
