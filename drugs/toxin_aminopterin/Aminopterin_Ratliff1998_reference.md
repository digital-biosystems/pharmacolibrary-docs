### Reviewer guidance

**The aminopterin AUC value of 1.2 h/L lacks the required mass dimension, conflicting with the definition of area under the concentration-time curve.**

The record lists the AUC for aminopterin as 1.2 h/L, which corresponds to time per volume rather than the correct mass per volume per time. This dimensional inconsistency makes the parameter physically meaningless for pharmacokinetic analysis. Furthermore, the entire model is derived solely from an abstract, meaning these values are reported summary statistics rather than parameters fitted from individual data. Extracted — aminopterin: AUC 1.2 h/L, t1/2z 3.64 hours, Fab 83.5.
