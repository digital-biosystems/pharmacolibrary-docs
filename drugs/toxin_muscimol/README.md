# Muscimol

- **generic name:** Muscimol
- **T3DB:** [T3D2456](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2456) · **PubChem:** [CID 205536](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/205536)
- **groups:** investigational

## About

Muscimol (Agarin, Pantherine) is the major psychoactive alkaloid present in many mushrooms of the Amanita genus. Unlike psilocybin, a tryptamine, muscimol is a potent, selective agonist of the GABAA receptor. Amanita muscaria contains two main active ingredients, Ibotenic acid and its derivative Muscimol. …

- **Category:** Fungal Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, dermal, inhalation, and parenteral (contaminated drugs).
- **Sources and uses:** Muscimol is produced naturally in the mushrooms Amanita muscaria, Amanita pantherina, and Amanita gemmata, along with muscarine, muscazone, and ibotenic acid.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Muscimol is a potent GABAA agonist, activating the receptor for the brain's major inhibitory neurotransmitter, GABA. Muscimol actually binds to the same binding site on the GABAA receptor complex as GABA itself, as opposed to other GABAergic drugs such as barbiturates and benzodiazepines which bind to separate regulatory sites. …
- **Health effects:** Muscimol is neurotoxic and produces hallucinations.
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 3.8 mg/kg (Subcutaneous, Mouse)
LD50: 2.5 mg/kg (Intraperitoneal, Mouse)
LD50: 4.5 mg/kg (Intravenous, Rat) 
LD50: 45 mg/kg (Oral, Rat)
