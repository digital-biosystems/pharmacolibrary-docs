**Infliximab volumes V1 and V2 carry clearance units (L/day) instead of volume, rendering magnitudes implausible.**

The extracted values for V1 (4.20) and V2 (1.63) are assigned units of L/day, which dimensionally conflict with their meaning as volumes of distribution. This dimensional inconsistency causes the clearance-to-volume ratio to fall outside physiological limits, flagging the values as implausible.
