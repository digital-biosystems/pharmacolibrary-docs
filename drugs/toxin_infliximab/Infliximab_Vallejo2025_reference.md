### Reviewer guidance

**The FcRn association rate constant kon (90 1/µM/day) was rejected because its units could not be converted to a standard SI equivalent.**

The model specifies kon as a second-order association rate constant for FcRn binding with units of 1/µM/day. This specific unit combination was not recognized by the system for standardization, preventing a valid SI value from being calculated. As a result, a dimensional consistency check flagged a conflict between the reported units and the expected physical dimensions for the parameter.
