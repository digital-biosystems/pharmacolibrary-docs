# 1-Pentene

- **generic name:** 1-Pentene
- **T3DB:** [T3D3458](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3458) · **PubChem:** [CID 8004](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/8004)

## About

1-Pentene is a hydrocarbon and one of the isomers of pentene. Pentenes are components of gasoline and are often produced as by-products of catalytic of thermal cracking of petroleum.

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Pentenes are components of gasoline and are often produced as by-products of catalytic of thermal cracking of petroleum.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Petroleum distillates are central nervous system depressants and cause pulmonary damage. During metabolism. 1-pentene is oxidized at the double bond and excreted as the alcohol or its conjugate.
- **Health effects:** Petroleum distillates are aspiration hazards and may cause pulmonary damage, central nervous system depression, and cardiac effects such as cardiac arrhythmias. They may also affect the blood, immune system, liver, and kidney.
- **Carcinogenicity:** 1-Pentene is found in gasoline, which is possibly carcinogenic to humans (Group 2B).
- **Treatment:** Treatment is mainly symptomatic and supportive. …
