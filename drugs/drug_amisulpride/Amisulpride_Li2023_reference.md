### Reviewer guidance

**The amisulpride two-compartment model was held back because the intercompartmental rate constants k12 and k21 (and a lag time) were left at library defaults instead of being estimated from Li_2023.**

The record reports fitted parameters for amisulpride in Chinese patients with schizophrenia (CL/F 60.5 L/h, V/F 645 L, kabs 0.106 h−1, CL 61.1 L/h, V 1720 L), but the intercompartmental transfer rate constants k12 and k21 of the two-compartment structure have no values from the paper and were filled with library defaults, which affect the simulated profile without support from this paper. In addition, the builder assumed F=1 and Fm=1 with no molar correction, so the parameters are apparent rather than absolute. The failed check on deviations returned 'defaulted_parameters: not acceptable'. Extracted — amisulpride: CL/F 60.5 L/h, V/F 645 L, kabs 0.106 h−1, CL 61.1 L/h, V 1.72e+03 L.

A second, independent reading of the paper (`gpt-oss-120b`) disagrees on the value of clearance: this record has 61.1, the second reading none; it also differs on 1 more field. That field does not shape the model.
