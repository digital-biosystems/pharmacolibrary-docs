## About

Yttrium-90Y silicate colloid is a therapeutic radiopharmaceutical used to treat inflammatory joint disease by delivering radiation directly into the joint. It is classified under yttrium (90Y) antiinflammatory radiopharmaceuticals and is used mainly in specialist nuclear medicine settings for joint injections.
