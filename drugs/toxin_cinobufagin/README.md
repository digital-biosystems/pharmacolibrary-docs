# Cinobufagin

- **generic name:** Cinobufagin
- **T3DB:** [T3D2535](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2535) · **PubChem:** [CID 10103](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/10103)

## About

Cinobufagin is a cardiotoxic bufanolide steroid secreted by the Asiatic toad (Bufo gargarizans).

- **Category:** Animal Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** ingestion
- **Sources and uses:** Cinobufagin is a cardiotoxic bufanolide steroid secreted by the Asiatic toad (Bufo gargarizans).
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Cinobufagin blocks the sodium/potassium pump in heart tissue.
- **Health effects:** Cinobufagin is cardiotoxic.
