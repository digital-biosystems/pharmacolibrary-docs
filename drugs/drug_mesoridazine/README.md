## About

Mesoridazine is a phenothiazine antipsychotic drug used to treat schizophrenia and schizophreniform disorder. It is usually taken by mouth as tablets, and can also be given by intramuscular injection as a suspension or solution. The drug is no longer used in the United States because it was withdrawn due to dangerous side effects affecting the heart rhythm.
