# (S,E)-Zearalenone

- **generic name:** (S,E)-Zearalenone
- **T3DB:** [T3D3665](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3665) · **PubChem:** [CID 5375083](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/5375083)

## About

cis-Zearalenone is a metabolite of Fusarium species.

- **Category:** Fungal Toxin, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, dermal, inhalation, and parenteral (contaminated drugs).
- **Sources and uses:** Zearalenone (ZEA), also known as RAL and F-2 mycotoxin, is a potent estrogenic metabolite produced by some Giberella species. Several Fusarium species also produce zearalenone as their primary toxin. …
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Zearalenone's genotoxicity results from it's ability to cause DNA fragmentation, chromosome aberrations, and DNA adduct formation. It exerts cytotoxic effects by enhancing lipid peroxidation, increasing oxidative stress, and inducing apoptosis. …
- **Health effects:** Zearalenone is a reproductive toxin and has been shown to cause infertility, abortion or other breeding problems in livestock. In humans it can cause earlier onset of puberty in children, endometrial adenocarcinomas, hyperplasia, and breast cancer in women. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 3, not classifiable as to its carcinogenicity to humans.
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: >2000 mg/kg (Oral, Mouse)
LD50: >500 mg/kg (Intraperitoneal, Mouse)
