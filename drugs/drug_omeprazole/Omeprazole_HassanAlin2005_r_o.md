### Reviewer guidance

**Omeprazole was rejected because metabolite links lacked rate constants and a clearance unit remained unconverted to standard SI units.**

The model structure defined metabolism from omeprazole to its 5-hydroxy and sulphone metabolites, but the link parameters were missing, making the metabolites unreachable from the dose. The clearance unit, ml/min, was not recognized for conversion to SI, so the 98.0 value remained non-standard. Additionally, a structural parameter failed a dimension check, indicating a unit inconsistency in the model definition. Extracted — omeprazole: AUC 0.62 µmol×h/l, Cmax 0.72 µmol/l, t1/2z 0.52 h, CL 98 ml/min, V 0.653 L, kabs 3 h-1, tlag 2.5 h.

A second, independent reading of the paper (`gpt-oss-120b`) disagrees on the links between molecules: this record has omeprazole → omeprazole 5-hydroxy metabolite (metabolism); omeprazole → omeprazole sulphone metabolite (metabolism), the second reading none. That field shapes the model, so the record is marked disputed.
