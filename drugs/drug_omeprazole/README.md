## About

This proton-pump inhibitor manages acid-related conditions such as gastroesophageal reflux disease, peptic ulcers, and Zollinger-Ellison syndrome. Patients typically swallow capsules or tablets, while an intravenous solution is available for those who cannot take oral medications. It is a global essential medicine widely prescribed as a generic product in many regions, including the United States.
