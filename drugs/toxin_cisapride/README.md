# Cisapride

- **generic name:** Cisapride
- **T3DB:** [T3D2836](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2836) · **DrugBank:** [DB00604](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00604) · **PubChem:** [CID 6917698](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/6917698)
- **groups:** approved, withdrawn

## About

In many countries (including Canada) cisapride has been either withdrawn or has had its indications limited due to reports about long QT syndrome due to cisapride, which predisposes to arrhythmias. The FDA issued a warning letter regarding this risk to health care professionals and patients.

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** cisapride is rapidly absorbed after oral administration, with an absolute bioavailability of 35-40%.
- **Sources and uses:** For the symptomatic treatment of adult patients with nocturnal heartburn due to gastroesophageal reflux disease.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Cisapride acts through the stimulation of the serotonin 5-HT<sub>4</sub> receptors which increases acetylcholine release in the enteric nervous system (specifically the myenteric plexus). This results in increased tone and amplitude of gastric (especially antral) contractions, relaxation of the pyloric sphincter and the duodenal bulb, and increased peristalsis of the duodenum and jejunum resulting in accelerated gastric emptying and intestinal transit.
