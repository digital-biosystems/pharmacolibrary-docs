# Pioglitazone

- **generic name:** Pioglitazone
- **T3DB:** [T3D4981](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4981) · **DrugBank:** [DB01132](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB01132) · **PubChem:** [CID 4829](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/4829)
- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

Pioglitazone is used for the treatment of diabetes mellitus type 2. Pioglitazone selectively stimulates nuclear receptor peroxisone proliferator-activated receptor gamma (PPAR-gamma). It modulates the transcription of the insulin-sensitive genes involved in the control of glucose and lipid metabolism in the lipidic, muscular tissues and in the liver.

- **Category:** Synthetic Toxin, Drug
- **Route of exposure:** following oral administration, in the fasting state, pioglitazone is first measurable in serum within 30 minutes, with peak concentrations observed within 2 hours. Food slightly delays the time to peak serum concentration to 3 to 4 hours, but does not alter the extent of absorption.
- **Sources and uses:** Treatment of Type II diabetes mellitus
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Pioglitazone acts as an agonist at peroxisome proliferator activated receptors (PPAR) in target tissues for insulin action such as adipose tissue, skeletal muscle, and liver. Activation of PPAR-gamma receptors increases the transcription of insulin-responsive genes involved in the control of glucose production, transport, and utilization. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 2A, probably carcinogenic to humans.
- **Acute toxicity:** Hypogycemia; LD<sub>50</sub>=mg/kg (orally in rat)
