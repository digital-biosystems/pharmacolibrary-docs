## Record summary

**As extracted:** Dose (the dose) drives Mean 24-h intragastric pH: direct Emax (saturable) effect.

**Model:** Model available in **Models**; browser simulation unavailable.

> Esomeprazole dose acts on mean 24-h intragastric pH via an additive Emax model where the drug increases pH above baseline. The baseline pH is 3.06 with a maximum increase of 3.46; the dose for half-maximum increase is 33.9 mg/day in healthy subjects and 63.3 mg/day in GERD patients, with esomeprazole being 1.60 times more potent than omeprazole.
