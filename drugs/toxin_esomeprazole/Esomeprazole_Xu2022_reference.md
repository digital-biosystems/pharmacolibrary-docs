### Reviewer guidance

**The esomeprazole record lacks explicit absorption rate and lag time estimates, relying on library defaults and unsupported F=1 assumptions for oral clearance and volume.**

The absorption rate constant (ka) and lag time (Tlag) were not extracted from the paper, forcing the use of library placeholders that do not reflect the specific pharmacokinetic behavior described. Additionally, the bioavailability was assumed to be unity (F=1) without justification, affecting the apparent clearance and volume estimates. These unsubstantiated defaults prevent the model from accurately representing the drug's absorption profile, resulting in a needs_review verdict. Extracted — esomeprazole: CL/F 102 L/h, V/F 4.91e+03 L.
