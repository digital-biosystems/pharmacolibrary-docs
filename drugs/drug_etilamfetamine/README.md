**Description.** Etilamfetamine is also known as N-ethylamphetamine and is in the Canadian Legislation - The Controlled Drugs and Substances Act, and is a Schedule 1 controlled drug. Like other amphetamines, this amphetamine analog was used as an appetite suppressant in the 1950s. However, being on the controlled drug and substance act does not necessitate that the drug is approved for sale on the market.
