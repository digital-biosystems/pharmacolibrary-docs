# Roquefortine

- **generic name:** Roquefortine
- **T3DB:** [T3D3662](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3662) · **PubChem:** [CID 6436831](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/6436831)

## About

Roquefortine is found in milk and milk products. Roquefortine is a metabolite of Penicillium roquefortii, Penicillium commune, Penicillium cyclopium, Penicillium farinosum and many other Penicillium species Common constituent of blue cheese Roquefortine belongs to the family of Pyrroloindoles. These are compounds containing a pyrroloindole moiety, which is a tricyclic heterocycle which consists of a pyrrole ring fused to an indole.

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Fungal Toxin, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, dermal, inhalation, and parenteral (contaminated drugs).
- **Sources and uses:** Roquefortine C is a tremorgenic mycotoxin produced primarily by Penicillium roquefortii, but also by other Penicillium species. Penicillium roquefortii is used in the production of Roquefort and similar types of blue cheese, and has also been found growing in corn silage and mixed grains. …
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Tremorgenic mycotoxins exert their toxic effects by interfering with neurotransmitter release, possibly by causing degeneration of nerve terminals. They are thought to inhibit gamma-aminobutyric acid (GABA) receptors, both pre- and postsynaptic, as well as inhibit transmitter breakdown at the GABA-T receptors. …
- **Health effects:** Tremorgenic mycotoxins affect central nervous system activity. They cause a neurological disease of cattle known as "staggers syndrome". In severe cases roquefortine C can cause respiratory tract irritation, which can progress to ARDS/acute lung injury. …
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 15-20 mg/kg (Intraperitoneal, Mouse)
- **Treatment:** To control severe tremors caused by tremorgenic mycotoxins, methocarbamol should be administered. …
