# Tizanidine

- **generic name:** Tizanidine
- **T3DB:** [T3D2862](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2862) · **DrugBank:** [DB00697](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00697) · **PubChem:** [CID 5487](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/5487)
- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

Tizanidine is a short-acting drug for the management of spasticity. Tizanidine is an agonist at a2-adrenergic receptor sites and presumably reduces spasticity by increasing presynaptic inhibition of motor neurons. In animal models, tizanidine has no direct effect on skeletal muscle fibers or the neuromuscular junction, and no major effect on monosynaptic spinal reflexes. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral
- **Sources and uses:** For the management of increased muscle tone associated with spasticity
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Tizanidine reduces spasticity by increasing presynaptic inhibition of motor neurons through agonist action at a2-adrenergic receptor sites.
- **Health effects:** May cause a potentially dangerous rash that may develop into Stevens Johnson syndrome, an extremely rare but potentially fatal skin disease.
