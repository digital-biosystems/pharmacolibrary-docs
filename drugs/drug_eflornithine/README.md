- **groups:** approved, investigational, withdrawn

## About

Eflornithine is used to treat excessive facial hair growth in women (hirsutism) and, in combination therapy, the childhood cancer neuroblastoma; it has also been used against sleeping sickness. It remains authorised in the European Union for these uses, with a topical dermatological product for hirsutism and an oncology product for neuroblastoma, though some applications have been withdrawn.
