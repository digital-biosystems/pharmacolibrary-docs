## About

Lesinurad is a uricosuric agent that inhibits urate transporters to treat elevated blood uric acid levels in patients with gout. It is taken orally as tablets and has good gastrointestinal absorption. The drug was withdrawn from the United States market for business reasons and later removed from European markets.
