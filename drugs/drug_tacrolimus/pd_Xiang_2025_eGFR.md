## Record summary

**As extracted:** Tacrolimus (concentrations from this paper's PK model) drives estimated glomerular filtration rate (in mL/min/1.73 m2): direct Emax (saturable) effect.

**Model:** Model available in **Models**; browser simulation unavailable.

> Tacrolimus trough concentration (C0) directly inhibits estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) via a maximal inhibitory effect (Emax) model, described as eGFR(t) = eGFR0 - (Imax * C0^gamma) / (IC50^gamma + C0^gamma). The model assumes an IC50 of 10 ng/mL (based on literature regarding nephrotoxicity risk) and fixes Imax at 30 mL/min/1.73 m², with a baseline eGFR0 estimated at 52.3 mL/min/1.73 m².
