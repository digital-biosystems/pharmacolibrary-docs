# Strontium-90

- **generic name:** Strontium-90
- **T3DB:** [T3D0120](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D0120) · **PubChem:** [CID 5486204](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/5486204)

## About

Isotope of strontium.

<p><small>Source: <a href="https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q544529">Wikidata Q544529</a> · <a href="https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/">CC0</a></small></p>

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral
- **Sources and uses:** Strontium-90 is widely used in medicine and industry, as a radioactive source for thickness gauges, and for superficial radiotherapy of some cancers. Being cheaper than the alternative 238Pu, it is used as a heat source in many Russian and Soviet radioisotope thermoelectric generators. …
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** The fact that strontium is chemically similar to calcium allows it to exchange for calcium in bone. It affects bone development and strength by binding directly to hydroxyapatite crystals, which interferes with the normal crystalline structure of bone. …
- **Health effects:** High levels of radioactive strontium can damage bone marrow, cause anemia and prevent the blood from clotting properly. Strontium-90 present in bones could cause bone cancer, cancer of nearby tissues, and leukemia.
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 1, carcinogenic to humans.
- **Minimal risk levels (ATSDR):** Intermediate Oral: 2 mg/kg/day
