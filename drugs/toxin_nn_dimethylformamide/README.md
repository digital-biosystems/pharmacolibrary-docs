# N,N-Dimethylformamide

- **generic name:** N,N-Dimethylformamide
- **T3DB:** [T3D0252](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D0252) · **DrugBank:** [DB01844](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB01844) · **PubChem:** [CID 6228](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/6228)
- **groups:** experimental

## About

N,N-Dimethylformamide (DMF) is a clear liquid that has been widely used in industries as a solvent, an additive, or an intermediate because of its extensive miscibility with water and most common organic solvents. Its health effects include hepatotoxicity and male reproductoxicity, possibly linked with mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA) alterations including mtDNA common deletion (delta-mtDNA4977) and mtDNA copy number; during the biotransformation of DMF in the body, free radicals are formed, including hydroxyl radicals. The world-wide consumption of DMF in 2001 was approximately 285,000 metric tonnes and most of that was used as an industrial solvent. …

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Food Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Dimethyl formamide (DMF) is commonly used as a solvent for chemical reactions.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** While the mechanism of action of dimethyl formamide has not bee fully elucidated, thiocarbamate pesticides have been shown to inhibit aldehyde dehydrogenases.
- **Health effects:** Dimethyl formamide is a known hepatatotoxin. It may also cause birth defects, kidney damage, and temporary alcohol intolerance.
- **Lethal dose:** 10 grams for an adult human.
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 3, not classifiable as to its carcinogenicity to humans.
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 2800 mg/kg (Oral, Rat)
LD50: 1400 mg/kg (Intraperitoneal, Rat)
LD50: 3800 mg/kg (Subcutaneous, Rat)
LD50: 2000 mg/kg (Intravenous, Rat)
- **Treatment:** As there is no antidote for dimethyl formamide poisoning, treatment is mainly supportive and symptomatic.
- **Detected in:** urine
