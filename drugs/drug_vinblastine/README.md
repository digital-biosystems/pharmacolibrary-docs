## About

Vinblastine, a Vinca alkaloid, is a cancer medicine used against tumours such as Hodgkin's lymphoma, breast, testicular and kidney cancer, Kaposi's sarcoma, and also certain blood and immune disorders. It remains an approved medicine and is included on the WHO list of essential medicines, so it is widely used in cancer treatment worldwide.
