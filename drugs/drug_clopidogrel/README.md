# clopidogrel

- **generic name:** clopidogrel
- **ATC codes:** `B01AC04`
- **DrugBank:** [DB00758](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00758)
- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

**Description.** Clopidogrel is a prodrug of a platelet inhibitor used to reduce the risk of myocardial infarction and stroke.[A180508,L7213] Clopidogrel is indicated to reduce the risk of myocardial infarction for patients with non-ST elevated acute coronary syndrome (ACS), patients with ST-elevated myocardial infarction, and in recent MI, stroke, or established peripheral arterial disease,[L7213]

It has been shown to be superior to [aspirin] in reducing cardiovascular outcomes in patients with cardiovascular disease and provides additional benefit to patients with acute coronary syndromes already taking aspirin.[A180547]

Clopidogrel was granted FDA approval on 17 November 1997.[L7213]
