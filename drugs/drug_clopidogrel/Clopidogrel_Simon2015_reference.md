### Reviewer guidance

**Clopidogrel's apparent clearance was flagged as an unacceptable assumption despite an F=1 parameterization for metabolite clopi-H4.**

The record defines the metabolite's parameter as an apparent clearance ratio, dividing by the unidentifiable fraction converted from the parent. The model builder explicitly assumed a bioavailability of 1 and a fractional conversion of 1, which constitutes the flagged deviation. Additionally, the absorption lag time was defaulted rather than estimated from the data. Extracted — clopi-H4: kabs 1.04 1/h, V/F 1.04e+03 L, CLm/F 1.33 L/h.
