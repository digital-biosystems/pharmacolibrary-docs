## Record summary

**As extracted:** Rosuvastatin (the dose) drives LDL-C reduction (in %): direct sigmoid Emax (Hill) effect.

**Model:** Model and browser simulation available in **Models** and **Simulation**.

> The paper describes a direct dose-dependent Emax model where rosuvastatin dose (mg) inhibits LDL-C reduction (%), with a maximal effect (Emax) of 57.0% and a baseline placebo effect (E0) of -0.802%. The dose associated with 50% of the maximal effect (ED50) is 1.74 mg for Westerners, while the race covariate for Asians is 0.564, indicating a lower ED50 for this population.
