**The absorption rate constant is reported with a volume per time unit, and the abstract-only extraction prevents coherent parameter interpretation.**

The absorption rate constant for citalopram is assigned the unit L/h, which corresponds to volume flow rather than the required inverse time dimension for a rate constant. The record was constructed solely from the abstract, meaning the values are reported summary statistics rather than estimates from a fitted pharmacokinetic model. This lack of model fitting prevents verification of parameter coherence and contributes to the rejection of the record. Extracted — citalopram: kabs 0.64 L/h, CL/F 12.7 L/h, V/F 705 L.
