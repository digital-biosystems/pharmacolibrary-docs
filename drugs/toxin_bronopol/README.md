# Bronopol

- **generic name:** Bronopol
- **T3DB:** [T3D1724](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D1724) · **PubChem:** [CID 2450](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/2450)

## About

Bronopol is a chemical compound of bromine and an antimicrobial substance. It is used as a preservative in pharmaceuticals, as well as numerous consumer products such as shampoos and cosmetics. It is also used as an antimicrobial in many industrial environments such as paper mills, oil exploration and production facilities, as well as cooling water disinfection plants. …

- **Category:** Household Toxin, Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Bronopol is used as a preservative in pharmaceuticals, as well as numerous consumer products such as shampoos and cosmetics. It is also used as an antimicrobial in many industrial environments such as paper mills, oil exploration and production facilities, as well as cooling water disinfection plants.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** While Bronopol is not in itself a nitrosating agent, under conditions where it decomposes (alkaline solution and/or elevated temperatures) it can liberate nitrite and low levels of formaldehyde. These decomposition products can react with any secondary amines or amides (which may contaminate cosmetic products) to produce significant levels of nitrosamines, which are believed to be carcinogenic. …
- **Health effects:** Bronopol may react to produce nitrosamines, which are believed to be carcinogenic. It also releases formaldehyde, a known human carcinogen.
- **Carcinogenicity:** No indication of carcinogenicity (not listed by IARC).
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 250 mg/kg (Oral, Dog); LD50: 64-160 mg/kg (Dermal, Rat); LC50: >5 mg/L over 6 hours (Inhalation, Rat)
- **Treatment:** EYES: irrigate opened eyes for several minutes under running water. …
