# Dicamba

- **generic name:** Dicamba
- **T3DB:** [T3D0807](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D0807) · **PubChem:** none

## About

Dicamba is an herbicide used to control annual and perennial broadleaf weeds in grain crops and grasslands, and it is used to control brush and bracken in pastures. It will kill broadleaf weeds before and after they sprout. Legumes will be killed by dicamba. …

- **Category:** Pesticide, Household Toxin, Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Dicamba is an herbicide. Dicamba controls annual and perennial rose weeds in grain crops and highlands, and it is used to control brush and bracken in pastures, as well as legumes and cacti.
- **Health effects:** In oral, dermal, and inhaled routes of exposure, dicamba has a low acute toxicity. Dicamba may have irritating or corrosive effects on the skin and eyes. …
- **Acute toxicity:** oral LD50 in rats: 757 mg/kg body weight, dermal LD50 in rats: >2,000 mg/kg, inhalation LC50 in rats: >200 mg/L
- **Treatment:** Treatment may include washing any areas of contact, GI decontamination if swallowed, administering an IV and forced alkaline diuresis.
