## About

**Description.** Sodium phosphate is a saline laxative that is thought to work by increasing fluid in the small intestine. It usually results in a bowel movement after 30 minutes to 6 hours.

**Indication.** Used to treat constipation or to clean the bowel before a colonoscopy [FDA Label].
