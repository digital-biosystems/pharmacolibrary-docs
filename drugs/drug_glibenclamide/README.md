## About

Glibenclamide (glyburide) is a sulfonylurea anti-diabetic medicine used to lower blood sugar in diabetes, including a form of maturity-onset diabetes of the young. It is an approved medicine, appears on the WHO essential medicines list, and is authorised in the European Union for diabetes.
