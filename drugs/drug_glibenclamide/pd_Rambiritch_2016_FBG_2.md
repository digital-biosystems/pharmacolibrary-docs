## What this record describes

**As extracted:** Glibenclamide (concentrations from the PK model of Rambiritch_2016_2::final) drives fasting blood glucose (in mmol/L): direct Emax (saturable) effect.

**Model:** A simulatable model was generated — see the **Models** and **Simulation** tabs.

> Glibenclamide steady-state plasma concentration (Cpss) inhibits fasting blood glucose (FBG) via a direct inhibitory Emax model, where the effect is calculated as E0 × (1 – [Cpss × Emax] / [Cpss + EC50]). The model parameters are E0 = 14.30 mmol/L, Emax = 0.39, and EC50 = 85.20 mg/L.
>
