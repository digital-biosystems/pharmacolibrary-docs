### Reviewer guidance

**Model parameters for salicylic acid were missing, forcing default absorption rates that yielded incorrect peak concentrations and half-lives compared to the paper.**

The absorption rate constant was not reported in the source, so library defaults were substituted, resulting in a simulated peak concentration of 0.0183 versus the reported 0.0273. Additionally, the simulated terminal half-life of 2.39 hours deviated significantly from the reported value of 5.025 hours. These discrepancies stem from unverified assumptions in the absorption phase and apparent parameterization. Extracted — aspirin: t1/2z 5.03 h, V/F 0.256 L/kg, CL/F 0.075 L/h/kg.
