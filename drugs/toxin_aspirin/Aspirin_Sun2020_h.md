### Reviewer guidance

**The aspirin model is quarantined because volume of distribution, absorption rate constant, and lag time were left at library defaults instead of being estimated from data.**

The record for aspirin in rats lists apparent parameters for salicylic acid but lacks explicit values for aspirin's own pharmacokinetic parameters. The model builder used library placeholder values for aspirin's volume of distribution, absorption rate constant, and absorption lag time. Consequently, the model structure relied on invented absorption characteristics not reported in the source, leading to the decision to hold the model back. Extracted — aspirin: AUC 3.88e+05 μg/L*h, MRT 5.53 h, t1/2z 5.32 h, tmax 1.17 h, CLm,norm/F 0.07 L/h/kg, V/F 0.544 L/kg, Cmax 4.92e+04 μg/L.
