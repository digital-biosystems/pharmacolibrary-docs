## About

Dimethyl fumarate is an immunosuppressive drug used to treat multiple sclerosis, including relapsing-remitting and secondary progressive forms, and has also been used for psoriasis. It is an approved medicine, authorised in the European Union for multiple sclerosis and psoriasis, and is used fairly widely.
