## About

Alprazolam is a triazolobenzodiazepine tranquilizer used to treat anxiety disorders, specifically panic and generalized anxiety, as well as insomnia. It is generally taken orally in tablet or liquid forms, and is well-absorbed by the body. The drug is widely used and available as a generic medication.
