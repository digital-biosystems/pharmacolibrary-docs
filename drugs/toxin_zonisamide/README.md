# Zonisamide

- **generic name:** Zonisamide
- **T3DB:** [T3D2928](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2928) · **DrugBank:** [DB00909](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00909) · **PubChem:** [CID 5734](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/5734)
- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

Zonisamide is a sulfonamide anticonvulsant approved for use as an adjunctive therapy in adults with partial-onset seizures. Zonisamide may be a carbonic anhydrase inhibitor although this is not one of the primary mechanisms of action. Zonisamide may act by blocking repetitive firing of voltage-gated sodium channels leading to a reduction of T-type calcium channel currents, or by binding allosterically to GABA receptors. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral. Variable, yet relatively rapid rate of absorption with a time to peak concentration of 2.8-3.9 hours. Food has no effect on the bioavailability of zonisamide.
- **Sources and uses:** For use as adjunctive treatment of partial seizures in adults with epilepsy.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Zonisamide binds to sodium channels and voltage sensitive calcium channels, which suppresses neuronal depolarization and hypersynchronization. Zonisamide also inhibits carbonic anhydrase to a weaker extent, but such an effect is not thought to contribute substantially to the drug's anticonvulsant activity.
- **Health effects:** May cause a potentially dangerous rash that may develop into Stevens Johnson syndrome, an extremely rare but potentially fatal skin disease.
