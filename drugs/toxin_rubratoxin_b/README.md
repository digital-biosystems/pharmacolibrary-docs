# Rubratoxin B

- **generic name:** Rubratoxin B
- **T3DB:** [T3D3730](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3730) · **PubChem:** [CID 11969548](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/11969548)

## About

Rubratoxin B is a mycotoxin produced by fungi such as Penicillum rubrum and Penicillum purpurogenum, which are common soil fungi that sometimes contaminate animal feeds. Rubratoxin B has been shown to be hepatotoxic, nephrotoxic, and splenotoxic.

- **Category:** Fungal Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, dermal, inhalation, and parenteral (contaminated drugs).
- **Sources and uses:** Rubratoxin B is a mycotoxin produced by fungi such as Penicillum rubrum and Penicillum purpurogenum, which are common soil fungi that sometimes contaminate animal feeds.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Rubratoxin B is cytotoxic, hinders cell proliferation, and induces apoptosis. It also inhibits the activity of matrix metalloproteinase-2 and -9. …
- **Health effects:** Rubratoxin B is hepatotoxic, nephrotoxic, and splenotoxic, causing congestive, hemorrhagic and degenerative lesions.
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 0.22-0.43 mg/kg (Intraperitoneal, Mouse)
