# Clofenotane

- **generic name:** Clofenotane
- **T3DB:** [T3D0012](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D0012) · **PubChem:** [CID 3036](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/3036)
- **groups:** experimental

## About

Insecticide. Clofenotane is a major component of commercial DDT (other names *Gespan*, *Gesarol*, *Geverol*, *Chlorophenotane*). Use banned or discouraged in many countries Clofenotane belongs to the family of Diphenylmethanes. …

- **Category:** Cigarette Toxin, Pesticide, Pollutant, Food Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral
- **Sources and uses:** DDT is used as a pesticide and in disease vector control.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** DDT toxicity occurs via at least four mechanisms, possibly all functioning simultaneously. DDT reduces potassium transport across the membrane. …
- **Health effects:** Exposure to DDT causes loss of weight and anorexia. DDT poisoning affects CNS function in humans, but pathologic changes are observed in the liver and reproductive organs. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** DDT is possibly carcinogenic to humans (Group 2B).
- **Minimal risk levels (ATSDR):** Acute Oral: 0.0005 mg/kg/day
Intermediate Oral: 0.0005 mg/kg/day
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 87 mg/kg (Oral, Rat) 
LD50: 1931 mg/kg (Dermal, Rat) 
LD50: 1500 mg/kg (Subcutaneous, Rat)
