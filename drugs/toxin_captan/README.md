# Captan

- **generic name:** Captan
- **T3DB:** [T3D3804](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3804) · **PubChem:** [CID 8606](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/8606)

## About

Captan is the name of a general use pesticide (GUP) that belongs to the phthalimide class of fungicides. Though it can be applied on its own, Captan is often added as a component of other pesticide mixtures. It is used to control diseases on a number of fruits and vegetables as well as ornamental plants. …

- **Category:** Pesticide, Household Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Synthetic Toxin
- **Sources and uses:** This is a man-made compound that is used as a pesticide.
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 3, not classifiable as to its carcinogenicity to humans.
