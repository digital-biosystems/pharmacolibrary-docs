# Cesium-137

- **generic name:** Cesium-137
- **T3DB:** [T3D0213](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D0213) · **PubChem:** [CID 5486527](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/5486527)

## About

Chemical compound.

<p><small>Source: <a href="https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q27260451">Wikidata Q27260451</a> · <a href="https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/">CC0</a></small></p>

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation
- **Sources and uses:** Cesium-137 is produced from the detonation of nuclear weapons and is produced in nuclear power plants. Cesium-137 was released to the atmosphere most notably from the 1986 Chernobyl meltdown. …
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Highly penetrating gamma rays are the major cause of damage to tissues and internal organs following external overexposure to radioactive cesium. Once radioactive cesium is taken internally, cells of nearby tissues are at highest risk for damage due to the emission of beta particles. …
- **Health effects:** Cesium-137 presents external as well as internal health hazard, both from beta and gamma radiation. Cesium-137 is water-soluble and extremely toxic in minute amounts. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 1, carcinogenic to humans.
- **Minimal risk levels (ATSDR):** Acute Radiation: 4 mSv
Chronic Radiation: 1 mSv/yr
