# Clofibrate

- **generic name:** Clofibrate
- **T3DB:** [T3D4784](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4784) · **DrugBank:** [DB00636](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00636) · **PubChem:** [CID 2796](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/2796)
- **groups:** approved, withdrawn

## About

A fibric acid derivative used in the treatment of hyperlipoproteinemia type III and severe hypertriglyceridemia.

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** completely but slowly absorbed from the intestine. Between 95% and 99% of an oral dose of clofibrate is excreted in the urine as free and conjugated clofibric acid, thus, the absorption of clofibrate is virtually complete.
- **Sources and uses:** For Primary Dysbetalipoproteinemia (Type III hyperlipidemia) that does not respond adequately to diet. This helps control high cholesterol and high triglyceride levels.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Clofibrate increases the activity of extrahepatic lipoprotein lipase (LL), thereby increasing lipoprotein triglyceride lipolysis. Chylomicrons are degraded, VLDLs are converted to LDLs, and LDLs are converted to HDL. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 3, not classifiable as to its carcinogenicity to humans.
- **Acute toxicity:** Oral, mouse: LD<sub>50</sub> = 1220 mg/kg; Oral, rabbit: LD<sub>50</sub> = 1370 mg/kg; Oral, rat: LD<sub>50</sub> = 940 mg/kg.
