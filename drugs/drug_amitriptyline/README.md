- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

Amitriptyline is a tricyclic antidepressant prescribed for major depressive disorder and various pain conditions, including neuropathic pain, migraine, and fibromyalgia. It is widely used as a generic medication and is listed as an essential medicine by the World Health Organization.
