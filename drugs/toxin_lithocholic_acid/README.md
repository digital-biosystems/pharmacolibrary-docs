# Lithocholic acid

- **generic name:** Lithocholic acid
- **T3DB:** [T3D4963](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4963) · **PubChem:** [CID 11740284](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/11740284)

## About

Lithocholic acid is a bile acid formed from chenodeoxycholate by bacterial action, usually conjugated with glycine or taurine. It acts as a detergent to solubilize fats for absorption and is itself absorbed. It is used as cholagogue and choleretic. …

- **Category:** Natural Toxin, Animal Toxin
- **Health effects:** Chronically high levels of lithocholic acid are associated with several forms of cancer including colon cancer.
- **Carcinogenicity:** Not listed by IARC.
