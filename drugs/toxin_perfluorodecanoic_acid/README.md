## About

Perfluorodecanoic acid (PFDA) is a perfluoroalkyl acid (PFAA). PFAAs have been frequently detected in both the environment and in plants fish and animals. It is a breakdown product of stain- and grease-proof coatings on food packaging, couches, carpets. …
