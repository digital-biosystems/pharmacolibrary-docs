## About

Human tissue plasminogen activator, purified, glycosylated, 527 residues purified from CHO cells.

- **Category:** Protein, Natural Toxin, Animal Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral
- **Sources and uses:** For management of acute myocardial infarction, acute ischemic strok and for lysis of acute pulmonary emboli
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Alteplase binds to fibrin rich clots via the fibronectin finger-like domain and the Kringle 2 domain. The protease domain then cleaves the Arg/Val bond in plasminogen to form plasmin. …
