## Record summary

**As extracted:** Cetuximab (concentrations from the PK model of Augustin_2026) drives cell counts: direct Emax (saturable) effect.

**Model:** Model and browser simulation available in **Models** and **Simulation**.

> Cetuximab concentrations (ranging up to 0.1 µg/mL) inhibit cell counts via a direct Emax mechanism, as described in the paper, which explicitly notes that an empirical model was used because it was not feasible to develop a receptor occupancy model without multiple concentrations. The paper does not state the Imax or IC50 values for cetuximab alone, noting that the effects were modeled concurrently with tetrac.
