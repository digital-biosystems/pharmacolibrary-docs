# Strontium nitrate

- **generic name:** Strontium nitrate
- **T3DB:** [T3D3575](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3575) · **PubChem:** [CID 24848](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/24848)

## About

Strontium nitrate is an inorganic compound composed of the elements strontium, nitrogen and oxygen with the formula Sr(NO3)2. This colorless solid is used as a red colorant and oxidizer in pyrotechnics.

<p><small>Source: <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strontium_nitrate?oldid=1380033009">Wikipedia</a> · <a href="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/">CC BY-SA 4.0</a></small></p>

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation
- **Sources and uses:** Strontium nitrate is used as an oxidizer and colorant in fireworks.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Nitrate's toxicity is a result of it's conversion to nitrite once in the body. Nitrite causes the autocatalytic oxidation of oxyhemoglobin to hydrogen peroxide and methemoglobin. …
- **Health effects:** Nitrate and nitrite poisoning causes methemoglobinemia. Nitrites may cause pregnancy complications and developmental effects. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** Ingested nitrate or nitrite under conditions that result in endogenous nitrosation is probably carcinogenic to humans (Group 2A).
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 540 mg/kg (Intraperitoneal, Rat)
