### Reviewer guidance

**The record was rejected because cardiac output was recorded as intrinsic clearance, creating a dimensional conflict with flow parameters.**

Cardiac output measurements (4.78 L/min) were extracted as intrinsic clearance (CLint), which conflicts with the expected units for metabolic capacity. This dimensional mismatch contrasts with the reported sum of compartmental blood flows (4.62 L/min) and intercompartmental clearances (5.1 L/min). Additionally, the model structure is limited as it relies on abstract-only summary statistics rather than a fitted model.
