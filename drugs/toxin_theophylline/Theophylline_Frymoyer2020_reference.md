### Reviewer guidance

**The record was refused because the tissue-to-plasma partition coefficient for theophylline has a dimensionally invalid unit of L/h instead of being dimensionless.**

The tissue-to-plasma partition coefficient was recorded with a value of 0.68 and the unit L/h, which is physically inconsistent as partition coefficients are dimensionless ratios. This dimensional conflict prevented the unit conversion to SI, leaving the parameter without a valid standard value. Additionally, the record lists abstract-derived summary statistics, such as a clearance of 0.049 L/h and volume of 2.7 L, rather than fitted model parameters. Extracted — theophylline: V3 2.7 L, Kp 0.68 L/H, t1/2z 39.2 hours, kabs 1.51 h⁻¹, tlag 0.825 h.
