### Reviewer guidance

**Theophylline record refused because bioavailability (F) was unreported and defaulted, invalidating the V/F estimate of 1.29 L/kg.**

The bioavailability parameter (F) lacks a value from the source text and relies on library defaults rather than an estimate from Kwack_2026. Consequently, the volume of distribution adjusted for bioavailability (V/F) of 1.29 L/kg is not supported by the paper's data. This discrepancy renders the simulated exposure profile for theophylline unreliable, as the scaling factor remains unjustified. Extracted — theophylline: CL 0.133 L/h, V/F 1.29 L/kg, V2 13.2 L, Q 6.85 L/h, tlag 0.825 h, kabs 0.545 h−1.
