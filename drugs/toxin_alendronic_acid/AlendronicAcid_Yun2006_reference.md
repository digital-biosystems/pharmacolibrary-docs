### Reviewer guidance

**The clearance of 0.59 ml/h is physiologically implausible compared to the 2061.3 L volume, indicating a likely unit or scale extraction error for alendronate.**

The record reports alendronate clearance as 0.59 ml/h, a value far below physiological expectations for an oral drug, especially when paired with a volume of distribution of 2061.3 L. This discrepancy triggered a rejection due to an implausible clearance-to-volume magnitude. Additionally, the model relied solely on abstract-derived summary statistics rather than a fitted population model, limiting its quantitative rigor. Extracted — alendronic acid: tmax 1.77 h, V/F 2.06e+03 L, CL/F 0.59 ml/h.
