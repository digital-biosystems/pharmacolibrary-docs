### Reviewer guidance

**Clearance and volume values for alendronic acid, 0.59 and 2.99 respectively, are unitly misassigned in ml/h, causing implausible physiological magnitudes.**

The clearance parameter CL/F has a value of 0.59 and the volume parameter V has a value of 2.99. Both parameters are recorded with the unit ml/h, which is dimensionally incorrect for clearance and volume. This unit assignment error results in physiologically implausible values that violate physiological windows, leading to the model's rejection. Extracted — alendronic acid: CL/F 0.59 ml/h, V 2.99 ml/h.
