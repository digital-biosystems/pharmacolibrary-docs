# Dicofol

- **generic name:** Dicofol
- **T3DB:** [T3D0144](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D0144) · **PubChem:** [CID 8268](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/8268)

## About

Dicofol is an organochlorine pesticide that is chemically related to DDT. Specifically, it is used as a miticide against the red spider mite. Though difocol is not extremely toxic, its use is controverial because one of the intermediates used in its production is DDT. …

- **Category:** Pesticide, Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal.
- **Sources and uses:** Dicofol is used as a pesticide on agricultural crops and ornamental plants, as well as for agricultural and domestic buildings for mite control.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Like other organochloride pesticides, dicofol is believed to act by at least four mechanisms, possibly all functioning simultaneously. It may reduce potassium transport across the membrane. …
- **Health effects:** Dicofol poisoning affects the liver, kidneys and the central nervous system. Very severe cases may result in convulsions, coma, or death from respiratory failure. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 3, not classifiable as to its carcinogenicity to humans.
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 595 mg/kg (Oral, Rat)
LD50: 2100 mg/kg (Percutaneous, Rabbit)
- **Treatment:** Treatment of dicofol poisoning is mainly symptomatic. …
