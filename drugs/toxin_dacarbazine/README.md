# Dacarbazine

- **generic name:** Dacarbazine
- **T3DB:** [T3D4689](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4689) · **DrugBank:** [DB00851](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00851) · **PubChem:** none
- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

Dacarbazine is only found in individuals that have used or taken this drug. It is an antineoplastic agent. It has significant activity against melanomas. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** erratic, slow and incomplete.
- **Sources and uses:** Dacarbazine is used in the treatment of various cancers, among them malignant melanoma, Hodgkin lymphoma, sarcoma, and islet cell carcinoma of the pancreas. As of mid-2006, dacarbazine is commonly used as a single agent in the treatment of metastatic melanoma, and as part of the ABVD chemotherapy regimen to treat Hodgkin lymphoma, and in the MAID regimen for sarcoma. (Wikipedia)
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** The mechanism of action is not known, but appears to exert cytotoxic effects via its action as an alkylating agent. Other theories include DNA synthesis inhibition by its action as a purine analog, and interaction with SH groups. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 2B, possibly carcinogenic to humans.
- **Acute toxicity:** LD<sub>50</sub>=350mg/kg (orally in mice)
