# Perfringolysin O

- **generic name:** Perfringolysin O
- **T3DB:** [T3D2601](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2601) · **PubChem:** none

## About

Perfringolysin O (PFO) or theta-toxin is produced by Clostridium perfringens. It is a member of the cholesterol-dependent cytolysin family.

- **Category:** Protein, Bacterial Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** ingestion, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Perfringolysin O (PFO) is produced by Clostridium perfringens.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Perfringolysin O binds to cholesterol on eukaryotic cell membranes, then forms oligomeric pores. This causes membrane damage and lysis of the cell.
- **Health effects:** Perfringolysin O (PFO) is produced by Clostridium perfringens. Clostridium perfringens can cause infections producing tissue necrosis and clostridial myonecrosis. …
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 13-16 ug/mg (Intravenous, Mouse)
