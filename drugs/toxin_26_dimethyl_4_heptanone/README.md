# 2,6-Dimethyl-4-heptanone

- **generic name:** 2,6-Dimethyl-4-heptanone
- **T3DB:** [T3D4884](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4884) · **PubChem:** [CID 7958](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/7958)

## About

2,6-Dimethyl-4-heptanone is a flavouring ingredient belonging to the family of Ketones. These are organic compounds in which a carbonyl group is bonded to two carbon atoms R2C=O (neither R may be H).

- **Category:** Household Toxin, Food Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
