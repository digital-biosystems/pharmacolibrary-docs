### Reviewer guidance

**The model outputs parent dexamethasone rather than the measured 6beta-hydroxydexamethasone, and lacks the metabolite compartment required for the parent-metabolite topology.**

The output variable is the parent drug concentration, conflicting with the recorded measured analyte of Dexamethasone which requires the metabolite compartment for the 6beta-hydroxydexamethasone pathway. The implementation is a single-compartment oral model, structurally incompatible with the parent-metabolite relationship defined by the formation clearance to 6beta-hydroxydexamethasone. This structural failure stems from abstract-only extraction, where summary statistics like 26 L/h/70 kg clearance substituted for a fitted mechanistic model. Extracted — dexamethasone: CL 26 L/h/70 kg, V/F 123 L/70 kg, t1/2z 3.3 h, CL 50 L/h/70 kg, kabs 0.773 day -1, tlag 0.29 h.
