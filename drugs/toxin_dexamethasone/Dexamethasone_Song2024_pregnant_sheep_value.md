### Reviewer guidance

**The intercompartmental clearance parameter k12 was left at library defaults rather than estimated, compromising the physiological validity of the dexamethasone two-compartment model.**

The review flagged that k12 and Tlag were not extracted from Song_2024, leading to the use of generic software placeholders instead of study-specific values. While CL/F, V1/F, and V2/F were successfully quantified for dexamethasone, the reliance on default distribution kinetics between compartments creates an unsupported structure. Additionally, the model builder assumed full bioavailability (F=1) without explicit justification, further limiting the parameter estimates' reliability.
