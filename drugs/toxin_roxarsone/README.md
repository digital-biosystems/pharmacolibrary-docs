# Roxarsone

- **generic name:** Roxarsone
- **T3DB:** [T3D0311](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D0311) · **PubChem:** [CID 5104](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/5104)
- **groups:** experimental, vet_approved

## About

Roxarsone is used to control enteric infections in chicken and to improve growth and feed efficiency. Roxarsone is a fda approved additive for chicken, turkey and swine feeds.

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Food Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** 3-nitro-4-hydroxy-phenyl-arsonic acid is found in poultry feed.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Arsenic and its metabolites disrupt ATP production through several mechanisms. At the level of the citric acid cycle, arsenic inhibits pyruvate dehydrogenase and by competing with phosphate it uncouples oxidative phosphorylation, thus inhibiting energy-linked reduction of NAD+, mitochondrial respiration, and ATP synthesis. …
- **Health effects:** Hypovolemia from capillary leakage (third-spacing of fluids) is a common, serious, early effect. Muscle cramps, facial edema, bronchitis, dyspnea, chest pain, dehydration, intense thirst, and fluid-electrolyte disturbances are also common following significant exposures. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 3, not classifiable as to its carcinogenicity to humans.
- **Minimal risk levels (ATSDR):** Acute Oral: 0.005 mg/kg/day
Chronic Oral: 0.0003 mg/kg/day
Chronic Inhalation: 0.01 mg/m3
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 81 mg/kg (Oral, Rat)
- **Treatment:** Arsenic poisoning can be treated by chelation therapy, using chelating agents such as dimercaprol, EDTA or DMSA. …
