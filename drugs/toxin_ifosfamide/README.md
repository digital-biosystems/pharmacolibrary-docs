# Ifosfamide

- **generic name:** Ifosfamide
- **T3DB:** [T3D3513](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3513) · **DrugBank:** [DB01181](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB01181) · **PubChem:** [CID 3690](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/3690)
- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

Ifosfamide is a chemotherapeutic agent chemically related to the nitrogen mustards and a synthetic analog of cyclophosphamide. It is active as an alkylating agent and an immunosuppresive agent. The exact mechanism of ifosfamide has not been determined, but appears to be similar to other alkylating agents. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** intravenous injection
- **Sources and uses:** Used as a component of various chemotherapeutic regimens as third-line therapy for recurrent or refractory germ cell testicular cancer. Also used as a component of various chemotherapeutic regimens for the treatment of cervical cancer, as well as in conjunction with surgery and/or radiation therapy in the treatment of various soft tissue sarcomas. …
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** After metabolic activation, active metabolites of ifosfamide alkylate or bind with many intracellular molecular structures, including nucleic acids. The cytotoxic action is primarily due to cross-linking of strands of DNA and RNA, as well as inhibition of protein synthesis.
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 3, not classifiable as to its carcinogenicity to humans.
- **Acute toxicity:** LD<sub>50</sub> (mouse) = 390-1005 mg/kg, LD<sub>50</sub> (rat) = 150-190 mg/kg.
