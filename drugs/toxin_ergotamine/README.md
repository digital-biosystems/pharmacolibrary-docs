# Ergotamine

- **generic name:** Ergotamine
- **T3DB:** [T3D2460](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2460) · **DrugBank:** [DB00696](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00696) · **PubChem:** [CID 8223](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/8223)
- **groups:** approved

## About

Ergotamine is only found in individuals that have used or taken this drug. It is a vasoconstrictor found in ergot of Central Europe. It is an alpha-1 selective adrenergic agonist and is commonly used in the treatment of migraine disorders. …

- **Category:** Drug, Fungal Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, dermal, inhalation, and parenteral (contaminated drugs).
The bioavailability of sublingually administered ergotamine has not been determined.
- **Sources and uses:** For use as therapy to abort or prevent vascular headache, e.g., migraine, migraine variants, or so called "histaminic cephalalgia". Ergotamine is a secondary metabolite (natural product) and principal alkaloid of the ergot fungus, Claviceps purpurea, and related fungi in the family Clavicipitaceae. It is used medicinally for treatment of acute migraine attacks (sometimes in combination with caffeine), and to induce childbirth and prevent post-partum haemorrhage.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Ergoline alkaloids tend to act as a group, producing complex and variable effects of partial agonism or antagonism at adrenergic, dopaminergic, and serotonergic receptors. Variables relating to these effects are influenced by the agent, dosage, species, tissue, physiological, and endocrinological state, and experimental conditions. …
- **Health effects:** Vasoconstrictive complications of a serious nature may occur at times. These include ischemia, cyanosis, absence of pulse, cold extremities, gangrene, precordial distress and pain, EKG changes and muscle pains. …
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 62 mg/kg (Intravenous, Rat)
- **Treatment:** Treatment consists of removal of the offending drug. …
