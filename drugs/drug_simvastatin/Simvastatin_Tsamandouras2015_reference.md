### Reviewer guidance

**The record was rejected because the tissue-to-plasma partition coefficients Kp for simvastatin (35.19) and simvastatin acid (61.00) carry the unit 'a', which could not be converted to SI, giving a dimension mismatch on a structural parameter.**

The Kp parameters, defined as tissue-to-plasma partition coefficients of PBPK compartments, were reported with the unit 'a' for both simvastatin and its metabolite simvastatin acid; this unit is dimensionless in pharmacokinetic terms but was not recognized, so the parameters arrived without SI values. The dimension check on this structural parameter failed, and the model was held back. Other parameters, including the CYP3A intrinsic clearances (14.208 µL/min/pmol CYP3A for simvastatin, 0.0504 mL/min/mg of MP for simvastatin acid) and the simvastatin acid active-uptake clearance of 8001.1 L/h, were extracted with their reported units. Extracted — simvastatin: CLint 14.2 µL/min/pmol CYP3A, Kp 35.2 a; simvastatin acid: CLint 0.0504 mL/min/mg of MP, Kp 61 a, CL 8e+03 L/h.
