# Acetaldehyde

- **generic name:** Acetaldehyde
- **T3DB:** [T3D3471](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3471) · **PubChem:** [CID 177](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/177)

## About

Acetaldehyde is a colorless, flammable liquid used in the manufacture of acetic acid, perfumes, and flavors. In the chemical industry, acetaldehyde is used as an intermediate in the production of acetic acid, certain esters, and a number of other chemicals. it is also an air pollutant resulting from combustion, such as automotive exhaust and tobacco smoke. It is also an intermediate in the metabolism of alcohol. …

- **Category:** Cigarette Toxin, Household Toxin, Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin, Plant Toxin
- **Sources and uses:** Acetaldehyde occurs naturally in ripe fruit, coffee, and bread, and is produced by plants as part of their normal metabolism. It is popularly known as a chemical that causes hangovers, as it is produced from the breakdown of ethanol. …
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Acetaldehyde can form adducts with DNA, causing damage such as cross-links.
- **Health effects:** Acetaldehyde is a probable carcinogen.
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 2B, possibly carcinogenic to humans. …
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 661 mg/kg (Oral, Rat); LD50: 212 mg/kg (Intravenous, Mouse); LD50: 3540 mg/kg (Dermal, Rabbit)
