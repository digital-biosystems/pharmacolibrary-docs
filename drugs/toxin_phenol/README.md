# Phenol

- **generic name:** Phenol
- **T3DB:** [T3D0182](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D0182) · **DrugBank:** [DB03255](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB03255) · **PubChem:** [CID 996](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/996)
- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

Phenol, is a toxic, colourless crystalline solid with a sweet tarry odor that resembles a hospital smell. It is commonly used as an antiseptic and disinfectant. It is active against a wide range of micro-organisms including some fungi and viruses, but is only slowly effective against spores. …

- **Category:** Drug, Household Toxin, Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** inhalation, oral, dermal, eye contact
- **Sources and uses:** Phenol is used to make plastics. Phenol is also used as a disinfectant in household cleaning products and in consumer products such as mouthwashes, gargles, throat sprays. …
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Phenol is irritating and corrosive at high concentrations. Phenol impairs the stratum corneum and produces coagulation necrosis by denaturing and precipitating proteins. …
- **Health effects:** Long-term exposure to phenol at work has been associated with cardiovascular disease, irritation of the respiratory tract and muscle twitching depedning of the route of exposure. Ingestion of liquid products containing concentrated phenol can cause serious gastrointestinal damage and even death. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 3, not classifiable as to its carcinogenicity to humans.
- **Minimal risk levels (ATSDR):** Acute Oral: 1 mg/kg/day (Rat)
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 400 mg/kg/day (Oral, Rat) 
LD50: 669 mg/cm2/day (Dermal, Rat) 
LD50: 1400 mg/cm2/day (Dermal, Rabbit)
- **Detected in:** blood, feces, urine
