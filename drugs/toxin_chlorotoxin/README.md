# Chlorotoxin

- **generic name:** Chlorotoxin
- **T3DB:** [T3D2508](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2508) · **PubChem:** none
- **groups:** investigational

## About

Chlorotoxin is a peptide toxin produced by the Egyptian scorpion (Leiurus quinquestriatus quinquestriatus). It blocks small-conductance chloride channels. The fact that Chlorotoxin binds preferentially to glioma cells compared with non-neoplastic cells or normal brain cells has allowed the development of new methods for the treatment and diagnosis of several types of cancer.

- **Category:** Protein, Natural Toxin, Animal Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** injection (sting/bite)
- **Sources and uses:** Chlorotoxin is a peptide toxin produced by the Egyptian scorpion (Leiurus quinquestriatus quinquestriatus). The fact that Chlorotoxin binds preferentially to glioma cells compared with non-neoplastic cells or normal brain cells has allowed the development of new methods for the treatment and diagnosis of several types of cancer.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Chlorotoxin blocks small conductance chloride channels.
- **Health effects:** Chlorotoxin has been shown to cause paralysis in prey animals of the Egyptian scorpion.
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 0.25 mg/kg (Subcutaneous, Mouse)
