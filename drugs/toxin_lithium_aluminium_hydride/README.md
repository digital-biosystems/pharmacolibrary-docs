# Lithium aluminium hydride

- **generic name:** Lithium aluminium hydride
- **T3DB:** [T3D1503](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D1503) · **PubChem:** [CID 28112](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/28112)

## About

Lithium aluminium hydride (LAH) is a hydride of lithium and aluminum. It is is a reducing agent used in organic synthesis. Aluminum is the most abundant metal in the earth's crust and is always found combined with other elements such as oxygen, silicon, and fluorine.

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** The main target organs of aluminum are the central nervous system and bone. Aluminum binds with dietary phosphorus and impairs gastrointestinal absorption of phosphorus. …
- **Health effects:** Aluminum targets the nervous system and causes decreased nervous system performance and is associated with altered function of the blood-brain barrier. The accumulation of aluminum in the body may cause bone or brain diseases. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** Not listed by IARC. …
- **Minimal risk levels (ATSDR):** Intermediate Oral: 1.0 mg/kg/day 
Chronic Oral: 1.0 mg/kg/day
- **Treatment:** EYES: irrigate opened eyes for several minutes under running water. …
- **Detected in:** blood, cerebrospinal fluid, saliva, urine
