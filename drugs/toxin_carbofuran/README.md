# Carbofuran

- **generic name:** Carbofuran
- **T3DB:** [T3D0944](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D0944) · **PubChem:** [CID 2566](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/2566)

## About

Carbofuran is a carbamate pesticide. It is used as systemic agricultural insecticide, acaricide and nematocide. Carbofuran has been shown to exhibit neuroprotectant and neuroprotective functions. …

- **Category:** Pesticide, Food Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** inhalation, oral, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Carbofuran is widely used as an insecticide or pesticide in homes, gardens and agricultural applications. It is a synthetic compound.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Carbofuran is a cholinesterase or acetylcholinesterase (AChE) inhibitor. Carbamates form unstable complexes with chlolinesterases by carbamoylation of the active sites of the enzymes. …
- **Health effects:** Acute exposure to cholinesterase inhibitors can cause a cholinergic crisis characterized by severe nausea/vomiting, salivation, sweating, bradycardia, hypotension, collapse, and convulsions. Increasing muscle weakness is a possibility and may result in death if respiratory muscles are involved. …
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 5 mg/kg (Oral, Rat) 
LD50: 120 mg/kg (Dermal, Rat) 
LD50: 450 ug/kg (Intravenous, Mouse)
- **Treatment:** If the compound has been ingested, rapid gastric lavage should be performed using 5% sodium bicarbonate. …
