## About

Allopurinol is a xanthine oxidase inhibitor used to treat gout, acute gout attacks, and high blood uric acid levels. It is taken by mouth in tablet or capsule form, or intravenously. It is widely used as a generic medication and is on the WHO list of essential medicines.
