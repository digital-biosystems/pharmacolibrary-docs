## About

Mebendazole is a benzimidazole antiparasitic used to treat worm infections such as ascariasis, pinworms, and hookworms. It is given by mouth in forms such as tablets or suspensions, though it is poorly absorbed from the gut. It is widely used as a generic medication and is included in the World Health Organization's essential medicines list.
