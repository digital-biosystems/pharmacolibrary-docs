## About

Mitomycin is a cytotoxic antibiotic chemotherapy drug used to treat various cancers, including bladder cancer, and glaucoma. It is administered intravenously or intravesically, typically as an injection or solution prepared from powder. The drug is approved for use and also has investigational applications.
