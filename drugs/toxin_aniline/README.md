# Aniline

- **generic name:** Aniline
- **T3DB:** [T3D4984](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4984) · **DrugBank:** [DB06728](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB06728) · **PubChem:** [CID 6115](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/6115)
- **groups:** investigational

## About

Aniline is an organic chemical compound, specifically a primary aromatic amine. It consists of a benzene ring attached to an amino group. Aniline is oily and, although colorless, it can be slowly oxidized and resinified in air to form impurities which can give it a red-brown tint. …

- **Category:** Synthetic Toxin, Industrial/Workplace Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** toxic by ingestion and a skin and eye irritant.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Aniline induces lipid peroxidation and protein oxidation in the spleen and that oxidative stress plays a role in the splenic toxicity of aniline. The hematopoietic system is the primary target of aniline insult in rats which is characterized by methemoglobinemia, hemolysis, and hemolytic anemia and by the development of splenic hyperplasia, siderosis, fibrosis, a variety of sarcomas, and, most commonly, fibrosarcomas on prolonged exposure. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 3, not classifiable as to its carcinogenicity to humans.
- **Detected in:** urine
