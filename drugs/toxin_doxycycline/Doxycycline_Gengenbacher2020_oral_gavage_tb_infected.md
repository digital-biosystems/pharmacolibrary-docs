### Reviewer guidance

**The doxycycline record was rejected because the AUC unit ng/h/ml represents mass/time/volume, conflicting with the expected area-under-curve dimension of mass*time/volume.**

The reported AUC value of 360 uses the unit ng/h/ml, which implies a rate of mass per volume per time rather than the integral of concentration over time. This dimensional inconsistency prevents the correct calculation of exposure metrics. Additionally, the unit could not be translated into standard SI units, leaving the parameter unusable for model construction. Extracted — doxycycline: AUCt 360 ng/h/ml, Cavg 15 ng/ml, Fab 0.7 %, V 1 liter/kg, CL 26 ml/min, kabs 10.3 h−1.
