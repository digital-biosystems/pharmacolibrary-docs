### Reviewer guidance

**Bioavailability and lag time were defaulted rather than estimated for doxycycline, while clearance, volume, and absorption were extracted from abstract statistics.**

Bioavailability (F) and lag time (Tlag) were substituted with library defaults because they were not estimated in the abstract-derived record. This violates the requirement that all parameters be supported by explicit estimates or justified conventions. Clearance, volume of distribution, and absorption rate constant were successfully extracted for doxycycline. The model's validity is compromised by the lack of specific bioavailability and lag time data for this population. Extracted — doxycycline: CL 3.32 L/h/70 kg, V 96.8 L/70kg, kabs 10.3 h−1.
