# Allyl isothiocyanate

- **generic name:** Allyl isothiocyanate
- **T3DB:** [T3D0778](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D0778) · **PubChem:** [CID 5971](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/5971)

## About

Allyl isothiocyanate is a volatile organic compound. Allyl isothiocyanate (AITC) is a constituent of mustard, horseradish and wasabi and certain vegetables found in the human diet, mostly in cruciferous vegetables. AITC is a colorless to pale yellow liquid that is slightly soluble in water, but well soluble in most organic solvents. …

- **Category:** Household Toxin, Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Synthetic Toxin, Food Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** inhalation, oral, dermal2)
- **Sources and uses:** Synthetic allyl isothiocyanate is used as an insecticide, bacteriocide, and nematocide, and is used in certain cases for crop protection.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Cyanide is an inhibitor of cytochrome c oxidase in the fourth complex of the electron transport chain (found in the membrane of the mitochondria of eukaryotic cells). It complexes with the ferric iron atom in this enzyme. …
- **Health effects:** Exposure to high levels of cyanide for a short time harms the brain and heart and can even cause coma, seizures, apnea, cardiac arrest and death. Chronic inhalation of cyanide causes breathing difficulties, chest pain, vomiting, blood changes, headaches, and enlargement of the thyroid gland. …
- **Lethal dose:** 200 to 300 milligrams for an adult human (cyanide salts).
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 3, not classifiable as to its carcinogenicity to humans.
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 112 mg/kg (Oral, Rat)
LD50: 88 mg/kg (Dermal, Rabbit
LD50: 12 mg/kg (Intravenous, Rabbit)
- **Treatment:** EYES: irrigate opened eyes for several minutes under running water. …
- **Detected in:** blood
