## About

Didanosine is a nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitor used to treat HIV infection as part of combination therapy. It is taken by mouth in various solid dosage forms and is well absorbed from the gut. The drug is used worldwide and is listed on the World Health Organization's Model List of Essential Medicines.
