# Pramipexole

- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

Pramipexole is a medication indicated for treating Parkinson's disease and restless legs syndrome (RLS). It is also sometimes used off-label as a treatment for cluster headache or to counteract the problems with low libido experienced by some users of SSRI antidepressant drugs. Pramipexole has shown robust effects on pilot studies in bipolar disorder. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, rapid. Absolute bioavailability is greater than 90%, indicating that pramipexole is well absorbed and undergoes little presystemic metabolism. Food does not affect the extent of absorption.
- **Sources and uses:** For the treatment of signs and symptoms of idiopathic Parkinson's disease
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** The precise mechanism of action of Pramipexole as a treatment for Parkinson's disease is unknown, although it is believed to be related to its ability to stimulate dopamine receptors in the striatum.
