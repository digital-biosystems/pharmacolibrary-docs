# Lolitrem B

- **generic name:** Lolitrem B
- **T3DB:** [T3D3767](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3767) · **PubChem:** [CID 3086140](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/3086140)

## About

Paspalinine is a tremorgenic mycotoxin that is produced by the fungi Neotyphodium lolii and Acremonium lolii. It may be found in contaminated cereal crops such as oats, barley, millet, corn and rice. Tremorgenic mycotoxins affect central nervous system activity and have been implicated in a number of neurologic diseases of cattle collectively known as "staggers syndromes" or “ryegrass staggers”.

- **Category:** Fungal Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, dermal, inhalation, and parenteral (contaminated drugs).
- **Sources and uses:** Lolitrem B is a tremorgenic mycotoxin that is produced by the fungi Neotyphodium lolii and Acremonium lolii. It may be found in contaminated cereal crops such as oats, barley, millet, corn and rice.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** The neurological effects of Lolitrem B are a result of its ability to inhibit large conductance calcium-activated potassium channels (BK channels). Lolitrem B binds to the alpha subunit of the BK channel, inhibiting channel potassium currents. …
- **Health effects:** Tremorgenic mycotoxins affect central nervous system activity. They cause a neurological disease of cattle known as "staggers syndrome" or “ryegrass staggers”.
- **Minimal risk levels (ATSDR):** Symptoms of "ryegrass staggers" develop in livestock when concentrations exceed 2.5 ug/g in dry matter.
- **Treatment:** To control severe tremors caused by tremorgenic mycotoxins, methocarbamol should be administered. …
