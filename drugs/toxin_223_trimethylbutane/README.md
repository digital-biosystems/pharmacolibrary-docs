# 2,2,3-Trimethylbutane

- **generic name:** 2,2,3-Trimethylbutane
- **T3DB:** [T3D3439](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3439) · **PubChem:** [CID 10044](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/10044)

## About

2,2,3-Trimethylbutane is a hydrocarbon and one of the isomers of heptane. Heptanes may be found in gasoline and are widely used as solvents. They are also sold as fuel for outdoor stoves.

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Heptanes may be found in gasoline and are widely used as solvents. They are also sold as fuel for outdoor stoves.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Petroleum distillates are central nervous system depressants and cause pulmonary damage.
- **Health effects:** Petroleum distillates are aspiration hazards and may cause pulmonary damage, central nervous system depression, and cardiac effects such as cardiac arrhythmias. They may also affect the blood, immune system, liver, and kidney.
- **Carcinogenicity:** 2,2,3-Trimethylbutane is found in gasoline, which is possibly carcinogenic to humans (Group 2B).
- **Treatment:** Treatment is mainly symptomatic and supportive. …
