# Polyethylene terephthalate

- **generic name:** Polyethylene terephthalate
- **T3DB:** [T3D3659](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3659) · **PubChem:** [CID 16212789](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/16212789)

## About

Polyethylene terephthalate (PET or PETE), is a thermoplastic polymer resin of the polyester family and is used in synthetic fibers; beverage, food and other liquid containers; thermoforming applications; and engineering resins often in combination with glass fiber. The majority of the world's PET production is for synthetic fibers (in excess of 60%) with bottle production accounting for around 30% of global demand. Some of the trade names of PET products are Dacron, Diolen, Tergal, Terylene, and Trevira fibers, Cleartuf, Eastman PET and Polyclear bottle resins, Hostaphan, Melinex, and Mylar films, and Arnite, Ertalyte, Impet, Rynite and Valox injection molding resins. …

- **Category:** Household Toxin, Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Food Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** inhalation, oral, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Polyethylene terephthalate is used in synthetic fibers; beverage, food and other liquid containers; thermoforming applications; and engineering resins often in combination with glass fiber.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Antimony is a catalyst that is often used as antimony trioxide or antimony triacetate in the production of PET. It remains in the material and can thus in principle migrate out into food and drinks, though the concentration is usually quite low. …
- **Health effects:** Antimony is a catalyst that is often used as antimony trioxide or antimony triacetate in the production of PET. It remains in the material and can thus in principle migrate out into food and drinks, though the concentration is usually quite low. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** Subcutaneous administration of polyethylene terephthalate is not classifiable as to its carcinogenicity to humans (Group 3).
- **Treatment:** Following oral exposure to antimony, administer charcoal as a slurry (240 mL water/30 g charcoal). …
