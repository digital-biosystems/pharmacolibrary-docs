### Reviewer guidance

**The model was rejected because the metabolite was assigned to the central compartment, forcing apparent parameter assumptions and causing a topology mismatch.**

O-demethyltramadol is assigned to the central compartment (n_cmt: 0), which contradicts the parent_metabolite topology requiring a distinct compartment. This structural mismatch forces the model builder to assume bioavailability F=1 and Fm=1 with no molar correction. Since the record was built from the abstract only, the summary statistics for clearance 15.2 l/h and central volume 8 l stand in for a fitted model. Extracted — tramadol: V1/F 8 l, Vss 46.2 l, CL 15.2 l/h, kabs 0.65 1/h.

A second, independent reading of the paper (`gpt-oss-120b`) disagrees on which compound was dosed: this record has tramadol, the second reading unknown; it also differs on 9 more fields. That field shapes the model, so the record is marked disputed.
