### Reviewer guidance

**The proline volume of distribution of 47.3 mL is implausibly small, causing rejection.**

The extracted volume of distribution is 47.3 mL, which is physiologically inconsistent with an amino acid like proline that distributes extensively in body water. This low value, combined with a clearance of 0.76 mL/min, indicates a likely unit or scale extraction error. The record was rejected because the volume parameter falls outside the expected physiological range. Extracted — l proline: CL 0.76 mL min−1, V 47.3 mL.
