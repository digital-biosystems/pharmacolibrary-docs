## About

Citrulline is an amino acid. It is made from ornithine and carbamoyl phosphate in one of the central reactions in the urea cycle. It is also produced from arginine as a by-product of the reaction catalyzed by NOS family. …

- **Category:** Drug, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin, Animal Toxin
- **Sources and uses:** Used for nutritional supplementation, also for treating dietary shortage or imbalance.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** L-citrulline is converted to L-arginine by argininosuccinate synthase. L-arginine is in turn responsible for citrulline's therapeutic affects. …
