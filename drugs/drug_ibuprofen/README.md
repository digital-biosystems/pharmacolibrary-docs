## About

Ibuprofen is a nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug used to treat pain, fever, and inflammation in conditions such as rheumatoid arthritis, osteoarthritis, and migraines. It is usually taken by mouth in tablet or capsule form, but an intravenous form is also used when rapid effect is needed.
