## Record summary

**As extracted:** Betaxolol (measured concentrations) drives IKIR6.2/SUR2a inward current (in pA): direct sigmoid Emax (Hill) effect.

**Model:** No model was generated from this record.

> Betaxolol inhibited the outward current of the KIR6.2/SUR2a channel in a sigmoidal Emax-like fashion, but did not significantly inhibit the inward current at concentrations up to 300 μM. The inhibition of the outward current was characterized by an IC50 of 27.5 ± 2.1 μM for the wild-type channel and a Hill coefficient of −0.98, whereas the Q52R and L164P mutants showed similar potency with IC50s of 37.1 ± 2.1 μM (Hill coefficient −1.17) and 30.6 ± 2.3 μM (Hill coefficient −0.87), respectively.
>
