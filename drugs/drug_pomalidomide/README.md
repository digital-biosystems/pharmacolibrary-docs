## About

Pomalidomide is an anti-cancer medication used for multiple myeloma, amyloidosis, myelofibrosis, plasma cell leukemia, and AIDS-related Kaposi sarcoma. It is given orally in capsule form and is well absorbed by mouth. Pomalidomide is approved for use in the United States and the European Union.
