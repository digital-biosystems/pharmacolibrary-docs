## About

Levamisole is an anthelmintic medication used to treat parasitic worm infections such as ascariasis and hookworm. It is given by mouth, usually as tablets, and is well absorbed from the gut. It is listed as an essential medicine but has been recommended for withdrawal from the European Union market.
