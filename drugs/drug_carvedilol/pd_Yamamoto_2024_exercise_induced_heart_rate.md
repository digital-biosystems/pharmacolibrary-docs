## Record summary

**As extracted:** (S)-carvedilol (concentrations from this paper's PK model) drives exercise-induced heart rate (in beats/min): direct Emax (saturable) effect.

**Model:** Model and browser simulation available in **Models** and **Simulation**.

> The model describes a direct, proportional inhibitory effect where (S)-carvedilol plasma concentrations reduce exercise-induced heart rate. It utilizes an Emax mechanism with a baseline effect (E0) of 146 beats/min, a maximum inhibition (Emax) of 19.2 beats/min, and a potency (EC50) of 7.7 ng mL−1.
