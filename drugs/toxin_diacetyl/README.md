# Diacetyl

- **generic name:** Diacetyl
- **T3DB:** [T3D3241](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3241) · **PubChem:** [CID 650](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/650)

## About

Diacetyl is a natural by-product of secondary or malolactic fermentation. It is a vicinal diketone (two C=O groups, side-by-side) with the molecular formula C4H6O2. Carrier of aroma of butter, vinegar, coffee, and other foods. …

- **Category:** Household Toxin, Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin, Animal Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal, eye contact
- **Sources and uses:** Carrier of aroma of butter, vinegar, coffee and other foods.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Diacetyl is a cholinesterase or acetylcholinesterase (AChE) inhibitor. A cholinesterase inhibitor (or 'anticholinesterase') suppresses the action of acetylcholinesterase. …
- **Health effects:** Acute exposure to cholinesterase inhibitors can cause a cholinergic crisis characterized by severe nausea/vomiting, salivation, sweating, bradycardia, hypotension, collapse, and convulsions. Increasing muscle weakness is a possibility and may result in death if respiratory muscles are involved. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** No indication of carcinogenicity (not listed by IARC).
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 1580 mg/kg (Oral, Rat)
LD50: >5 gm/kg (Dermal, Rabbit)
