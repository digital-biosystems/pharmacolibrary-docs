## About

Levocabastine is a non-sedating antihistamine used to treat allergic conjunctivitis and giant papillary conjunctivitis. It is given as eye drops or a nasal spray for allergic eye conditions and rhinitis, respectively. The medication is approved and marketed under specific trade names in different regions.
