## About

Azithromycin is a macrolide antibiotic used to treat many bacterial infections, including pneumonia, bronchitis, sinusitis, otitis media, chlamydia infection, and skin infections. It is widely used around the world and appears on the WHO list of essential medicines; it is also available as an eye drop for eye infections.
