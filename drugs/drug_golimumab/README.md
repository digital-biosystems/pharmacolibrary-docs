## About

Golimumab is a monoclonal antibody that blocks tumour necrosis factor alpha and is used to treat inflammatory conditions such as rheumatoid and psoriatic arthritis, ankylosing spondylitis, and ulcerative colitis. It is an approved medicine, authorised in the European Union, and is widely used for these immune-mediated diseases.
