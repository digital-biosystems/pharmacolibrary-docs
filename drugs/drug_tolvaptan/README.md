## About

Tolvaptan is a vasopressin antagonist used to treat low blood sodium levels associated with heart failure, cirrhosis, and inappropriate antidiuretic hormone secretion. It is given orally as tablets. It is authorised in the European Union for polycystic kidney disease and inappropriate antidiuretic hormone syndrome.
