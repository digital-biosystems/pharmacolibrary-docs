# Caffeic acid

## About

Caffeic acid is a polyphenol present in normal human urine positively correlated to coffee consumption and influenced by the dietary intake of diverse types of food..

- **Category:** Cigarette Toxin, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin, Animal Toxin
- **Sources and uses:** This is a toxic chemical found in cigarettes or generated by tobacco combustion.
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 2B, possibly carcinogenic to humans.
