# Silver bromide

- **generic name:** Silver bromide
- **T3DB:** [T3D1753](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D1753) · **PubChem:** [CID 66199](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/66199)

## About

Silver bromide is a chemical compound of silver and bromine that is found naturally as the mineral bromargyrite. It is used in black-and-white photography film and as a semiconductor. Bromine is a halogen element with the symbol Br and atomic number 35. …

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Bromine is a powerful oxidizing agent and is able to release oxygen free radicals from the water in mucous membranes. These free radicals are also potent oxidizers and produce tissue damage. …
- **Health effects:** Bromine vapour causes irritation and direct damage to the mucous membranes. Elemental bromine also burns the skin. …
- **Treatment:** EYES: irrigate opened eyes for several minutes under running water. …
- **Detected in:** blood, cerebrospinal fluid, urine
