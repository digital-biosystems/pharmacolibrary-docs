### Reviewer guidance

**Posaconazole bioavailability (F) and lag time (Tlag) were defaulted rather than estimated, and covariate scenarios remained unsimulated despite the defined theta_q319_category effect.**

The record specifies absolute bioavailability as a defaulted parameter rather than an explicit estimate from Winchell_2024, mirroring a missing lag time default. Although a covariate effect theta_q319_category with a value of 0.624 is defined for Posaconazole, the model was simulated only for the reference individual. Consequently, the impact of this covariate on the population prediction was never exercised or validated. Extracted — posaconazole: CL 4.71 L/h, V 112 L, kabs 0.212 h−1, Fab 0.826.
