# Merbromin

- **generic name:** Merbromin
- **DrugBank:** none · **PubChem:** [CID 441373](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/441373)
- **groups:** investigational

## About

**Description.** Mercurochrome is a trade name of merbromin commonly used as a topical first-aid antiseptic agent. It is an organomercuric disodium salt compound and a fluorescein that is available in many countries, except Switzerland, France, Germany, and the United States where the drug was withdrawn from market due to the possibility of mercury poisoning.
