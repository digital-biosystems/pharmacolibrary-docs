## About

Tolcapone is an antiparkinson drug that works as an enzyme inhibitor, indicated for the treatment of Parkinson's disease. It is given as an oral tablet, which is well absorbed through the gut. It is authorised in the European Union, although it carries a boxed warning and has been suspended in several countries due to liver toxicity.
