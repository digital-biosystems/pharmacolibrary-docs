# Dieldrin

- **generic name:** Dieldrin
- **T3DB:** [T3D0017](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D0017) · **PubChem:** [CID 969491](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/969491)
- **groups:** experimental

## About

Dieldrin is a chlorinated hydrocarbon used as an insecticide, either by itself or as a component of the closely related insectide aldrin. As dieldrin is neurotoxin and tends to bioaccumulate, its use is now banned in most parts of the world. Dieldrin is a chlorinated hydrocarbon originally produced in 1948 by J. …

- **Category:** Pesticide, Pollutant, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Dieldrin is used as an insecticide.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Dieldrin antagonizes the action of the neurotransmitter gamma-aminobutyric acid (GABA) acting at the GABA-A receptors, effectively blocking the GABA-induced uptake of chloride ions. Dieldrin also inhibits Na+ K+ ATPase and Ca2+ and Mg2+ ATPase which are essential for the transport of calcium across membranes. …
- **Health effects:** Dieldrin is a neurotoxin and works by overstimulating the central nervous system. Ingestion of large amounts of dieldrin causes convulsions and death. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 3, not classifiable as to its carcinogenicity to humans.
- **Minimal risk levels (ATSDR):** Intermediate Oral: 0.0001 mg/kg/day
Chronic Oral: 0.00005 mg/kg/day
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 38 mg/kg (Oral, Rat)
LD50: 56 mg/kg (Dermal, Rat)
LD50: 49 mg/kg (Subcutaneous, Rat)
LD50: 9 mg/kg (Intravenous, Rat)
LC50: 13 mg/m3 over 4 hours (Inhalation, Rat)
- **Treatment:** Treatment is symptomatic, aimed at controlling convulsions, coma, and respiratory depression. …
