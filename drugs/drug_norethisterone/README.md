## About

Norethisterone is a synthetic progestogen used in hormonal contraception and for conditions such as endometriosis, endometrial hyperplasia, acne, and hypogonadism. It is widely used, appearing in many combined and progestogen-only contraceptive preparations, and is also approved for other investigational uses.
