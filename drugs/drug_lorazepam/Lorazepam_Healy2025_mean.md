### Reviewer guidance

**The lorazepam two-compartment model was held back because the intercompartmental rate constants k12 and k21 were left at library defaults instead of being estimated from the paper.**

The record reports lorazepam parameters for mechanically ventilated pediatric ICU patients (CL 0.232 L/h/kg, V1 1.15 L, V 1.37 L/kg, Cmax 563.8 ng/mL, AUC 12990.7 ng·h/mL), but the transfer rate constants k12 and k21, which govern distribution between the two compartments, have no reported values. Library defaults were substituted for these parameters, so the simulated concentration profile depends on values not supported by this paper. Additionally, a reported unit could not be converted to SI, so one parameter reached the model builder without an SI value. Extracted — lorazepam: Cmax 564 ng/mL, AUC 1.3e+04 ng·h/mL, CL 0.232 L/h/kg, V1 1.15 L, V 1.37 L/kg.
