## About

Lorazepam is a benzodiazepine medication used for anxiety disorders, insomnia, and seizures. It is given orally, intravenously, or intramuscularly, and is well absorbed by mouth. It is widely used as a generic medication and is on the World Health Organization's essential medicines list.
