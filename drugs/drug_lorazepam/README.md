## About

Lorazepam is a benzodiazepine used for anxiety and anxiety disorders, insomnia, status epilepticus and other epileptic seizures, catatonia, vomiting, and related sleep-wake and neurotic disorders. It is an approved medicine and appears on the WHO list of essential medicines, so it is widely used, though it carries a boxed warning.
