### Reviewer guidance

**The record was rejected because the source paper studies palbociclib, not lorazepam, which appears only as a co-medication covariate.**

The paper describes a population PK study of palbociclib in pediatric brain tumor patients, where lorazepam is merely a tested concomitant medication. Consequently, the extracted parameters, such as the apparent oral clearance of 58.0 L/h, are incorrectly labeled as belonging to lorazepam. A second reader also disputed the verdict, disagreeing with the values recorded for the absorption rate constant and volume of distribution. Extracted — lorazepam: CL/F 58 L/h, V/F 946 L, tlag 0.8 h, kabs 0.48 /h.

A second, independent reading of the paper (`gpt-oss-120b`) disagrees on parameter Q312: this record has 0.26, the second reading 0.29; it also differs on 4 more fields. That field shapes the model, so the record is marked disputed.
