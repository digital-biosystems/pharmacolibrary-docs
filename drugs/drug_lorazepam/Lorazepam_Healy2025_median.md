### Reviewer guidance

**The lorazepam two-compartment model was held back because the intercompartmental transfer parameters k12 and k21 were left at library defaults instead of being estimated from Healy_2025.**

The record reports lorazepam clearance (0.232 L/h/kg), central volume (1.15 L) and volume of distribution (1.37 L/kg) for mechanically ventilated pediatric ICU patients, but the micro-rate constants k12 and k21 governing distribution between the two compartments were never extracted or justified, so defaults were substituted. These defaulted values affect the simulated concentration profile without support from the paper. Additionally, a reported unit could not be converted to SI, so at least one parameter reached the model build without an SI value.
