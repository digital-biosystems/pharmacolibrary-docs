### Reviewer guidance

**The lorazepam two-compartment record was held back because the intercompartmental rate constants k12 and k21 were left at library defaults instead of being estimated from Nielsen-Kudsk_1983.**

The paper (read abstract-only, so summary statistics stood in for a fitted model) reports for lorazepam in healthy adults a terminal half-life of 14.1 h, distribution half-life of 0.31 h, Vd-area of 1.24 l kg-1, clearance of 62.17 ml kg-1 hr-1, apparent central volume of 0.59 l kg-1, Ka of 0.5 h-1 and Tlag of 0.8 h — but no k12 or k21. The model builder substituted default values for these micro-constants and assumed F=1, Fm=1 with no molar correction (apparent parameterization), so the distribution parameters governing exchange between central and peripheral compartments are not supported by the paper. The deviations check failed on these defaulted parameters, and the record was marked needs_review. Extracted — lorazepam: t1/2z 14.1 hrs, t1/2α 0.31 hrs, V 1.24 l kg-1, CL 62.2 ml kg-1 hr-1, V1/F 0.59 l kg-1, kabs 0.5 h-1, tlag 0.8 h.

A second, independent reading of the paper (`gpt-oss-120b`) disagrees on the value of apparent central volume of distribution: this record has 0.59, the second reading none; it also differs on 7 more fields. That field does not shape the model.
