# Procyclidine

- **generic name:** Procyclidine
- **T3DB:** [T3D2773](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2773) · **DrugBank:** [DB00387](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00387) · **PubChem:** [CID 4919](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/4919)
- **groups:** approved

## About

Procyclidine is only found in individuals that have used or taken this drug. It is a muscarinic antagonist that crosses the blood-brain barrier and is used in the treatment of drug-induced extrapyramidal disorders and in parkinsonism. The mechanism of action is unknown. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral
- **Sources and uses:** For the treatment of all forms of Parkinson's Disease, as well as control of extrapyramidal reactions induced by antipsychotic agents.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** The mechanism of action is unknown. It is thought that Procyclidine acts by blocking central cholinergic receptors, and thus balancing cholinergic and dopaminergic activity in the basal ganglia. …
- **Acute toxicity:** LD<sub>50</sub>=60 mg/kg (IV in mice)
