# Edema factor

- **generic name:** Edema factor
- **T3DB:** [T3D2592](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2592) · **PubChem:** none

## About

Edema factor (EF) is a component of the anthrax (Bacillus anthracis) toxins, with a calcium- and calmodulin-dependent adenylate cyclase activity. The anthrax toxins consist of three distinct proteins: the protective antigen (PA; 83 kDA), the edema factor (EF; 89 kDA) and the lethal factor (LF; 90 kDa). Individually, none of the three is toxic; PA combined with LF is the lethal toxin (LeTx) and PA combined with EF is the edema toxin (EdTx).

- **Category:** Protein, Bacterial Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** ingestion, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Edema factor (EF) is a component of the anthrax (Bacillus anthracis) toxins.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** After binding to a host cell surface receptor, PA is cleaved by membrane endoproteases of the furin family. Cleaved PA molecules assemble into heptamers, which then associate with EF and LF. …
- **Health effects:** Edema factor (EF) is a component of the anthrax (Bacillus anthracis) toxins. Anthrax is an acute diease that may manifest as a pulmonary, gastrointestinal, or cutaneous infection. …
