# Hydroquinone

- **generic name:** Hydroquinone
- **T3DB:** [T3D4577](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4577) · **PubChem:** [CID 785](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/785)
- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

Hydroquinone, also benzene-1,4-diol, is an aromatic organic compound which is a type of phenol. Hydroquinone is commonly used as a biomarker for benzene exposure. The presence of hydroquinone in normal individuals stems mainly from direct dietary ingestion, catabolism of tyrosine and other substrates by gut bacteria, ingestion of arbutin containing foods, cigarette smoking, and the use of some over-the-counter medicines. …

- **Category:** Cigarette Toxin, Household Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Food Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Sources and uses:** Hydroquinone has a variety of uses principally associated with its action as a reducing agent which is soluble in water. It is a major component in most photographic developers where, with the compound Metol, it reduces silver halides to elemental silver. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 3, not classifiable as to its carcinogenicity to humans.
