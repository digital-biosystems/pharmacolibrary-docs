# Mesoridazine

- **generic name:** Mesoridazine
- **T3DB:** [T3D2933](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2933) · **DrugBank:** [DB00933](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00933) · **PubChem:** [CID 4078](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/4078)

## About

Mesoridazine is only found in individuals that have used or taken this drug. It is a phenothiazine antipsychotic with effects similar to chlorpromazine. [PubChem]Based upon animal studies, mesoridazine, as with other phenothiazines, acts indirectly on reticular formation, whereby neuronal activity into reticular formation is reduced without affecting its intrinsic ability to activate the cerebral cortex. In addition, the phenothiazines exhibit at least part of their activities through depression of hypothalamic centers. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, intramuscular injection. Well absorbed from the gastrointestinal tract.
- **Sources and uses:** Used in the treatment of schizophrenia, organic brain disorders, alcoholism and psychoneuroses.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Based upon animal studies, mesoridazine, as with other phenothiazines, acts indirectly on reticular formation, whereby neuronal activity into reticular formation is reduced without affecting its intrinsic ability to activate the cerebral cortex. In addition, the phenothiazines exhibit at least part of their activities through depression of hypothalamic centers. …
- **Acute toxicity:** Oral LD<sub>50</sub> is 560 &plusmn; 62.5 mg/kg and 644 &plusmn; 48 mg/kg in mouse and rat, respectively.
