## About

Carbamazepine is an anticonvulsant used for epilepsy and seizures, trigeminal neuralgia, and bipolar disorder, and has also been used for other mood and psychiatric conditions. It remains widely used and is included on the WHO essential medicines list, though it carries a boxed warning.
