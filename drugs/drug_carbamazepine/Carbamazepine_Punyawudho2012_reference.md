### Reviewer guidance

**The carbamazepine population model (CL/F 3.59 L/hr, V/F 102 L, Ka 0.197 hr−1) was held back because the builder assumed F=1, Fm=1 and no molar correction, and left the lag time at a default instead of an explicit estimate.**

The record reports apparent parameters for carbamazepine in elderly epilepsy patients: CL/F 3.59 L/hr, V/F 102 L and an absorption rate constant of 0.197 hr−1. The refusal reason is the builder's deviations: bioavailability was assumed to be 1 (F=1) and metabolite fraction Fm=1 with no molar correction, so the parameters are apparent rather than explicitly estimated, which the adjudication judged not acceptable. In addition, the absorption lag time (Tlag) was defaulted rather than given an explicit value. A second reader also disagreed with the record's designation of carbamazepine as the dosed compound and primary analyte, leaving those fields uncertain. Extracted — carbamazepine: CL/F 3.59 L/hr, V/F 102 L, kabs 0.197 hr−1.
