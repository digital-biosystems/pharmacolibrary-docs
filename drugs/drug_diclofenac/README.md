## About

Diclofenac is a non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drug used to manage pain and inflammatory conditions like arthritis and gout. It is given orally with good absorption, or by injection, topical application, eye drops, and rectal suppositories. It is widely used as one of the most commonly prescribed medications in the United States.
