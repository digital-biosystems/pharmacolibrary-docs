# Halazepam

## About

Halazepam is a benzodiazepine derivative. It possesses anxiolytic, anticonvulsant, sedative and skeletal muscle relaxant properties. It is a trifluoromethyl derivative of nordazepam. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral
- **Sources and uses:** Used to relieve anxiety, nervousness, and tension associated with anxiety disorders.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Benzodiazepines bind nonspecifically to benzodiazepine receptors BNZ1, which mediates sleep, and BNZ2, which affects affects muscle relaxation, anticonvulsant activity, motor coordination, and memory. As benzodiazepine receptors are thought to be coupled to gamma-aminobutyric acid-A (GABAA) receptors, this enhances the effects of GABA by increasing GABA affinity for the GABA receptor. …
- **Health effects:** Withdrawal symptoms include anxiety, convulsions, and possible death. They cause slurred speech, disorientation and "drunken" behavior. …
- **Treatment:** The patient should be evaluated to determine adequacy of airway, breathing and circulation. …
