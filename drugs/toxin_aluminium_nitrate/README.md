# Aluminium nitrate

- **generic name:** Aluminium nitrate
- **T3DB:** [T3D1515](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D1515) · **PubChem:** [CID 26053](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/26053)

## About

Aluminium nitrate is a chemical compound of aluminum. It is used in tanning leather, antiperspirants, corrosion inhibitors, extraction of uranium, petroleum refining, and as a nitrating agent. Aluminum is the most abundant metal in the earth's crust and is always found combined with other elements such as oxygen, silicon, and fluorine. …

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation
- **Sources and uses:** Aluminium nitrate is used in tanning leather, antiperspirants, corrosion inhibitors, extraction of uranium, petroleum refining, and as a nitrating agent.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** The main target organs of aluminum are the central nervous system and bone. Aluminum binds with dietary phosphorus and impairs gastrointestinal absorption of phosphorus. …
- **Health effects:** Aluminum targets the nervous system and causes decreased nervous system performance and is associated with altered function of the blood-brain barrier. The accumulation of aluminum in the body may cause bone or brain diseases. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** Ingested nitrate or nitrite under conditions that result in endogenous nitrosation is probably carcinogenic to humans (Group 2A).
- **Minimal risk levels (ATSDR):** Intermediate Oral: 1.0 mg/kg/day 
Chronic Oral: 1.0 mg/kg/day
- **Treatment:** Methemoglobinemia can be treated with supplemental oxygen and methylene blue 1% solution administered intravenously slowly over five minutes followed by IV flush with normal saline. …
- **Detected in:** blood, cerebrospinal fluid, saliva, urine
