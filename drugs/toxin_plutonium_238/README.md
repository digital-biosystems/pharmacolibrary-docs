# Plutonium-238

- **generic name:** Plutonium-238
- **T3DB:** [T3D0124](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D0124) · **PubChem:** [CID 61709](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/61709)

## About

Plutonium-238 (238 Pu or Pu-238) is a radioactive isotope of plutonium that has a half-life of 87.7 years.

<p><small>Source: <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plutonium-238?oldid=1374366449">Wikipedia</a> · <a href="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/">CC BY-SA 4.0</a></small></p>

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation
- **Sources and uses:** Plutonium-238 is a heat source in radioisotope thermoelectric generators, which are used to power some spacecraft.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** The alpha radiation plutonium emits does not penetrate the skin but can irradiate internal organs when plutonium is inhaled or ingested. Particularly at risk are the skeleton, where it is likely to be absorbed by the bone surface, and the liver, where it collects and becomes concentrated. …
- **Health effects:** Plutonium's radioactivity can cause cancers of the bone, liver, and lungs if ingested or inhaled. Large amounts may also cause acute radiation poisoning.
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 1, carcinogenic to humans.
