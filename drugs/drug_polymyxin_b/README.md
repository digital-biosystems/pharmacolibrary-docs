## About

Polymyxin B is an antibiotic used to treat infections caused by susceptible bacteria such as Escherichia coli and Bacteroides, and is also applied as an intestinal antiinfective and in eye and ear preparations. It remains in use, with approved and veterinary-approved status, though it is not an EU-authorised product according to the available data.
