## About

Clioquinol is a hydroxyquinoline derivative used to treat skin infections such as tinea and fungal or protozoal conditions. It is administered topically as a cream, ointment, or emulsion, applied vaginally, or used in the ear. The drug has been withdrawn from use due to reports of neurotoxicity.
