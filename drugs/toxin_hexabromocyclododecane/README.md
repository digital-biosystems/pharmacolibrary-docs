## About

Hexabromocyclododecane is an organobromide compound. It is a brominated flame retardant used in extruded (XPS) and expanded (EPS) polystyrene foam that is used as thermal insulation in the building industry. Hexabromocyclododecane is also a persistent organic pollutant, thus its use is controversial. …

- **Category:** Household Toxin, Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** It is a brominated flame retardant used in extruded (XPS) and expanded (EPS) polystyrene foam that is used as thermal insulation in the building industry. Diatomic bromine does not occur naturally, but bromine salts can be found in crustal rock.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Bromine is a powerful oxidizing agent and is able to release oxygen free radicals from the water in mucous membranes. These free radicals are also potent oxidizers and produce tissue damage. …
- **Health effects:** Bromine vapour causes irritation and direct damage to the mucous membranes. Elemental bromine also burns the skin. …
- **Treatment:** EYES: irrigate opened eyes for several minutes under running water. …
