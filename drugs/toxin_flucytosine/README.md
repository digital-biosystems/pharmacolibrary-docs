# Flucytosine

- **generic name:** Flucytosine
- **T3DB:** [T3D2983](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2983) · **DrugBank:** [DB01099](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB01099) · **PubChem:** [CID 3366](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/3366)
- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

Flucytosine is only found in individuals that have used or taken this drug. It is a fluorinated cytosine analog that is used as an antifungal agent. [PubChem]Although the exact mode of action is unknown, it has been proposed that flucytosine acts directly on fungal organisms by competitive inhibition of purine and pyrimidine uptake and indirectly by intracellular metabolism to 5-fluorouracil. Flucytosine enters the fungal cell via cytosine permease; thus, flucytosine is metabolized to 5-fluorouracil within fungal organisms. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** rapidly and virtually completely absorbed following oral administration. Bioavailability 78% to 89%.
- **Sources and uses:** For the treatment (in combination with amphotericin B) of serious infections caused by susceptible strains of Candida (septicemia, endocarditis and urinary system infections) and/or Cryptococcus (meningitis and pulmonary infections).
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Although the exact mode of action is unknown, it has been proposed that flucytosine acts directly on fungal organisms by competitive inhibition of purine and pyrimidine uptake and indirectly by intracellular metabolism to 5-fluorouracil. Flucytosine enters the fungal cell via cytosine permease; thus, flucytosine is metabolized to 5-fluorouracil within fungal organisms. …
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 15 gm/kg (Oral, Rat)
- **Treatment:** In the management of overdosage, prompt gastric lavage or the use of an emetic is recommended. …
