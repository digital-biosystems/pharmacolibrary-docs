## About

Pazopanib is a protein kinase inhibitor used to treat kidney cancer, especially renal cell carcinoma, and other tumours such as gastrointestinal stromal tumours and soft-tissue sarcomas. It is an approved anticancer medicine authorised in the European Union for renal cell carcinoma.
