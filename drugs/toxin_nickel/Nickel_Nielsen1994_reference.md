### Reviewer guidance

**The volume of distribution for nickel is implausibly small at 10 mL in mice, derived from an acetone volume rather than a fitted pharmacokinetic parameter.**

The record lists a volume of distribution value of 10.0 mL with the label 'acetone volume', indicating an extraction error where solvent volume was mapped to a PK parameter. The record is rejected because this magnitude is physiologically impossible for a whole-body distribution volume in mice. Additionally, the model relies on abstract-only summary statistics rather than a fitted model, further compromising validity. Extracted — nickel: body_burden 15 μg, V 10 mL.
