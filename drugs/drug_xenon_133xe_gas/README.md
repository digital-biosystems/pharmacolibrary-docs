## About

Xenon-133 is a radioactive isotope of xenon used as a diagnostic radiopharmaceutical for imaging of the respiratory system. It is an approved diagnostic agent, classified among respiratory-system diagnostic radiopharmaceuticals, and is used in nuclear medicine for lung ventilation studies.
