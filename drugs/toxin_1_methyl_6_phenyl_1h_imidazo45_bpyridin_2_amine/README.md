## About

1-Methyl-6-phenyl-1H-imidazo[4,5-b]pyridin-2-amine is a food-related mutagen, reported to be the most abundant heterocyclic amine found in cooked meat and fish.

- **Category:** Food Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Sources and uses:** 1-Methyl-6-phenyl-1H-imidazo[4,5-b]pyridin-2-amine is a food-related mutagen, reported to be the most abundant heterocyclic amine found in cooked meat and fish.
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 2B, possibly carcinogenic to humans.
