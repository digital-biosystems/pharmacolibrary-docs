## About

Stepronin is a mucolytic (mucokinetic) agent that was used to loosen mucus in cough and cold preparations, and has also been described as an immunosuppressive and antiviral agent. It is currently considered experimental and does not appear to be an approved medicine in major markets such as the European Union.
