# Deoxycholic acid

- **generic name:** Deoxycholic acid
- **T3DB:** [T3D4961](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4961) · **DrugBank:** [DB03619](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB03619) · **PubChem:** [CID 222528](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/222528)
- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

Deoxycholic acid is a bile acid formed by bacterial action from cholate. It is usually conjugated with glycine or taurine. Deoxycholic acid acts as a detergent to solubilize fats for intestinal absorption, is reabsorbed itself, and is used as a choleretic and detergent. …

- **Category:** Natural Toxin, Animal Toxin
- **Health effects:** Chronically high levels of deoxycholic acid are associated with several forms of cancer including colon cancer.
- **Carcinogenicity:** Not listed by IARC.
