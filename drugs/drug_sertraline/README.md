## About

Sertraline is a selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor used to treat depression, anxiety disorders, obsessive-compulsive disorder, and panic disorder. It is given orally in forms such as tablets and capsules. It is widely used and available as a generic medication, ranking among the most prescribed drugs in countries like the United States and Australia.
