# 1-Methyl-4-phenyl-1,2,3,6-tetrahydropyridine

- **generic name:** 1-Methyl-4-phenyl-1,2,3,6-tetrahydropyridine
- **T3DB:** [T3D3474](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3474) · **PubChem:** [CID 1388](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/1388)

## About

1-methyl-4-phenyl-1,2,3,6-tetrahydropyridine (MPTP) is a neurotoxin that causes permanent symptoms of Parkinson's disease by killing certain neurons in the substantia nigra of the brain. While MPTP itself does not have opioid effects, it is related to MPPP, a synthetic opioid drug with effects similar to those of heroin and morphine. MPTP can be accidentally produced during the illicit manufacture of MPPP, and that is how its Parkinson-inducing effects were first discovered. …

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Sources and uses:** 1-methyl-4-phenyl-1,2,3,6-tetrahydropyridine (MPTP) is a neurotoxin that causes permanent symptoms of Parkinson's disease by killing certain neurons in the substantia nigra of the brain. MPTP can be accidentally produced during the illicit manufacture of MPPP, and that is how its Parkinson-inducing effects were first discovered. …
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** MPTP itself is not toxic, and as a lipophilic compound can cross the blood-brain barrier. Once inside the brain, MPTP is metabolized into the toxic cation 1-methyl-4-phenylpyridinium (MPP+) by the enzyme MAO-B of glial cells. …
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 54 mg/kg (Subcutaneous, Mouse)
- **Treatment:** Because MPTP itself is not directly harmful, toxic effects of acute MPTP poisoning can be mitigated by the administration of monoamine oxidase inhibitors (MAOIs) such as selegiline. …
