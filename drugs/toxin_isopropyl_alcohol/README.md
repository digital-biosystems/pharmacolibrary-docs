## About

Isopropyl alcohol or 2-propanol is an isomer of 1-propanol. It is a colorless liquid having disinfectant properties. It is used in the manufacture of acetone and its derivatives and as a solvent. …

- **Category:** Household Toxin, Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Food Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Sources and uses:** It is used in the manufacture of acetone and its derivatives and as a solvent. Topically, it is used as an antiseptic. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 3, not classifiable as to its carcinogenicity to humans.
- **Detected in:** blood, cerebrospinal fluid, saliva, urine
