# Estriol

- **T3DB:** [T3D4790](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4790) · **DrugBank:** [DB04573](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB04573) · **PubChem:** [CID 5756](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/5756)
- **groups:** approved, investigational, vet_approved

## About

A hydroxylated metabolite of estradiol or estrone that has a hydroxyl group at C3-beta, 16-alpha, and 17-beta position. Estriol is a major urinary estrogen. During pregnancy, large amount of estriol is produced by the placenta. …

- **Category:** Drug, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin, Animal Toxin
- **Sources and uses:** Used as a test to determine the general health of an unborn fetus.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Estriol levels can be measured to give an indication of the general health of the fetus. DHEA-S is produced by the adrenal cortex of the fetus. …
- **Acute toxicity:** ORAL: Acute: >2000 mg/kg [Rat].
