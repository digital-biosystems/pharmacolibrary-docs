# 3-Methyl-2-oxovaleric acid

- **generic name:** 3-Methyl-2-oxovaleric acid
- **T3DB:** [T3D4424](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4424) · **PubChem:** [CID 47](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/47)
- **groups:** experimental

## About

3-Methyl-2-oxovaleric acid is a metabolite of isoleucine in man, animals and bacteria. It is the alpha-keto acid analogue of isoleucine. 3-Methyl-2-oxovaleric acid is produced from isoleucine by cytosolic branched chain aminotransferase 1 (EC:2.6.1.42), whereupon it is further degraded by branched chain keto acid dehydrogenase E1 to 2-Methyl-1-hydroxybutyl-ThPP. It is used as a clinical marker for maple syrup urine disease (MSUD). …

- **Category:** Animal Toxin, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Sources and uses:** This is an endogenously produced metabolite found in the human body. It is used in metabolic reactions, catabolic reactions or waste generation.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** 3-Methyl-2-oxovaleric acid is produced from isoleucine by cytosolic branched chain aminotransferase 1, whereupon it is further degraded by branched chain keto acid dehydrogenase E1 to 2-methyl-1-hydroxybutyl-ThPP. It is used as a clinical marker for maple syrup urine disease (MSUD). …
- **Health effects:** Chronically high levels of 3-methyl-2-oxovaleric acid are associated with Maple Syrup Urine Disease.
