### Reviewer guidance

**The CLm/F parameter for olanzapine has unit L/h, conflicting with the required unit of L/kg/h, causing a dimension mismatch and record rejection.**

The CLm/F parameter, representing metabolite clearance, was recorded with a value of 21.0 and a unit of L/h. The model structure expects a normalized clearance with dimensions of volume per mass per time (L/kg/h), but the extracted unit L/h lacks the mass dimension. Consequently, the dimension check failed because the reported unit could not be converted to the required SI-normalized format.
