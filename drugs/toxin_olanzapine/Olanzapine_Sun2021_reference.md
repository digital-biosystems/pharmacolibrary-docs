### Reviewer guidance

**Olanzapine’s absorption rate constant of 6.61 was extracted with unit hours instead of inverse hours, creating a dimensional mismatch that prevented model construction.**

The absorption rate constant for olanzapine is recorded as 6.61 h, but this parameter physically requires units of inverse time (1/h) to represent the first-order absorption rate. The extracted unit of hours is dimensionally incompatible with the parameter’s definition. Consequently, the parameter could not be converted to standard SI units, leaving it without a valid value for the model. Extracted — olanzapine: CL/F 35.4 L/h, V1/F 297 L, kabs 6.61 h, tlag 0.782 h, V2/F 124 L, Q/F 12.1 L/h.
