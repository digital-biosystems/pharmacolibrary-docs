# Deltamethrin

- **generic name:** Deltamethrin
- **T3DB:** [T3D1035](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D1035) · **PubChem:** [CID 40585](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/40585)
- **groups:** experimental

## About

Deltamethrin is a pyrethroid (type 2) ester insecticide. This material is a member of one of the safest classes of pesticides: synthetic pyrethroids. While mammalian exposure to deltamethrin is classified as safe, this pesticide is highly toxic to aquatic life, particularly fish, and therefore must be used with extreme caution around water. …

- **Category:** Pesticide, Household Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** inhalation, oral, dermal, eye contact.
- **Sources and uses:** Pyrethroids are used as insecticides.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Both type I and type II pyrethroids exert their effect by prolonging the open phase of the sodium channel gates when a nerve cell is excited. They appear to bind to the membrane lipid phase in the immediate vicinity of the sodium channel, thus modifying the channel kinetics. …
- **Health effects:** At high doses, signs of poisoning attributable to deltamethrin include profuse salivation and pulmonary edema, clonic seizures, opisthotonos (i.e., the spine is bent forward such that a supine body rests on its head and heels), coma, and death. At lower doses, commonly observed effects include paresthesia and erythema.
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 3, not classifiable as to its carcinogenicity to humans.
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 4123 mg/kg (Oral, Rat)
LD50: >2460 mg/kg (Dermal, Rabbit)
- **Treatment:** Following oral exposure, the treatment is symptomatic and supportive and includes monitoring for the development of hypersensitivity reactions with respiratory distress. …
