## About

Perhexiline is only found in individuals that have used or taken this drug. It is a coronary vasodilator used especially for angina of effort. It may cause neuropathy and hepatitis. [PubChem]Perhexiline binds to the mitochondrial enzyme carnitine palmitoyltransferase (CPT)-1 and CPT-2. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** well absorbed (>80%) from the gastrointestinal tract following oral administration.
- **Sources and uses:** For the management of severe angina pectoris.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Perhexiline binds to the mitochondrial enzyme carnitine palmitoyltransferase (CPT)-1 and CPT-2. It acts by shifting myocardial substrate utilisation from long chain fatty acids to carbohydrates through inhibition of CPT-1 and, to a lesser extent, CPT-2, resulting in increased glucose and lactate utilization. …
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 2150 mg/kg (Oral, Rat)
LD50: 2641 mg/kg (Oral, Mouse)
