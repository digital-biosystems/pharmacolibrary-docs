# Aplysiatoxin

- **generic name:** Aplysiatoxin
- **T3DB:** [T3D4235](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4235) · **PubChem:** [CID 21672114](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/21672114)

## About

Aplysiatoxin is a cyanotoxin produced by certain cyanobacteria species. It is used as a defensive secretion to protect these cyanobacteria from predation by fish, being a potent irritant and carcinogen, by acting as a powerful activator of Protein kinase C. It has dermatotoxic activity causing inflamation of the skin. …

- **Category:** Bacterial Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** dermal, ingestion.
- **Sources and uses:** Aplysiatoxin is a cyanotoxin produced by certain cyanobacteria species. It is used as a defensive secretion to protect these cyanobacteria from predation by fish, being a potent irritant and carcinogen, by acting as a powerful activator of Protein kinase C. …
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Aplysiatoxin is a 12-O-tetradecanoylphorbol-13-acetate (TPA)-type tumor promoter that activates protein kinase C (PKC). In cancer cells, PKC isozymes are involved in cell proliferation, survival, invasion, migration, apoptosis, angiogenesis, and anticancer drug resistance through their increased or decreased participation in various cellular signaling pathways.
- **Health effects:** Aplysiatoxin is one of the causative agents of 'swimmer’s itch'. It also has tumor-promoting effects. Aplysiatoxin and debromoaplysiatoxin were found to be the causative agents of a red alga Gracilaria coronopifolia poisoning in Hawaii.
- **Carcinogenicity:** Not listed by IARC.
