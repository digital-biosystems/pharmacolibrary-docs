## Record summary

**As extracted:** L-OHP (measured concentrations) drives number of withdrawal responses in the acetone test (in times): indirect response — drug stimulates the production of number of withdrawal responses in the acetone test.

**Model:** Model available in **Models**; browser simulation unavailable.

> The model posits that L-OHP plasma concentrations stimulate the number of withdrawal responses in the acetone test by increasing the production rate according to an indirect response III model. Key parameters include a baseline (x0,acute) of 3.1 times, a production rate (kin,acute) of 0.004 times·day−1, a loss rate (kout,acute) of 0.06 day−1, a maximum effect (Emax,acute) of 10.1 times·day−1, and an EC50 of 0.36 μg·mL−1 with a fixed Hill coefficient (gamma) of 6.
