# Rifapentine

- **generic name:** Rifapentine
- **T3DB:** [T3D3018](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3018) · **DrugBank:** [DB01201](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB01201) · **PubChem:** [CID 6323497](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/6323497)
- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

Rifapentine is only found in individuals that have used or taken this drug. It is an antibiotic drug used in the treatment of tuberculosis. Rifapentine has shown higher bacteriostatic and bactericidal activities especially against intracellular bacteria growing in human monocyte-derived macrophages. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** rapidly and well absorbed from the gastrointestinal tract.
- **Sources and uses:** For the treatment of pulmonary tuberculosis.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Rifapentine has shown higher bacteriostatic and bactericidal activities especially against intracellular bacteria growing in human monocyte-derived macrophages. Rifapentine inhibits DNA-dependent RNA polymerase in susceptible strains of M. tuberculosis. …
