# Ammonium diuranate

- **generic name:** Ammonium diuranate
- **T3DB:** [T3D1160](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D1160) · **PubChem:** [CID 197096](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/197096)

## About

Ammonium diuranate or (ADU) ((NH4)2U2O7), is one of the intermediate chemical forms of uranium produced during yellowcake production. The name "yellowcake" originally given to this bright yellow salt, now applies to mixtures of uranium oxides which are actually hardly ever yellow. It also is an intermediate in mixed-oxide (MOX) fuel fabrication. Although it is usually called "ammonium diuranate" as though it has a "diuranate" ion U 2O2− 7, this is not necessarily the case. It can also be called diammonium diuranium heptaoxide. The structure was theorized to be similar to that of uranium trioxide dihydrate. Recent literature has shown that the structure more closely resembles the mineral metaschoepite, the partially dehydrated form of schoepite.

<p><small>Source: <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ammonium_diuranate?oldid=1336024452">Wikipedia</a> · <a href="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/">CC BY-SA 4.0</a></small></p>

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Uranium is combined with either bicarbonate or a plasma protein in the blood but once in the kidney, it is released and forms complexes with phosphate ligands and proteins in the tubular wall, causing damage. Uranium may also inhibit both sodium transport-dependent and independent ATP utilization and mitochondrial oxidative phosphorylation in the renal proximal tubule. …
- **Health effects:** Uranium primarily damages the kidney, but may also damage the lungs, central nervous system, and immune system. Uranium's radioactivity is believed to damage the DNA, resulting in carcinogenic effects and reproductive and developmental damage.
- **Carcinogenicity:** Uranium: Group 1, carcinogenic to humans
- **Minimal risk levels (ATSDR):** Intermediate Inhalation: 0.0004 mg/m3 (Soluble uranium salts)
Chronic Inhalation: 0.0003 mg/m3 (Soluble uranium salts)
Intermediate Oral: 0.002 mg/kg/day (Soluble uranium salts)
Intermediate Inhalation: 0.008 mg/m3 (Insoluble uranium compounds)
