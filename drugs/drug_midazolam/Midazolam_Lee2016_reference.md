### Reviewer guidance

**Raltegravir is incorrectly linked to midazolam metabolites, creating an orphan structure with no valid dose connection.**

The model includes raltegravir and raltegravir glucuronide alongside midazolam and its metabolites, but the measured compound is raltegravir while the drug is midazolam. The raltegravir-to-glucuronide link is disconnected from the midazolam parent, rendering the raltegravir compartment unreachable from the dose. Additionally, raltegravir clearance and volume parameters (41.7 L/h and 157 L) were extracted but have no valid parent drug context. Extracted — midazolam: CL/F 41.7 L/h, MTT 1.04 h, Fab 1, kabs 4.23 1/h, n_transit 1.07, V 157 L; raltegravir glucuronide: fm 0.0324, CL 0.715 L/h, V 1 L.

A second, independent reading of the paper (`gpt-oss-120b`) disagrees on which compound was dosed: this record has raltegravir, the second reading midazolam + raltegravir; it also differs on 9 more fields. That field shapes the model, so the record is marked disputed.
