# Fumitremorgin B

- **generic name:** Fumitremorgin B
- **T3DB:** [T3D3743](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3743) · **PubChem:** [CID 628311](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/628311)

## About

Fumitremorgin B is a mycotoxin produced by Aspergillus fumigatus, Aspergillus caespitosus, Penicillium lanosum and Penicillium piscarium. Fumitremorgin B is isolated from A. fumigatus infected rice and miso Fumitremorgin b belongs to the family of Indoles. These are compounds containing an indole moiety, which consists of pyrrole ring fused to benzene to form 2,3-benzopyrrole.

- **Category:** Fungal Toxin, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, dermal, inhalation, and parenteral (contaminated drugs).
- **Sources and uses:** Fumitremorgin B is a mycotoxin that has been found in the fungus Aspergillus fumigatus.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Tremorgenic mycotoxins exert their toxic effects by interfering with neurotransmitter release, possibly by causing degeneration of nerve terminals. They are thought to inhibit gamma-aminobutyric acid (GABA) receptors, both pre- and postsynaptic, as well as inhibit transmitter breakdown at the GABA-T receptors. …
- **Health effects:** Tremorgenic mycotoxins affect central nervous system activity. They cause a neurological disease of cattle known as "staggers syndrome". Fumitremorgin B is also genotoxic and causes DNA damage.
- **Treatment:** To control severe tremors caused by tremorgenic mycotoxins, methocarbamol should be administered. …
