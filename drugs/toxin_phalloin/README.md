# Phalloin

- **generic name:** Phalloin
- **T3DB:** [T3D2469](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2469) · **PubChem:** [CID 34183](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/34183)

## About

Phalloin is one of a group of toxins from the death cap (Amanita phalloides) known as phallotoxins. The phallotoxins consist of at least seven compounds, all of which have seven similar peptide rings, isolated from the death cap (Amanita phalloides).

- **Category:** Fungal Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, dermal, inhalation, and parenteral (contaminated drugs).
- **Sources and uses:** Prophalloin is one of a group of toxins from the death cap (Amanita phalloides) known as phallotoxins.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Phallotoxins bind actin, preventing its depolymerization and poisoning the cell. They bind specifically at the interface between F-actin subunits, locking adjacent subunits together. …
- **Health effects:** Liver lesions, tachycardia, hypoglycemia, hypotension and electrolyte imbalance with acid- base disturbance.
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 1.5 mg/kg (Mouse)
- **Treatment:** Consider gastric lavage and activated charcoal.
