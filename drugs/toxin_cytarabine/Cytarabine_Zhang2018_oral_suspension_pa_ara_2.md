### Reviewer guidance

**The record was rejected because the metabolite PA-Ara lacks an identified conversion parameter from cytarabine, leaving its compartment unreachable from the dose.**

The structural definition links PA-Ara as a metabolite of cytarabine, but the link parameter is marked as unknown with no corresponding kinetic value provided. This missing formation rate constant creates an orphan compartment with no path from the administered cytarabine dose. Consequently, the model cannot simulate PA-Ara exposure derived from cytarabine metabolism. Extracted — PA-Ara: AUC∞ 4.63e+03 ng h mL−1, Cmax 167 ng mL−1, tmax 6 h, t1/2z 19.7 h, MRT 29.1 h, CL/F 4.32 L h−1 kg−1.
