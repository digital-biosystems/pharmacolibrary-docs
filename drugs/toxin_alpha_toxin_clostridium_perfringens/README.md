# Alpha-toxin (Clostridium perfringens)

- **generic name:** Alpha-toxin (Clostridium perfringens)
- **T3DB:** [T3D2599](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2599) · **PubChem:** none

## About

Clostridium perfringens alpha toxin is a toxin produced by Clostridium perfringens. It exhibits a phospholipase C (PLC) and sphingomyelinase activity. Clostridium perfringens alpha toxin is responsible for gas gangrene and myonecrosis in infected tissues. …

- **Category:** Protein, Bacterial Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** ingestion, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Clostridium perfringens alpha toxin is a toxin produced by Clostridium perfringens.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Clostridium perfringens alpha toxin is a zinc metallophospholipase requiring zinc for activation. The C-terminal C2-like PLAT domain binds calcium and allows the toxin to bind to the phospholipid head-groups on the cell surface. …
- **Health effects:** Clostridium perfringens alpha toxin is responsible for gas gangrene and myonecrosis in infected tissues. It also possesses hemolytic activity.
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 3 ug/kg (Intravenous, Mouse)
