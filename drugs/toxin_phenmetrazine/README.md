# Phenmetrazine

- **generic name:** Phenmetrazine
- **T3DB:** [T3D2904](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2904) · **DrugBank:** [DB00830](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00830) · **PubChem:** [CID 4762](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/4762)
- **groups:** approved, illicit

## About

A sympathomimetic drug used primarily as an appetite depressant. Its actions and mechanisms are similar to dextroamphetamine. [PubChem]

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** readily absorbed from the gastro-intestinal tract and buccal mucosa.
- **Sources and uses:** Used as an anorectic in the treatment of obesity.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Phenmetrazine is thought to block the reuptake of norepinephrine and dopamine into the presynaptic neuron leading to an increase in the release of these monoamines into the extraneuronal space. Dopamine integrates incoming sensory stimuli, initiates and controls fine movement (nigro-neostriatal pathway), controls emotional behavior (midbrain mesolimbic-forebrain system) and controls hypothalamic-pituitary endocrine system (tubero-infundibular system). …
- **Health effects:** Using large amounts of these drugs can result in a condition known as amphetamine psychosis -- which can result in auditory, visual and tactile hallucinations, intense paranoia, irrational thoughts and beliefs, delusions, and mental confusion.
- **Acute toxicity:** Adult monkeys have an LD<sub>50</sub> of 15 to 20 mg/kg, whereas for young monkeys the LD<sub>50</sub> is only 5 mg/kg.
