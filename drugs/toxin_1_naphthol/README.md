# 1-Naphthol

- **generic name:** 1-Naphthol
- **T3DB:** [T3D3968](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3968) · **PubChem:** [CID 7005](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/7005)

## About

1-Naphthol, or α-naphthol, is an organic compound with the formula C10H7OH. It is a fluorescent white solid. 1-Naphthol differs from its isomer 2-naphthol by the location of the hydroxyl group on the naphthalene ring. The naphthols are naphthalene homologues of phenol. Both isomers are soluble in simple organic solvents. They are precursors to a variety of useful compounds.

<p><small>Source: <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1-Naphthol?oldid=1379983197">Wikipedia</a> · <a href="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/">CC BY-SA 4.0</a></small></p>

- **Category:** Pesticide, Household Toxin, Natural Toxin, Animal Toxin
- **Sources and uses:** This is a natural compound that is used as a pesticide.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** 1-Naphthol is a cholinesterase or acetylcholinesterase (AChE) inhibitor. A cholinesterase inhibitor (or 'anticholinesterase') suppresses the action of acetylcholinesterase. …
- **Health effects:** Acute exposure to cholinesterase inhibitors can cause a cholinergic crisis characterized by severe nausea/vomiting, salivation, sweating, bradycardia, hypotension, collapse, and convulsions. Increasing muscle weakness is a possibility and may result in death if respiratory muscles are involved. …
