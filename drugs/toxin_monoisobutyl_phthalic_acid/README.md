# Monoisobutyl phthalic acid

- **generic name:** Monoisobutyl phthalic acid
- **T3DB:** [T3D3960](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3960) · **PubChem:** [CID 92272](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/92272)

## About

Monoisobutyl phthalic acid is a phthalate metabolite that has been found in human meconium and in semen , saliva , and urine Phthalate esters are a family of multifunctional compounds widely used as plasticizers, solvents, or additives in many diverse products such as poly(vinyl chloride) (PVC) materials, pharmaceuticals and medical devices, pesticides, lubricants, and personal care products. Humans have been exposed to phthalates through the manufacture, ubiquitous use, and disposal of PVC materials and other phthalate-containing products..

- **Category:** Pesticide, Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin, Animal Toxin
- **Sources and uses:** This is a natural compound that is used as a pesticide.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Phthalates are synthetic industrial compounds capable of disrupting the endocrine system. The phthalates have been the most common chemical compounds with the ability to disrupt the endocrine system. …
