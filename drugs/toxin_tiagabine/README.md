# Tiagabine

- **generic name:** Tiagabine
- **T3DB:** [T3D2926](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2926) · **DrugBank:** [DB00906](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00906) · **PubChem:** [CID 60648](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/60648)
- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

Tiagabine is an anti-convulsive medication. It is also used in the treatment for panic disorder as are a few other anticonvulsants. Though the exact mechanism by which tiagabine exerts its effect on the human body is unknown, it does appear to operate as a selective GABA reuptake inhibitor.

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral. Tiagabine is nearly completely absorbed (>95%).
- **Sources and uses:** For the treatment of partial seizures
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Though the exact mechanism by which Tiagabine exerts its effect on the human body is unknown, it does appear to operate as a selective GABA reuptake inhibitor.
- **Health effects:** Respiratory depression was seen in a number of patients, including children, in the context of seizures. May cause a potentially dangerous rash that may develop into Stevens Johnson syndrome, an extremely rare but potentially fatal skin disease.
