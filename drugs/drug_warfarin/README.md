## About

Warfarin is an anticoagulant medication used to treat conditions such as deep vein thrombosis, pulmonary embolism, and atrial fibrillation. It is usually taken orally, as it is well-absorbed by mouth, but it may also be given intravenously. It is widely used and listed on the World Health Organization essential medicines list.
