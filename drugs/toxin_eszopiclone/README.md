## About

Eszopiclone, marketed by Sepracor under the brand-name Lunesta, is a nonbenzodiazepine hypnotic agent (viz., a sedative) used as a treatment for insomnia. Eszopiclone is the active stereoisomer of zopiclone, and belongs to the class of drugs known as cyclopyrrones. Its main selling point is that it is approved by the U.S. …

- **Category:** Drug, Food Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** rapidly absorbed following oral administration
- **Sources and uses:** For the treatment of insomnia
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** The mechanism of action of Eszopiclone is not completely understood. It is thought that Eszopiclone acts on the benzodiazepine receptors as an agonist and interacts with GABA-receptor complexes.
