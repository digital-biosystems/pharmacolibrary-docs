# Polymyxin B Sulfate

- **generic name:** Polymyxin B Sulfate
- **T3DB:** [T3D3529](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3529) · **DrugBank:** [DB00781](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00781) · **PubChem:** [CID 4868](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/4868)
- **groups:** approved, investigational, vet_approved

## About

Polymyxin B sulfate is a mixture of polymyxins B1 and B2, obtained from Bacillus polymyxa strains. They are basic polypeptides of about eight amino acids and have cationic detergent action on cell membranes. Polymyxin B is used for infections with gram-negative organisms, but may be neurotoxic and nephrotoxic. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** eye contact, parenteral (intrathecal, intramuscular injection, intravenous drip). Not absorbed from the normal alimentary tract.
- **Sources and uses:** Used in the treatment of infections of the urinary tract, meninges, and bloodstream caused by susceptible strains of <i>Pseudomonas aeruginosa</i>. It may also be used topically and subconjunctivally in the treatment of infections of the eye caused by susceptible strains of Ps. aeruginosa.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Polymyxin B sulfate has a bactericidal action against almost all gram-negative bacilli except the Proteus group. Polymyxin B sulfate interacts with the lipopolysaccharide of the cytoplasmic outer membrane of Gram-negative bacteria, altering membrane permeability and causing cell death. …
