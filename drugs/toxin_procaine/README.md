# Procaine

- **groups:** approved, investigational, vet_approved

## About

Procaine is only found in individuals that have used or taken this drug. It is a local anesthetic of the ester type that has a slow onset and a short duration of action. It is mainly used for infiltration anesthesia, peripheral nerve block, and spinal block. (From Martindale, The Extra Pharmacopoeia, 30th ed, p1016). [PubChem]Procaine acts mainly by inhibiting sodium influx through voltage gated sodium channels in the neuronal cell membrane of peripheral nerves. …

- **Category:** Drug, Food Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, Infiltration, Intramuscular
- **Sources and uses:** It is used primarily to reduce the pain of intramuscular injection of penicillin, and it is also used in dentistry. [Wikipedia]
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Procaine acts mainly by inhibiting sodium influx through voltage gated sodium channels in the neuronal cell membrane of peripheral nerves. When the influx of sodium is interrupted, an action potential cannot arise and signal conduction is thus inhibited. …
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 350 mg/kg (Oral, mouse).
- **Detected in:** urine
