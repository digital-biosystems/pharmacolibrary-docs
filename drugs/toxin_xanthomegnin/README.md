# Xanthomegnin

- **generic name:** Xanthomegnin
- **T3DB:** [T3D3693](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3693) · **PubChem:** [CID 15519](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/15519)

## About

Xanthomegnin is a mutagenic mycotoxin isolated from Penicillium citreo-viride. It is known to cause nephropathy and death in farm animals exposed to food-borne Penicillium (and Aspergillus fungi). In humans and animals, oral intake of xanthomegnin leads to kidney and liver abnormalities, often first presenting as jaundice. …

- **Category:** Fungal Toxin, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, dermal, inhalation, and parenteral (contaminated drugs).
- **Sources and uses:** Xanthomegnin is a mutagenic mycotoxin isolated from Penicillium citreo-viride.
