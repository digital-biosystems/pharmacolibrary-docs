# Mitomycin

- **generic name:** Mitomycin
- **T3DB:** [T3D2743](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2743) · **DrugBank:** [DB00305](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00305) · **PubChem:** [CID 5746](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/5746)
- **groups:** approved, investigational

## About

Mitomycin is only found in individuals that have used or taken this drug. It is an antineoplastic antibiotic produced by Streptomyces caespitosus. It is one of the bi- or tri-functional alkylating agents causing cross-linking of DNA and inhibition of DNA synthesis. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** intravesical, erratic, intravenous.
- **Sources and uses:** For treatment of malignant neoplasm of lip, oral cavity, pharynx, digestive organs, peritoneum, female breast, and urinary bladder. Also used as an adjunct to ab externo glaucoma surgery.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Mitomycin is activated in vivo to a bifunctional and trifunctional alkylating agent. Binding to DNA leads to cross-linking and inhibition of DNA synthesis and function. …
- **Health effects:** Mitomycin C causes delayed bone marrow toxicity and therefore it is usually administered at 6-weekly intervals. Prolonged use may result in permanent bone-marrow damage. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 2B, possibly carcinogenic to humans.
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 23 mg/kg (Oral, Mouse)
LD50: 30 mg/kg (Oral, Rat)
