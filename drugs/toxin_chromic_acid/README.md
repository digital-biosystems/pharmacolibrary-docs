# Chromic acid

- **generic name:** Chromic acid
- **T3DB:** [T3D0215](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D0215) · **PubChem:** [CID 24425](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/24425)

## About

Chromic acid is a chemical compound with the chemical formula H2CrO4. More generally, it is the name for a solution formed by the addition of sulfuric acid to aqueous solutions of dichromate. It consists at least in part of chromium trioxide.

<p><small>Source: <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromic_acid?oldid=1380058352">Wikipedia</a> · <a href="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/">CC BY-SA 4.0</a></small></p>

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, inhalation, dermal
- **Sources and uses:** Chromic acid is an intermediate in chromium plating, and is also used in ceramic glazes, and colored glass.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Chromic acid is highly corrosive and strongly oxidative. Many strong acids cause tissue burns through the denaturation of proteins and partial hydrolysis of proteins. …
- **Health effects:** Skin contact with chromic acid can cause redness, pain, and severe skin burns. Chromic acid may cause severe burns to the eye and permanent eye damage. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 1, carcinogenic to humans.
- **Minimal risk levels (ATSDR):** Intermediate Oral: 0.005 mg/kg/day; Chronic Oral: 0.001 mg/kg/day
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 330 mg/kg (Oral, Dog)
