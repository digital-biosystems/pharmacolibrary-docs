# 1,1-Dichloroethane

- **generic name:** 1,1-Dichloroethane
- **T3DB:** [T3D0160](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D0160) · **PubChem:** [CID 6365](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/6365)

## About

1,1-Dichloroethane is a chlorinated hydrocarbon. It is a colorless oily liquid with a chloroform-like odor. It is not easily soluble in water, but miscible with most organic solvents.

<p><small>Source: <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1%2C1-Dichloroethane?oldid=1365239793">Wikipedia</a> · <a href="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/">CC BY-SA 4.0</a></small></p>

- **Category:** Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Pollutant, Airborne Pollutant, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** inhalation, oral, dermal, eye contact
- **Sources and uses:** 1,1-Dichloroethane is used primarily to make 1,1,1-trichloroethane and a number of other chemicals. It is also used to dissolve other substances such as paint, varnish and finish removers, and to remove grease. …
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** The limited information available about 1,1-dichloroethane suggests that it may be nephrotoxic, fetotoxic, and possibly carcinogenic. 1,1-Dichloroethane has been observed to enhance cell transformation and results suggest that 1,1-dichloroethane or a metabolite can bind to cellular macromolecules such as DNA. It had been reported that 1,1-dichloroethane binds to nucleic acids and proteins in vivo and in vitro. …
- **Health effects:** 1,1-dichloroethane can cause kidney disease after long-term, high-level exposure in the air. It is also known to cause liver damage, as well as central nervous system depression. 1,1-Dichloroethane can cause dermatitis on prolonged dermal exposure.
- **Carcinogenicity:** No indication of carcinogenicity (not listed by IARC).
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 750 mg/kg (Oral, Rat) 
LD50: 14.1 g/kg (Oral, Rat)
