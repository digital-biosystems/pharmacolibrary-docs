### Reviewer guidance

**The temazepam record was rejected because the paper reports no distribution volume and no clearance, and the parameter D1 (0.41 mg/kg, labeled dose) is actually a duration of zero-order input, a dimension mismatch.**

The paper is an exposure/outcome study, not a compartmental population PK model: it gives no distribution volume and no clearance or elimination rate for temazepam. Additionally, one reported parameter's unit could not be converted to SI units, so it entered the model without a usable numeric value. Extracted — temazepam: D1 0.41 mg/kg, fe 79.7, Fab 80.
