### Reviewer guidance

**The hydrochlorothiazide record was held back because the lag time was not extracted and apparent bioavailability was assumed to be one.**

The lag time parameter was not extracted from the record, so no explicit estimate was available for the one-compartment model. The model builder substituted apparent assumptions, specifically setting oral and metabolic bioavailability to one without molar correction. These deviations meant the record relied on library placeholders rather than fitted data for its pharmacokinetic description. Extracted — hydrochlorothiazide and telmisartan: kabs 1.13 1/h, kel 0.25 1/h, V/F 7.25 l/kg.
