## About

Albendazole is an anthelmintic and antiparasitic medicine used to treat worm and protozoal infections such as parasitic helminthiasis, cysticercosis, echinococcosis, giardiasis, and various roundworm and tapeworm diseases. It is widely used in human medicine and is also approved for veterinary use, and it appears on the WHO list of essential medicines.
