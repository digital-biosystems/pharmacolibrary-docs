## Record summary

**As extracted:** Albendazole (concentrations from the PK model of Algorta_2022) drives Percent rapid touch-responsive motility (in %): direct sigmoid Emax (Hill) effect.

**Model:** No model was generated from this record.

> Albendazole (0–5 μM mixed into agar) inhibits the percent rapid touch-responsive motility of C. elegans, fitted with a four-parameter sigmoid Emax dose-response curve (bottom/top constrained to 0–100%). The paper does not state an absolute EC50 for this assay, but reports a 1.7-fold increase in albendazole EC50 in skn-1(k1023gf) worms (and 1.6-fold decrease in skn-1(zj15lf)) versus wild type N2, with full effectiveness at 5 μM in N2; the mechanism of the drug effect on motility is not described beyond benzimidazole β-tubulin targeting.
>
