# Thiourea

- **generic name:** Thiourea
- **T3DB:** [T3D4891](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4891) · **PubChem:** [CID 2723790](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/2723790)

## About

Thiourea is an organic compound of carbon, nitrogen, sulfur and hydrogen, with the formula CSN2H4 or2CS. It is similar to urea, except that the oxygen atom is replaced by a sulfur atom. The properties of urea and thiourea differ significantly because of the relative electronegativities of sulfur and oxygen. …

- **Category:** Household Toxin, Industrial/Workplace Toxin, Food Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 3, not classifiable as to its carcinogenicity to humans.
