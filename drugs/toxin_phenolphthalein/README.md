# Phenolphthalein

- **generic name:** Phenolphthalein
- **T3DB:** [T3D4708](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4708) · **DrugBank:** [DB04824](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB04824) · **PubChem:** [CID 4764](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/4764)
- **groups:** approved, withdrawn

## About

Phenolphthalein was withdrawn in Canada due to concerns with carcinogenicity in 1997.

- **Category:** Household Toxin, Synthetic Toxin
- **Sources and uses:** Used for over a century as a laxative.
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 2B, possibly carcinogenic to humans.
