# Flupenthixol

- **generic name:** Flupenthixol
- **T3DB:** [T3D2920](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2920) · **DrugBank:** [DB00875](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00875) · **PubChem:** [CID 25137855](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/25137855)
- **groups:** approved, investigational, withdrawn

## About

Flupentixol is an antipsychotic neuroleptic drug. It is a thioxanthene, and therefore closely related to the phenothiazines. Its primary use is as a long acting injection given two or three weekly to people with schizophrenia who have a poor compliance with medication and suffer frequent relapses of illness. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** fairly slow and incomplete after oral administration
- **Sources and uses:** For use in the treatment of schizophrenia and depression
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Flupenthixol is a thioxanthene antipsychotic. The mechanism of action of Flupenthixol is not completely understood. …
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 300 mk/kg (Oral,Mouse)
LD50: 791 mg/kg (Oral, Rat) 
LD50: 87 mk/kg (Intravenous, Mouse)
LD50: 37 mg/kg (Intravenous, Rat)
