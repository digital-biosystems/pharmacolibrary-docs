## About

Paracetamol is a non-opioid analgesic and antipyretic used to treat fever, mild to moderate pain, and specific headache types. It is administered orally, rectally, or intravenously in forms such as tablets, suppositories, or solution. This medication is widely used in the United States and Europe as an over-the-counter option, listed on the World Health Organization's essential medicines list.
