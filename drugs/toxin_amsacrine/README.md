# Amsacrine

- **generic name:** Amsacrine
- **T3DB:** [T3D4674](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D4674) · **DrugBank:** [DB00276](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00276) · **PubChem:** [CID 2179](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/2179)
- **groups:** approved, investigational, withdrawn

## About

Aminoacridine derivative that is a potent intercalating antineoplastic agent. It is effective in the treatment of acute leukemias and malignant lymphomas, but has poor activity in the treatment of solid tumors. It is frequently used in combination with other antineoplastic agents in chemotherapy protocols. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** poorly absorbed.
- **Sources and uses:** For treatment of acute myeloid leukaemia.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Amsacrine binds to DNA through intercalation and external binding. It has a base specificity for A-T pairs. …
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 2B, possibly carcinogenic to humans.
