### Reviewer guidance

**The two-compartment model structure is inconsistent because the peripheral compartment lacks a defined inter-compartmental clearance, rendering it pharmacokinetically isolated.**

The record specifies a two-compartment topology (2C) with central and peripheral volumes but omits the inter-compartmental clearance parameter. Without this distribution clearance, the peripheral compartment is structurally disconnected from the central compartment. This creates an unreachable compartment, preventing valid simulation of drug distribution between the two spaces. Extracted — metronidazole: CL 0.068 l/kg/h, V1 0.18 l/kg, V2 0.32 l/kg.
