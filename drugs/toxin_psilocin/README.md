# Psilocin

- **generic name:** Psilocin
- **T3DB:** [T3D2458](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2458) · **PubChem:** [CID 4980](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/4980)

## About

Psilocin (4-OH-DMT), an aromatic compound, sometimes also spelled psilocine, psilocyn, or psilotsin, is a psychedelic mushroom alkaloid. It is found in most psychedelic mushrooms together with its phosphorylated counterpart psilocybin. Psilocin is a Schedule I drug under the Convention on Psychotropic Substances. …

- **Category:** Fungal Toxin, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, dermal, inhalation, and parenteral (contaminated drugs).
- **Sources and uses:** Psilocin is a psychedelic (hallucinogenic) mushroom alkaloid. It is found in most psychedelic mushrooms together with its phosphorylated counterpart psilocybin. …
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Psilocin acts as a partial agonist at the 5-HT2A serotonin receptor in the brain where it mimics the effects of serotonin (5-HT). It is an 5-HT1A and 5-HT2A/2C agonist.
- **Health effects:** Psilocin is neurotoxic and can cause hallucinations.
