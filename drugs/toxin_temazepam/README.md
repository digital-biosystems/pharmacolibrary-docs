# Temazepam

- **generic name:** Temazepam
- **T3DB:** [T3D2719](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D2719) · **DrugBank:** [DB00231](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB00231) · **PubChem:** [CID 5391](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/5391)
- **groups:** approved

## About

Temazepam is only found in individuals that have used or taken this drug. It is a benzodiazepine that acts as a gamma-aminobutyric acid modulator and anti-anxiety agent. [PubChem]Benzodiazepines bind nonspecifically to benzodiazepine receptors, which affects muscle relaxation, anticonvulsant activity, motor coordination, and memory. As benzodiazepine receptors are thought to be coupled to gamma-aminobutyric acid-A (GABA<sub>A</sub>) receptors, this enhances the effects of GABA by increasing GABA affinity for the GABA receptor. …

- **Category:** Drug, Synthetic Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, well absorbed, minimal first-pass metabolism.
- **Sources and uses:** Officially indicated for severe insomnia and other severe or disabling sleep disorders. For the short-term treatment of insomnia (generally 7-10 days).
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Benzodiazepines bind nonspecifically to benzodiazepine receptors, which affects muscle relaxation, anticonvulsant activity, motor coordination, and memory. As benzodiazepine receptors are thought to be coupled to gamma-aminobutyric acid-A (GABA<sub>A</sub>) receptors, this enhances the effects of GABA by increasing GABA affinity for the GABA receptor. …
- **Health effects:** They cause slurred speech, disorientation and "drunken" behavior. They are physically and psychologically addictive.
- **Carcinogenicity:** IARC group 3, not classifiable as to its carcinogenicity to humans.
- **Acute toxicity:** LD50: 1963 mg/kg (oral, mice)
LD50: 1833 mg/kg (oral, rat)
LD50: >2400 mg/kg (oral, rabbit)
