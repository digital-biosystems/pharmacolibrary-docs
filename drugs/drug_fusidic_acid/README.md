## About

Fusidic acid is an antibiotic used to treat bacterial infections of the skin, eyes, and systemic sites. It is commonly applied topically to the skin or eyes, and may also be taken orally or given intravenously for systemic infections. The drug is approved for use and has seen renewed interest in medical practice.
