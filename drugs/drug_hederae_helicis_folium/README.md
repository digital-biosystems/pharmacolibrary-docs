## About

Hederae helicis folium (ivy leaf extract) is a herbal expectorant used to relieve cough associated with colds and respiratory conditions. It is not an approved medicine in major regulatory databases and remains investigational, though ivy leaf preparations are traditionally used as cough remedies in some countries.
