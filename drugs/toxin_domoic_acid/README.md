# Domoic acid

## About

Isodomoic acid F is found in mollusks. Isodomoic acid F is isolated from mussels.

- **Category:** Animal Toxin, Food Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** ingestion
- **Sources and uses:** Isodomoic acid F is found in mollusks. Isodomoic acid F is isolated from mussels.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Domoic acid acts as a neurotoxin. It induces excitotoxicity by an integrative action on ionotropic glutamate receptors at both sides of the synapse for which it has high affinity, preferentially the KA subtype, coupled with an effect that prevents the channel from rapid desensitization. …
- **Health effects:** Exposure to domoic acid causes short-term memory loss, brain damage (including epilepsy), in severe cases, death. It may also cause kidney damage – even at levels considered safe for human consumption.
