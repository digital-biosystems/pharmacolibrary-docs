### Reviewer guidance

**All parameters are misassigned to Mescaline while Psilocybin's PK parameters lack values, and the model topology mismatched the expected parent-metabolite structure.**

The extracted clearance, volume of distribution, and absorption rate constant for Psilocybin remained as library placeholders because no explicit values were reported. Furthermore, the entire set of summary statistics (Cmax, tmax, AUC, etc.) is explicitly tagged with the compound 'Mescaline,' contradicting the study's focus on Psilocybin. The resulting model structure was a simple single-compartment enteral model rather than the required parent-metabolite topology, leading to quarantine. Extracted — Mescaline: Cmax 786 ng/mL, tmax 2.4 h, t1/2z 3.7 h, AUC 5.84e+03 ng·h/mL, AUC∞ 6.02e+03 ng·h/mL, CL/F 43 L/h, V/F 228 L; psilocybin: tlag 0 h.
