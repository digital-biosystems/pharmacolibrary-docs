### Reviewer guidance

**Psilocin AUC parameters AUClast and AUCSDI have invalid unit '0-10', causing dimensional mismatch with required mass-time/volume units.**

The AUClast and AUCSDI parameters for Psilocin list unit '0-10', which is dimensionless rather than the required [mass] * [time] / [volume] for area under the curve. This unit cannot be converted to SI, leaving the parameters without valid values. The mismatch violates dimensional consistency for PK parameters. Extracted — Psilocin: Cmax 15.7 µg/L, tmax 2.3 h, t1/2z 1.6 h, AUClast 47.7 0-10; psilocybin: tmax 2.3 h, AUC 9.5 0-10.
