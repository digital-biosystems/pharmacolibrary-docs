# Aurovertin B

- **generic name:** Aurovertin B
- **T3DB:** [T3D3750](http://www.t3db.ca/toxins/T3D3750) · **DrugBank:** [DB07394](https://go.drugbank.com/drugs/DB07394) · **PubChem:** [CID 6441012](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/6441012)
- **groups:** experimental

## About

Aurovertin B is a mycotoxin and antibiotic produced by the fungus Calcarisporium arbuscula. It is known for its ability to inhibit oxidative phosphorylation.

- **Category:** Fungal Toxin, Natural Toxin
- **Route of exposure:** oral, dermal, inhalation, and parenteral (contaminated drugs).
- **Sources and uses:** Aurovertin B is a mycotoxin and antibiotic produced by the fungus Calcarisporium arbuscula.
- **Mechanism of toxicity:** Aurovertins are a mixed, noncompetitive inhibitors of both ATP hydrolysis and synthesis. They do this by inhibiting the proton-pumping F1F0-ATP synthase by binding to beta-subunits in its F1 catalytic sector. …
